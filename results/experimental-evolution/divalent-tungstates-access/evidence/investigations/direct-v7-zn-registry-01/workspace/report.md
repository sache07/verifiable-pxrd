# Research diagnostic: alternate wolframite coordinate registry

This workspace is not a solver submission or a reusable answer. It starts from
the physical exact-inventory v4 harder-Zn project, retains its three phases,
cells, profile/nuisance model and public measurement, and replaces only the
independent ZnWO4 coordinates with those from a physical recovered solve of
the same frozen case. That donor is oracle-informed research evidence.

The standard chemistry-agnostic staged-polish helper then compared its three
bounded coordinate orders. All three final branches were eligible and
converged near Rwp 3.27%, versus 9.20% for the original failed coordinate
basin. The selected heavy-to-light branch has no frozen variables, final
maximum shift/esd 0.032, stable positive fractions, no catastrophic contacts,
and no excessive pair-distance contraction. `final.gpx`, all CIFs,
`fit.csv`, and `result.json` were re-exported from that exact endpoint.

This establishes that a different physical same-topology parent registry can
repair the gross intensity basin when compared before local polish. It does
not validate copying coordinates from another answer; a reusable procedure
must instead preserve multiple independently available physical parent starts
and compare them at equal adaptation depth.
