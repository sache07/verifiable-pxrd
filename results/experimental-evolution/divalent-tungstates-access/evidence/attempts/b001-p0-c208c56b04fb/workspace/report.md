# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled as three crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over those phases; no amorphous fraction was determined.

| Phase | Structural evidence | Refined cell | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Scheelite SrWO4, *I* 41/*a* | Dominant scheelite sequence, including 17.74, 27.49, 29.69, 32.94, 45.10 and 47.45° 2θ | *a* = 5.38559 Å, *c* = 11.90587 Å | 80.54 wt% |
| Monoclinic WO3, *P* 21/*c* | Diagnostic 22.80/23.27/24.07° triplet and its higher-angle reflections; this polymorph fit better than the other accessible WO3 models | *a* = 7.69017 Å, *b* = 7.53540 Å, *c* = 10.51628 Å, β = 136.0746° | 11.68 wt% |
| Orthorhombic SrCO3, *Pnma* | Weak but coherent reflections near 24.98, 25.56, 31.40 and 35–36° 2θ | *a* = 5.98884 Å, *b* = 5.07472 Å, *c* = 8.40631 Å | 7.78 wt% |

The final GSAS-II Rietveld fit has Rwp = 4.302%, Rp = 3.340% and goodness-of-fit = 1.083 for 8,301 points. The calculated profile and background remain nonnegative across the full measured range. Preferred orientation and bounded size/microstrain terms were included where supported by the profile.

Phase identities are strong, while the quoted fractions should be treated as approximately ±1–2 wt% absolute because peak overlap, preferred orientation and line broadening dominate the formal counting-statistical errors. SrWO4 was initialized from an isostructural scheelite analogue because an exact starting model was unavailable; its oxygen coordinate is therefore less certain than its Sr/W framework. WO3 oxygen coordinates and the C/O coordinates of the weak SrCO3 phase were not independently determined with high precision and remain close to reference values. The data do not reliably constrain amorphous material or phases below the residual detection level.
