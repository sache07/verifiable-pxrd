# Powder XRD phase analysis

The modeled crystalline material is 76.11 wt% wolframite MnWO4, 14.22 wt% monoclinic WO3, and 9.67 wt% of a provisional low-symmetry Mn2WO5 phase. Fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases; this experiment does not determine an amorphous fraction. The native, no-refinement replay of `final.gpx` gives Rwp = 15.229%, Rp = 8.126%, and GOF = 8.599.

MnWO4 is securely identified by the dominant wolframite reflection family (reference COD 1520200) and refines in P 2/c to a = 4.79680, b = 5.71061, c = 4.97908 A, beta = 91.1132 degrees. The second established phase is monoclinic WO3 (reference COD 2311041; P 21/c in the submitted nonstandard cell setting), supported by a broad set of otherwise unassigned reflections; its refined cell is a = 7.69030, b = 7.53955, c = 10.52229 A, beta = 136.061 degrees. An explicit Mn3O4 precursor trial refined to zero scale and was rejected.

After fitting MnWO4 and WO3, 23 coherent residual peaks index to one primitive, nearly orthogonal cell with dimensions 4.44652, 7.70050, and 10.46103 A (V = 358.190 A^3). The family includes peaks at 20.324, 20.879, 25.899, 29.148, 32.256, 32.984, 35.298, 35.624, 46.208, 47.012, 54.901, 55.847, and 56.998 degrees 2theta and continues to 99 degrees. Odd axial reflections rule out the tested P212121 and Pbam descriptions. Among exact-stoichiometry and chemically plausible analogue tests, an inversion-symmetric Mn2WO5 model derived from low-temperature La2RuO5 (COD 2015098; La to Mn and Ru to W) was the best physical model that covered the complete indexed family. Fe2WO6-, Y2WO6-, and Mn2BiO5-type alternatives either missed systematic reflection families, gave worse fits, or required poor geometry.

The final profile includes a converged isotropic microstrain term for wolframite and supported March-Dollase terms for WO3 [010] and the provisional Mn2WO5 [110]. Surface roughness, a separate zero-offset term, and polarization refinement were rejected because they were unstable or worsened the fit. The prescribed staged coordinate polish was run once; all lower-Rwp coordinate branches were rejected for bound clipping or nonconvergence, so `final.gpx` is the complete pre-polish physical checkpoint. A read-only native replay verified the project, phase fractions, and exported profile with no review flags.

The Mn2WO5 assignment is not a reliable full structure solution. Its topology and composition are chemically supported and its cell accounts for every coherent residual family, but important intensities remain undercalculated, its shortest W-O distance is unusually short (1.522 A), and coordinate refinement is unstable. Its reported 9.67 wt% fraction is therefore model-dependent; the formal least-squares uncertainty (0.16 wt%) substantially understates the systematic uncertainty. The MnWO4 and WO3 identities are much firmer, but their fractions also inherit uncertainty from this provisional minor-phase model and from profile parameters at the instrument-envelope limits.
