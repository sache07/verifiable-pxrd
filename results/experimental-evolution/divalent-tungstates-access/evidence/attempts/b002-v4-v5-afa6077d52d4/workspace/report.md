# Powder XRD phase analysis

The crystalline specimen is best described by wolframite MnWO4, residual monoclinic WO3, and a broad Mn2WO6-type minority component. Fractions below are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; an amorphous fraction was not measured.

## Evidence

MnWO4 accounts for the dominant wolframite fingerprint, including the families near 15.48, 18.44, 23.67/24.19, and 29.98/30.42 degrees 2theta. Its refined geometry remains ordinary: Mn-O is 2.047-2.150 A and W-O is 1.884-2.100 A.

The strong residual triplet near 23.10, 23.55, and 24.32 degrees, together with lines around 28.6 and 33-35 degrees, selects monoclinic WO3, consistent with unreacted precursor. The chosen ambient P21/c model fits slightly better than the close alternatives while retaining physical W-O distances of 1.69-2.18 A. An alternative checkpoint with short O-O contacts was rejected.

After those two phases were fitted, a coherent broad family remained at approximately 19.85, 20.41, 28.64, 31.76, 35.12, 46.49, and 54.41 degrees. Exact-composition precursor/product candidates did not account for the family with a stable positive scale. A charge-balanced Mn2WO6 model transferred from the P212121 Y2WO6 framework indexes this family broadly and gives physical final distances (W-O 1.78-1.97 A; Mn-O 2.08-2.47 A). No additional coherent reflection family remained unindexed.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The GSAS-II refinement used all 8,301 measured points from 9.9034 to 100.0946 degrees, the supplied Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, a Bragg-Brentano profile, refined phase scales/cells, specimen displacement, background, bounded profile terms, and an effective polarization of 0.9116. The final fit is Rwp=14.368%, Rp=8.795%, chi-square=431114.5, and GOF=7.219. The relatively large GOF reflects raw-count weighting without supplied point uncertainties as well as remaining relative-intensity mismatch.

The Mn2WO6 assignment is tentative. Its peaks require the lower crystallite-size bound (0.040 micrometre) and upper microstrain bound (8000 ppm), and bounded coordinate trials either hit their limits or degraded the coordination geometry. The submitted coordinates therefore retain the physical analogue topology rather than an over-refined model. Its approximately 15 wt% fraction is model-dependent and cannot be claimed with the small formal s.u.; the WO3 fraction is correspondingly correlated. MnWO4 and WO3 identifications are substantially more secure. Unbounded coordinate trials that produced implausibly short W-O distances were rolled back, and a correlated surface-roughness correction was rejected after replay.
