# Powder XRD phase analysis

The accepted model contains three crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases only.

| Phase | Structure | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| MnWO4 wolframite | P 2/c (COD 1520200 start) | 0.63465 (formal 1σ 0.00147) |
| Mn3WO6 | R -3 (COD 2002703 start) | 0.19077 (formal 1σ 0.00139) |
| WO3 | P n m a (COD 2107312 start) | 0.17459 (formal 1σ 0.00118) |

Evidence: MnWO4 accounts for the monoclinic families near 15.27, 18.25, 23.47–23.99, 29.77–30.23 and 35.86° 2θ. Rhombohedral Mn3WO6 accounts for its strong families near 19.73, 20.24, 25.23, 28.52 and 31.67°. Orthorhombic WO3 accounts for the characteristic 22.89/23.38/24.18° group and its higher-angle families. With these phases present, all coherent peak families are indexed. A separately developed Mn3O4 fourth-phase test lowered Rwp by only about 0.023 percentage point, borrowed intensity primarily from overlaps, and worsened its diagnostic reflections; it was therefore rejected.

The final fresh GSAS-II replay gives Rwp = 6.8012%, Rp = 4.1309% and goodness of fit = 4.773 using Poisson-count weights. The replay regenerated atomic structure factors and reflection lists, left the project unchanged, and reported no negative ADPs, peak widths or background. Geometry checks found no catastrophic contacts. The required bounded polish selected its unchanged baseline because no converged physical coordinate branch improved Rwp by the required 0.02 percentage point.

Remaining uncertainty: the strongest residuals are centered on accepted WO3 and overlapping tungstate reflections rather than forming a new coherent family, so they are attributed to imperfect relative intensities/profile modeling, not an additional phase. Surface roughness was rejected as bound-pinned and >99.9% correlated. Common Lorentzian broadening and phase size are inseparable; the common X term was fixed to an interior convention, and the Mn3WO6 size update remained lower-bound-sensitive. Consequently, the reported crystallite-size/profile values are not reliable physical measurements. The fraction ESDs above are covariance-only and understate model-form uncertainty; branch-to-branch fraction stability suggests a practical uncertainty of roughly 0.5–1 absolute wt% per phase. No amorphous content can be quantified from this refinement.
