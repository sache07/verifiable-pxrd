# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by four crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases.

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, °) | Weight fraction |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| CuWO4 | P -1 | 4.62204, 5.74790, 4.85971; 91.3249, 91.4796, 84.7034 | 0.64913 |
| Cu3WO6 | Pa-3 | a = 9.79196 | 0.13171 |
| WO3 | P21/c | 7.68488, 7.55943, 10.5087; β = 136.005 | 0.19296 |
| CuO (tenorite) | C2/c | 4.67017, 3.43139, 5.12022; β = 99.5603 | 0.02621 |

Evidence for the assignments includes the CuWO4 series near 15.0, 18.8, 29.1 and 30.1° 2θ; Cu3WO6 reflections near 15.2, 19.8, 32.5 and 33.8°; the characteristic monoclinic WO3 cluster from about 22.7–23.9°; and the weaker but repeated tenorite lines near 35.1, 38.3, 48.5, 53.0, 58.1 and 65.3°. The resulting bulk Cu:W molar ratio is 1.018:1, consistent with the CuO/WO3 chemical context. Orthorhombic and triclinic WO3 alternatives did not justify their poorer or extra degrees of freedom; the retained P21/c model accounts for the full WO3 peak series.

The independently replayable full-pattern result has Rwp = 1.733%, Rp = 1.298%, and a Poisson-weighted goodness of fit of 1.072 (8301 points; counting-statistical expected Rwp = 1.616%). All submitted structures pass the contact/occupancy screen, and replay reports no negative-width, negative-background, ADP, or polarization flags.

The CuO identity is supported by multiple reflections, but its 2.6 wt% abundance is the least reliable fraction because its peaks are weak and overlap the major phases. Oxygen coordinates, especially in WO3 and trace CuO, are less precisely determined than the phase symmetry and lattice. No fifth crystalline phase is required by the residual. An amorphous fraction or absolute crystalline yield cannot be determined without an internal standard, so the reported fractions must not be interpreted as absolute sample fractions.
