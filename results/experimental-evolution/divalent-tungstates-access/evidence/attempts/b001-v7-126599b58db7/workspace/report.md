# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by four crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases.

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction | Reference start |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CuWO4 | P-1 | 0.64659 (64.659 wt%) | COD 4000806 |
| Cu3WO6 | Pa-3 | 0.13234 (13.234 wt%) | COD 1527404 |
| WO3 | P21/c | 0.19760 (19.760 wt%) | COD 2311041 |
| CuO (tenorite) | C2/c | 0.02346 (2.346 wt%) | COD 1011194 |

## Evidence

Triclinic CuWO4 accounts for the dominant dense reflection set. Cubic Cu3WO6 is required by the coherent family near 15.19, 17.63, 19.79, 21.75, 32.49, and 33.77 degrees 2theta. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the family near 22.66, 26.07, 28.14/28.49, and 32.78/33.09 degrees. After those phases were refined, a positive family at 35.10, 38.32, 48.44, 58.10, 61.21, 67.67, 74.88, and 82.22 degrees identified residual tenorite CuO after applying the specimen-displacement correction. No coherent positive reflection family remains unassigned.

Symmetry-diverse alternatives were compared at equally developed checkpoints. Monoclinic WO3 COD 2311041 reached the physical fit basin; a triclinic WO3 start stalled at much higher Rwp, while another monoclinic reference had an unphysical 1.57 A O--O contact. For CuO, C2/c COD 1011194 and Cc COD 9016057 gave essentially tied fits (3.179% and 3.182% Rwp before final profile work); the higher-symmetry C2/c model was retained.

The final independently replayed atomic model has Rwp = 2.4213%, Rp = 1.8747%, Rexp = 1.6134%, and goodness of fit = 1.5007 over all 8301 points. The required one-time bounded polish selected its converged phasewise branch. All phases have positive scales, no frozen parameters, no catastrophic contacts, and physically reasonable nearest oxide distances. Texture and surface roughness were tested but not supported. Supported profile terms include uniaxial microstrain for CuWO4 along [100] and WO3 along [001]; strain is inactive for Cu3WO6 and CuO.

## Remaining uncertainty

The quoted least-squares fraction standard uncertainties are only about 0.02--0.03 wt percentage points, but they do not include model, overlap, background, or calibration systematics. The 2.35 wt% CuO fraction is therefore less secure quantitatively than the three larger fractions, although its multi-peak identification is secure. Several WO3 oxygen coordinates are weakly determined because of overlap and X-ray scattering contrast; the submitted topology and cell are reliable, but individual oxygen positions should not be treated as high-precision crystallography. The specimen displacement is near the supplied upper range and was fixed to remove its strong correlation with angular zero; a zero-fixed alternative pinned at the +800 micrometre bound and was rejected. No amorphous fraction or absolute crystalline content can be inferred from this scan, so all fractions are conditional on the modeled crystalline inventory.
