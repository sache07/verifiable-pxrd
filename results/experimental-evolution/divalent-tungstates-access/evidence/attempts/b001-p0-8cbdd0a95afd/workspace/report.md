# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is an essentially unreacted crystalline precursor mixture: **73.98 wt% monoclinic WO3** and **26.02 wt% wurtzite ZnO**, normalized over the modeled crystalline material. This is chemically consistent with the 1:1 W:Zn precursor charge and the low reported treatment temperature of 200 °C.

The monoclinic WO3 assignment is fixed by its dense fingerprint, including the strong 23.13°, 23.60°, and 24.39° triplet and the matched groups at 28.63–28.94°, 33.27–34.18°, 41.44–41.91°, and higher angles. Peaks not belonging to that phase form the wurtzite ZnO sequence at approximately 31.80°, 36.32°, 47.68°, 56.65°, 63.09°, and 68.08–69.17°. A diagnostic ZnWO4 model was tested: its scale refined to the nonnegative lower bound, and its characteristic 18.9° and 30.4–30.7° peaks are absent.

The submitted structures are refinements of published parent models (COD 2311041 for WO3 and COD 9004178 for ZnO). Refined cells are a = 7.29540, b = 7.53926, c = 10.51316 Å, beta = 133.01035° for P21/c WO3 in the exported setting, and a = 3.24712, c = 5.18400 Å for P63mc ZnO. Both exported structures pass the occupancy/contact screen.

The final atomic-model replay over all 8,301 measured points gives Rwp = 2.8190% and Rp = 1.9981%; the fitted-interval goodness of fit is 1.0098. No unmatched Bragg peaks remain above the counting-noise residuals. The formal fraction e.s.d. is about 0.03 wt%, but profile/model systematics make roughly 0.3–0.5 wt% a more realistic fraction uncertainty. Trace crystalline material below about the sub-percent level cannot be excluded, and this diffraction-only normalization does not quantify amorphous material.
