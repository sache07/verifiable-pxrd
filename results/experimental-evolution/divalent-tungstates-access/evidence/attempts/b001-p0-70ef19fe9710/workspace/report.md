# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

The pattern is best described by three modeled crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over those phases only.

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| Wolframite MnWO4 | monoclinic P2/c | 74.62 wt% |
| gamma-WO3 | monoclinic P21/c setting | 16.16 wt% |
| AlWO4-type MnWO4 polymorph | monoclinic P2/m, analogue-derived | 9.22 wt% |

The final GSAS-II profile gives Rwp = 11.99%, Rp = 6.67% and goodness of fit = 6.77 using Poisson count weights over all 8301 points.

## Evidence

The dominant peak series is indexed and intensity-matched by wolframite MnWO4 (a = 4.79641, b = 5.71103, c = 4.97857 A, beta = 91.1224 degrees). The split WO3 peak groups near 23-25 and 33-35 degrees 2theta require gamma-monoclinic WO3 rather than the tested tetragonal, orthorhombic or triclinic alternatives. The WO3 CIF uses a valid nonstandard P21/c cell setting (beta approximately 136.04 degrees); its interatomic-contact screen is physically acceptable.

After those two phases were fitted, a coherent residual series at approximately 20.32, 20.88, 29.1, 35.3-35.6, 46.2-47.0 and 55 degrees indexed to a small near-orthogonal monoclinic cell (a = 4.32656, b = 3.14410, c = 4.44840 A). An AlWO4-type ABO4 framework with Mn and W on the cation sites accounts for this series and lowered Rwp from about 18.4% for the two-phase model to 12.0%. Scheelite MnWO4, residual Mn3O4/Mn2O3, MnO/MnO2 and tungsten suboxide models either refined to negligible scale or required implausible cell changes and profile parameters at bounds.

## Uncertainty

The wolframite and gamma-WO3 identifications are secure. The third phase's cell and presence are supported, but its AlWO4-type atomic arrangement is tentative: oxygen coordinates are weakly constrained by laboratory X-rays and several reflections overlap the major phases. Its 9.22 wt% fraction is therefore semiquantitative and should be regarded as uncertain by several absolute weight percent; the major-phase fractions inherit correlated uncertainty.

Weak unmatched residual maxima remain, especially near 25.91, 32.26 and 32.98 degrees 2theta. No fourth phase could be identified and quantified reliably, so no fraction is assigned to those residuals. Instrument/specimen terms also reached limits (notably sample displacement, U/X profile terms, WO3 strain and the minor-phase size), indicating unresolved profile correlation. The reported fractions exclude amorphous content and any unidentified trace crystalline material.
