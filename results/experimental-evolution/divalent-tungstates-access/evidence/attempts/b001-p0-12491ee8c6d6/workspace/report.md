# Powder XRD phase analysis

The modeled crystalline assemblage is 73.42 wt% wolframite MnWO4 (P 2/c), 17.69 wt% monoclinic WO3 (P 21/c), and 8.89 wt% Mn2WO6 in a tentative P 21 21 21 model. Fractions are normalized over these crystalline phases; they do not measure amorphous material.

The dominant peak set indexes consistently as wolframite MnWO4 and refines to a = 4.79538, b = 5.71079, c = 4.97735 A, beta = 91.1192 degrees. A second, distinct set is matched by monoclinic WO3 and refines to a = 7.30208, b = 7.54205, c = 10.53187 A, beta = 133.0816 degrees in the submitted P 21/c setting. These assignments are chemically consistent with firing WO3 and Mn3O4 at 800 degrees C.

After fitting MnWO4 and WO3, a linked residual family at approximately 20.34, 20.89, 29.15, 35.30-35.62, 46-47, and 54.9-57.0 degrees 2theta required a third phase. Its positions and relative intensity pattern are best represented by an Mn-substituted Y2WO6-type P 21 21 21 framework, refined to a = 5.05350, b = 8.65178, c = 9.38762 A. A moderate coordinate relaxation gives physically sensible nearest contacts (about 1.69 A W-O, 1.90 A Mn-O, and 2.24 A O-O), and all three submitted CIFs pass the occupancy/contact screen.

The full-grid fit has Rwp = 14.797% and Rp = 7.603% using weights 1/max(observed,1). The final project replay gives no negative-background, peak-width, polarization, or displacement-parameter flags.

The Mn2WO6 assignment is less certain than MnWO4 and WO3. Its structure was derived from an isostructural analogue rather than found as an exact Mn reference, and its refined crystallite size, microstrain, and March-Dollase ratio reach the allowed bounds (1.5 micrometers, 8000 ppm, and 0.70 along [010]). Consequently, its exact atomic coordinates and 8.89 wt% fraction cannot be determined reliably to high precision from this pattern alone; the fraction is model- and texture-dependent. The elevated Poisson goodness of fit (8.34) likewise indicates residual model discrepancy beyond counting noise. No separate coherent peak family justifies a fourth crystalline phase, but trace material below this residual level cannot be excluded.
