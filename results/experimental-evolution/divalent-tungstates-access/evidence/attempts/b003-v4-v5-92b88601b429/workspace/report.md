# Powder XRD phase analysis

The crystalline model contains three phases:

| Phase | Space group | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| MnWO4 (wolframite) | P 2/c | 0.6337 (63.37 wt%) |
| Mn3WO6 (corundum-related) | R -3 | 0.1809 (18.09 wt%) |
| WO3 (monoclinic) | P 21/c | 0.1855 (18.55 wt%) |

These fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases only. No amorphous fraction can be obtained from this uncalibrated pattern.

## Evidence

MnWO4 accounts for the dominant families near 15.3, 18.2, 23.5–24.0, and 29.7–30.2 degrees 2theta. The coherent residual families near 19.7–20.2, 25.3, 28.5, 31.7, 35.0, and 46.4 degrees require R -3 Mn3WO6. The 22.9, 24.2, and 33–34 degree groups require WO3. In the final residual map, the remaining large extrema coincide with reflections of these accepted phases and do not form another coherent peak family.

The inventory also closes the precursor chemistry. Converting the refined weights to formula-unit amounts gives an overall Mn:W molar ratio of 1.004:1, consistent with the reported 1:1 precursor-metal ratio without an ad hoc phase.

Distinct WO3 topologies were compared in the same multiphase state. A geometry-valid P 21/c parent gave Rwp 2.621% before final cleanup, versus 2.633% for physical P -1 and 7.679% for Pnma. An older P 21/c deposition was rejected despite a good pattern match because it contains an inherited 1.575 A O–O contact. The submitted modern P 21/c model has six-coordinate W and a 1.710–2.154 A W–O range. The submitted MnWO4 and Mn3WO6 models also retain six-coordinate cations, with shortest W–O distances of 1.774 and 1.887 A, respectively.

## Fit and uncertainty

The full 8,301-point fit has Rwp = 2.5247%, Rp = 1.8323%, Rexp = 1.4263%, and GOF = 1.770. Supported corrections include bounded surface roughness, one March-Dollase family per phase, crystallite size, and [010] uniaxial microstrain for MnWO4; extra anisotropic-strain families for Mn3WO6 and WO3 collapsed or became singular and were rejected.

The phase identities are well supported. The P 21/c versus P -1 distinction for WO3 is less secure because their checkpoint fits differ by only 0.012 percentage point in Rwp; P 21/c was selected for the slightly better fit, higher symmetry, and physical geometry. Atomic coordinates, especially oxygen positions, should not be treated as a precision structure determination. The Mn3WO6 texture correction is weakly determined. Scale, surface roughness, thermal factors, and texture remain correlated, so a realistic systematic uncertainty on each reported crystalline fraction is about 1–2 wt% absolute even though least-squares uncertainties would be smaller.
