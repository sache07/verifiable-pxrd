# Powder XRD phase analysis

The Cu Kα Bragg–Brentano pattern from the 800 °C WO₃/Mn₃O₄ synthesis is modeled by three crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over those modeled crystalline phases.

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction | wt% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Wolframite MnWO₄ | P2/c | 0.687542 | 68.75 |
| Monoclinic WO₃ | P2₁/c | 0.155918 | 15.59 |
| Rhombohedral Mn₃WO₆ | R-3 | 0.156540 | 15.65 |

## Evidence

The dominant reflection family, including the strong groups near 15.9, 18.9, 24.1–24.6, and 30.4–30.8° 2θ, indexes as wolframite MnWO₄. A second family is accounted for by monoclinic P2₁/c WO₃; a clean COD 2311041 coordinate start gave a substantially better and more physical multiphase model than the alternative same-cell WO₃ reference and was relaxed only locally.

The remaining coherent family near 20.3–20.9, 29.2, 32.3–33.0, 35.3–35.6, 46.2–47.0, and 54.9–55.9° did not index to the accepted phases or plausible precursor oxides. It is matched by an R-3 Mn₃TeO₆ framework after Te→W substitution, yielding Mn₃WO₆. Competing Mn oxides and other transferred tungstate frameworks required implausibly large cell changes or gave much poorer residual-family coverage. No coherent unassigned reflection family remains in the final three-phase fit.

The refined fractions independently give a bulk Mn:W molar ratio of 1.010:1, consistent with the reported 1:1 precursor-metal dosing. This provides a chemical cross-check on both the phase set and its quantification.

## Refinement quality

The submitted `final.gpx` was replayed from its saved atomic and profile parameters on all 8,301 observed points without optimization. The independent replay gives Rwp = 2.18081%, Rp = 1.70283%, Rexp = 1.77000%, χ² = 12,545.19, and goodness of fit = 1.23209. The replay reported no negative-background, peak-width, occupancy, or displacement-parameter warnings. All three exported CIFs pass the broad short-contact/occupancy screen.

## Remaining uncertainty

The Mn₃WO₆ symmetry, lattice, and reflection-family assignment are well supported, but its atomic model is analogue-derived; in particular, its oxygen coordinates cannot be regarded as uniquely determined from this powder pattern alone. The WO₃ and Mn₃WO₆ peaks overlap substantially with MnWO₄, and scale, Uiso, weak texture, and profile terms remain correlated, so the displayed fraction digits describe the fitted normalization rather than absolute accuracy. An amorphous fraction cannot be measured without an internal standard, and is not included. Absolute lattice parameters also retain some correlation with zero/specimen displacement.
