# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled by three crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; no amorphous fraction or absolute yield can be obtained without an internal standard.

| Phase | Structural evidence | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| CaWO4, scheelite, *I* 41/*a* | Dominant lines at about 18.27, 28.43/28.77, 31.21, 33.85, and 46.89° 2θ; refined from COD 7219229 | 0.7982 (statistical s.u. 0.0024) |
| WO3, monoclinic, *P* 21/*c* | Diagnostic 22.77/23.18/23.99° triplet and matching higher-angle lines; COD 2311041 gave the best joint fit among tested WO3 references | 0.1364 (statistical s.u. 0.0006) |
| CaCO3, calcite, *R* -3 *c* | Coherent two-phase residuals at about 22.65, 29.06, 35.67, 39.16, 43, 47–49, and 57°; refined from room-temperature COD 1547347 | 0.0654 (statistical s.u. 0.0027) |

Calcite was not inferred from one isolated peak: with the CaWO4 and WO3 model and all existing nuisance parameters fixed, its scale converged repeatedly to a positive value and fresh full-grid Rwp decreased from 3.461% to 3.267%. Successful repeated common-state trials drove aragonite, vaterite, Ca3WO6, and CaO scales to zero. Failed preliminary trials were not used as rejection evidence. After the three-phase closure, no coherent unassigned peak family remained, so residual-directed prototype transfer was not invoked.

The final physically bounded model was selected by a new atomic full-grid replay, not by cached GSAS-II profile arrays. It gives Rwp = 3.2171%, Rp = 2.4830%, and χ² = 8723.27 for 8301 points (GOF 1.027; expected R factor 3.1382%). Preferred orientation, surface roughness, and active microstrain were tested but not retained; texture ratios refined essentially to one and strain improvements were negligible.

All submitted CIFs pass the supplied geometry/occupancy screen with no flagged hard contacts or close oxide O–O contacts. The phase frameworks and phase inventory are reliable. Exact light-atom coordinates are less certain: WO3 oxygen coordinates were retained from the reference, while the calcite oxygen coordinate and Uiso reached imposed physical bounds (the resulting C–O distance is 1.223 Å). Consequently the quoted least-squares fraction errors are conditional; calcite has an estimated systematic uncertainty of roughly one weight-percentage point from its correlated size, displacement parameter, and scale. The other fractions inherit that normalization uncertainty.
