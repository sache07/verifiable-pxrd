# Powder XRD phase analysis

Four crystalline phases are required. Weight fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; amorphous material cannot be quantified without an internal standard.

The native atomic forward replay gives Rwp = 2.729%, Rp = 1.975%, Rexp = 1.611%, and goodness of fit = 1.693 for 8,301 points with weights 1/max(observed, 1). The phase assemblage gives an overall Cu:W molar ratio of 1.009, independently consistent with the nominal 1:1 precursor ratio.

## Evidence and model selection

The principal triclinic CuWO4 model accounts for the strong families near 15.01, 18.81, 29.09, and 30.08 degrees 2theta. Cubic Cu3WO6 is independently required by families near 15.18, 19.80, 32.51, and 33.76 degrees. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the characteristic 23–24 degree group and reflections near 26, 28, and 33 degrees. Weak tenorite CuO accounts for the coherent reflections near 35.09 and 38.32 degrees.

Starting structures were COD 4000806 (CuWO4), 1527404 (Cu3WO6), 2311041 (WO3), and 1011194 (CuO). Cells, physically bounded atomic coordinates, one common isotropic displacement parameter per phase, phase scales, and supported profile/specimen terms were refined. All final structures pass contact and coordination checks; shortest metal–oxygen distances are 1.768 A (CuWO4), 1.769 A (Cu3WO6), 1.730 A (WO3), and 1.937 A (CuO). Alternative WO3 coordinate frameworks were distinctly worse or became chemically unreasonable. Removing CuO increased weighted residual chi-square by 38,840; replacing it with the clean Cc CuO reference increased it by 428, while retaining a stable approximately 2.3 wt% scale. The more economical C2/c tenorite model was therefore retained.

The bounded calibration selected a 780 micrometre specimen displacement and -0.0124 degree zero offset. A small [001] March–Dollase correction for Cu3WO6 and uniaxial WO3 microstrain were reproducible; surface roughness and additional phases were not supported. Every coherent residual reflection family indexes to an accepted phase, so no cross-chemistry phase transfer was needed.

## Remaining uncertainty

The tabulated ESDs are covariance-derived scale errors only and do not include reference choice, overlap, background, texture, or profile systematics. Practical fraction uncertainty is therefore larger, especially for the 2.46 wt% CuO component; its presence and C2/c topology are secure, but its crystallite size reached the allowed upper range and is not reliable. Oxygen coordinates in the minor Cu3WO6 and WO3 phases are less independently determined than the cells and heavy-atom substructures; they should be treated as physically constrained Rietveld estimates rather than high-precision single-crystal coordinates. No claim is made about amorphous content.
