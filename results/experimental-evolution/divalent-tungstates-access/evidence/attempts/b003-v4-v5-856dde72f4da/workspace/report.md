# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

The crystalline specimen is modeled by three phases. Fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases; no internal standard was present, so amorphous content cannot be measured.

| Phase | Space group | COD model | Weight fraction | Refined cell |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| MnWO4, wolframite | P 2/c | 1520200 | 0.6399 (63.99 wt%) | a = 4.79433, b = 5.70477, c = 4.97255 Å; beta = 91.1622° |
| Mn3WO6, rhombohedral | R -3 | 2002703 | 0.1868 (18.68 wt%) | a = 8.88497, c = 10.47697 Å |
| WO3, monoclinic | P 21/c | 2311041 | 0.1733 (17.33 wt%) | a = 7.68294, b = 7.52461, c = 10.51761 Å; beta = 136.1726° |

The WO3 cell is the valid nonstandard monoclinic setting used by the reference structure. The final full-pattern statistics are Rwp = 2.9605%, Rp = 2.1889%, and GOF = 2.0773 for all 8,301 measured points from 9.9034–100.0946° 2theta. Counting-statistical weights were used because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied. The frozen final model reproduces its stored calculated profile exactly in an independent zero-cycle replay.

## Evidence and model choice

The precursor context (Mn3O4 + WO3 heated at 800 °C) supports Mn/W oxides and tungstates. Wolframite MnWO4 explains the dominant families near 18.25°, 23.46°, 29.77/30.23°, 36.02°, and 51–53°. Mn3WO6 is required by distinct families near 19.68/20.18°, 28.47°, 35.00°, and 46.43°. Residual peaks near 22.89°, 23.46°, and 24.16°, together with their higher-angle family, require monoclinic WO3. The resulting crystalline inventory gives Mn:W = 1.028:1, chemically consistent with the Mn/W precursor system. No coherent unassigned reflection family remains.

Exact-composition WO3 references were tested. A numerically competitive model with an inherited 1.575 Å O–O contact was rejected. COD 2311041 retained physical geometry after refinement (W–O 1.711–2.155 Å; nearest O–O 2.534 Å). The other final structures are also physical: MnWO4 has W–O 1.809–2.100 Å and nearest O–O 2.437 Å; Mn3WO6 has W–O 1.902–1.940 Å, Mn–O 2.093–2.331 Å, and nearest O–O 2.608 Å. Bounded MnWO4 coordinates were supported by the profile. Mn3WO6 and WO3 light-atom refinements were not stable or significant, so their published atomic coordinates were retained while their cells, scales, texture, and supported profile terms were refined.

A trial Mn3O4 phase refined to only about 0.44 wt% and improved Rwp by about 0.004 percentage point, but that scale was driven mainly by an overlap near 35.8°. Most strong, more diagnostic Mn3O4 lines did not improve and several worsened. Mn3O4 therefore lacks broad-family support and is not included. Its presence or fraction cannot be determined reliably at this level; a trace below roughly 0.5–1 wt% remains possible.

## Uncertainty

The covariance-only fraction uncertainties are approximately 0.05–0.06 wt% absolute, but they do not include model choice, peak overlap, preferred orientation, profile mismatch, or possible amorphous material. A more realistic interpretation is about ±1–2 wt% absolute for the three normalized crystalline fractions. Phase identity, topology, and lattice metrics are well supported; the detailed oxygen coordinates of Mn3WO6 and WO3 are reference-model dependent rather than independently established by this powder pattern. Remaining residuals occur mainly on intense, overlapping peaks and are consistent with imperfect profile/intensity modeling rather than another coherent phase.
