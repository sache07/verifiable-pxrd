# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by four crystalline phases. Fractions below are normalized over the modeled crystalline material; they do not measure any amorphous content.

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction (1σ, formal) |
|---|---|---:|
| MnWO₄, wolframite | P 2/c | 63.81(39)% |
| γ-WO₃, monoclinic | P 2₁/c | 22.96(19)% |
| Mn₂WO₆ | Pbcn | 6.85(29)% |
| Mn₃O₄, hausmannite | I 4₁/amd | 6.37(35)% |

## Evidence and model selection

The Cu Kα₁/Kα₂ reflection pattern was refined over the supplied unsmoothed counts. Exact-composition references account for the dominant wolframite MnWO₄ family, a monoclinic γ-WO₃ family, and a weaker residual hausmannite Mn₃O₄ family. The remaining coherent low-angle and higher-angle reflections are indexed together by an orthorhombic Pbcn Mn₂WO₆ model. That model was made by full Mn substitution into the chemically nearest Fe₂WO₆ parent (COD 2310464), then refined against this specimen. Every coherent peak family is assigned by the four-phase model, and its bulk crystalline Mn:W molar ratio is 1.006:1, independently supporting the inventory.

Serious same-state alternatives were tested. MnO and Mn₃WO₆ scales collapsed under stable refinement. Mn₂O₃ and trirutile Mn₂WO₆ required a 5 nm size bound or developed nonphysical contacts; the Pbcn model retained sensible coordination. A triclinic WO₃ branch fit closely but heavy-atom relaxation drove a W–O bond to about 1.60 Å at its coordinate bound, so the physical monoclinic γ-WO₃ model was retained. A single mild γ-WO₃ [100] March–Dollase term (0.909) was supported; no additional texture or anisotropy family was introduced.

The final full-grid joint refinement gives Rwp = 16.687%, Rp = 10.315%, and goodness-of-fit = 8.386. A zero-cycle replay changes Rwp by only 0.000005 percentage point. The relatively high residual statistics reflect overlapping multiphase peaks, imperfect relative intensities/profile shape, and the absence of supplied pointwise uncertainties or a calibrated instrument profile, rather than a coherent unassigned peak family.

## Remaining uncertainty

The phase fractions are reliable enough to establish the major/minor inventory, but their listed 1σ values are least-squares-only uncertainties. They exclude reference choice, preferred orientation, microstructure, absorption, and other systematic effects; the two approximately 6–7 wt% phases therefore have larger practical uncertainty than their formal errors imply. The Mn₂WO₆ topology is the least secure structural assignment because it is transferred from an Fe analogue rather than an exact Mn₂WO₆ reference. Fine oxygen coordinates, especially in WO₃ and Mn₂WO₆, cannot be determined reliably from this overlapped laboratory pattern and were retained from physical reference/analogue geometries except for the individually supported Pbcn O3 adjustment. No amorphous fraction can be determined without an internal standard.
