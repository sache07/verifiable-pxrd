# b003-c01 oracle diagnostic sequence

This file is researcher-only diagnostic evidence. None of these private realization values or identities may enter a reusable package.

The accepted frozen realization is a full-reference three-phase case: MnWO4 (P2/c), Mn3WO6 (R-3), and clean monoclinic WO3 (P21/c), with 63.23/18.00/18.77 wt% recipe fractions. The known-structure oracle reaches Rwp 1.409% and recovers fractions within 0.000344. Omitting R-3 Mn3WO6 leaves a 6.089-noise-unit signal.

The realization is deliberately nontrivial despite exact availability. It has a high power-law background, nonzero angular/specimen displacement, surface roughness, small phase domains, and phase-specific preferred orientation and isotropic/anisotropic microstrain. Every registered structure also has its independent symmetry-preserving cell/coordinate mismatch. The exact Mn3WO6 parent is especially close structurally; most remaining difficulty after retrieval is profile/specimen execution.

An oracle-informed route to the floor is therefore:

1. Retrieve physical exact MnWO4, R-3 Mn3WO6, and clean WO3 references and establish positive scales in one multiphase project.
2. Fit the curved background without using it to erase coherent peaks; refine cells plus a conservative zero/displacement combination.
3. Refine phase sizes and broadening from accepted reflections, then test supported texture/anisotropy one family at a time with rollback.
4. Refine symmetry-allowed heavy and then oxygen coordinates in each accepted framework, retaining physical geometry.
5. Test roughness only in the complete model, replay the full grid, and submit the best physical checkpoint.

Diagnostic interpretation after P0 completes:

- Exact R-3 absent: residual-family recognition/search persistence is limiting even without reference adaptation.
- Exact R-3 present with wrong abundance or large D: reference retrieval works; ordinary multiphase profile/coordinate completion is limiting.
- Clean three-phase recovery: compare its sequence with tier-1 c06 and tier-2 harder-Mn to isolate topology-transfer failure.
