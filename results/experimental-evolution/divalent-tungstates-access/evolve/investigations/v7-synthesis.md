## Cross-case mechanism

The executable is not the bottleneck. In 17 audited solves its exact hash and
submitted-project identity are preserved; it improves Rwp by at least 0.02
point in 14 and safely returns baseline in 3, with no physical or export
regression. It is a bounded local finisher and should remain unchanged.

The dominant reusable defect is loss between enumeration and a genuinely
matched comparison. Clean ordinary parents or symmetry-distinct missing-phase
families are often listed and then omitted; c06 rep1 constructs R-3 but
rebuilds it outside the mature common state at the wrong metric and later gives
only C2/c the safer downstream method. Harder-Mn rep1 adds a prior failure:
a partial residual indexing becomes stoichiometric proof, so extensive later
search is confined to the wrong Mn2WO5 family and never reaches substituted
R-3 chemistry. The second defect is a discrete
setting/site registry: indexed positions with reversed intensities are
sometimes attacked with coordinates, texture or extra phases instead of a
normalized ordered-registry alternative. The third is false terminality:
replay stability alone permits gross GOF/residuals and near-boundary
broadening/calibration compensation.

Candidate 2 should therefore be a concise behavioral replacement, not more
search breadth or a stronger coordinate helper: require each serious
formula/topology family and at least one clean parent per accepted ordinary
formula to reach equal-depth construction and downstream adaptation;
normalize settings and trigger ordered registries only for systematic
indexed-intensity reversals; and reopen discrete choices when the alleged
terminal model is grossly compensating. A lower-symmetry candidate cannot win
merely because it alone received a safer coordinate/profile method. Redundant
numerical source failures may remain explicitly unknown, as b004 shows, so the
gate does not recreate v1/v4 prototype sprawl.
