# v2 package review

v2 is a guidance-only replacement for v1. It keeps the mechanism that worked on
the first c06 replicate: residual-triggered, cross-chemistry prototype transfer
and candidate additions/replacements in the current stable multiphase state. It
explicitly excludes isolated phase-only fits as evidence.

It removes v1's mandatory ledger, exhaustive same-formula gate, detailed fit
staging and one-term-at-a-time nuisance prescription. Those additions repaired
candidate validity but consumed attention and produced a 33.61-point regression
on the P0-solved c03 control, plus severe final-profile failures on c05 and
b002-c01. After phase closure, v2 explicitly returns control to ordinary PXRD
refinement and requires selection from a fresh physical full-grid replay.

No helper ships. The successful c06 worker found Mn3TeO6 COD 2211528 and built
R-3 Mn3WO6 from prose alone; the exploratory ranker has known false leads and
poor raw-mixture recall. The package contains no case answers or numerical
generator knowledge.
