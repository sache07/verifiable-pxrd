# Cycle 2 panel design

Cycle 1 retained P0 after v1-v3 all failed the fixed promotion rule. The next six-case active panel carries five unresolved mechanisms and replaces the now-reproducible b001-c05 with one new prospective control:

| Case | Role in cycle 2 |
|---|---|
| b001-c02 | Full-reference weak calcite persistence plus physical WO3 selection |
| b001-c03 | Full-reference four-phase inventory with unstable ordinary profile/coordinate refinement |
| b001-c06 | Tier-1 R-3 Mn3WO6 secondary adaptation |
| b002-c01 | Tier-2 hidden ZnWO4 with accepted-framework coordinate-basin variance |
| b002-c02 | Tier-2 R-3 Mn3WO6 secondary adaptation |
| b003-c01 | New full-reference R-3 Mn3WO6 near-matched control at 18 wt% |

b001-c05 moves to occasional regression-control status after v3 recovered its exact physical three-phase inventory in all three runs. It is not considered permanently solved: its earlier package variance remains a warning against regressions, but keeping it in every active panel would duplicate the harder b002-c01 mechanism and consume one of six comparison slots.

The full-reference/tier-1/tier-2 Mn3WO6 ladder is intended to identify where topology recovery fails. It distinguishes failure to recognize the residual phase family when the exact R-3 record is searchable from failure to retrieve and adapt a symmetry-matched analogue. Package changes in cycle 2 must remain reusable and may use only chemistry, phase closure, residual families, physical screening, and controlled candidate comparison; no case-specific identity or coordinates may enter a package.
