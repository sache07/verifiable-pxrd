## Outcomes as collected

- **c01 replicate 1 — recovered, not floor:** score 85.75, D 0.152, Rwp 2.802 versus oracle 2.632. The solver did search beyond exact/one-substitution chemistry, adapting mixed Fe/Mn hubnerite COD 9008131 to ZnWO4 rather than using the prospectively identified NbInO4 family. This is still a two-element tier-2 route: both disordered divalent species were collapsed to Zn while W and the wolframite topology were retained. It recovered ZnWO4/WO3/ZnO at 81.88/13.18/4.94 wt% versus 82.00/13.32/4.68 wt%, with ZnWO4 site accuracy 92.95. The remaining gap is downstream refinement: WO3 oxygen refinement was rolled back and its site accuracy is 34.5; zero and specimen shift remain correlated; the submitted model lacks the generating roughness and does not recover ZnO's axial strain/true texture axis. Broad prototype construction itself succeeded.
- **c01 replicate 2 — recovered, not floor:** score 79.56, D 0.334, Rwp 3.032. A manually adapted Co/Ni-wolframite prototype reached ZnWO4 site accuracy 97.0 and the solver ultimately retained clean WO3 plus 5.16 wt% ZnO. It initially declared ZnO unsupported, then obeyed residual closure after improving the dominant atomic model: the coherent 32.05/34.9/36.6 degree family and higher-angle partners lowered replay Rwp from 4.84 to 3.03 when ZnO was restored. Fractions were within 1.21 percentage points. The remaining gap is a distorted/equivalent WO3 fit with only 26.2 site accuracy and correlated displacement/profile terms, not inventory failure.
- **c01 replicate 3 — inventory found, operationally unrecovered:** score 51.51 provisional, D 2.191, Rwp 4.684. It independently adapted hubnerite COD 9008131 and then a NiWO4 COD 5910277 coordinate start, recovering ZnWO4/WO3/ZnO at 81.81/12.99/5.19 wt%. It nevertheless retained the older monoclinic WO3 model with four ~1.58 A O-O contacts after explicitly identifying that defect, rather than testing the clean COD 2311041 framework. Instrument X and polarization and minor-phase sizes also approached bounds. The hidden-framework challenge was solved, but physical candidate rejection and nuisance regularization failed.
- **c02 replicate 1 — composition found, topology unrecovered:** score 22.89, D 26.596, Rwp 9.703. The solver persisted beyond MnWO4 + WO3 and submitted 60.13/20.16/19.71 wt% MnWO4/WO3/Mn3WO6, close to the generating fractions. Its Mn3WO6 was instead a wrong C2/c Co3TeO6-derived framework with site accuracy 12.73, so only two of three frameworks were recovered. A broad residual prototype screen did include the intended surviving R-3 Cd3TeO6 route, but ranked it far below the C2/c candidates by a peak-position heuristic; the solver then queried and constructed only space-group-15 parents. This is not a failure of phase closure or target stoichiometry. It is premature symmetry commitment: a powder fingerprint should shortlist rather than eliminate distinct plausible framework families before common-state Rietveld tests.
- **c02 replicate 2 — residual pursued, substituted chemistry excluded:** score 24.34, D 58.819, Rwp 13.435. The solver searched all W-containing oxide/tungstate records plus Mn-O and transformed foreign cations to Mn, but that element filter made the surviving Cd3TeO6 and other Te-derived R-3 parents unreachable. It generated Pnma and Fm-3m Mn3WO6 candidates without importing them into a common-state test, then selected a P212121 Y2WO6-derived Mn2WO6 model at 15.22 wt% and added 3.38 wt% MnO. Those additions made the overall Mn/W ratio nearly exactly 1:1 and were physically valid, but the hidden 20 wt% R-3 Mn3WO6 framework remained wholly missing. Composition balance and a broad residual-profile rank reinforced the wrong model after the topology search was truncated.
- **c02 replicate 3 — target-system decoy proliferation:** score 17.27, D 44.919, Rwp 11.872. Its residual screen explicitly required every candidate element set to be contained in Mn-W-O, so the withheld-analogue challenge was impossible to solve. It refined a six-phase inventory of MnWO4, triclinic WO3, wurtzite MnO, rutile MnO2, Mn3O4, and a provisional Pnnm W-O framework adapted from Li2WO4. The last model lacked any defensible cation site and its fraction depended on the incomplete cell mass, yet all six decoys were retained while structured residual remained. No Mn3WO6 composition or R-3 family was constructed.

Across c01, analogue construction and complete inventory are 3/3, scorer recovery is 2/3, and the noise floor is 0/3. This does not justify an unranked enumeration helper; it does validate v1's separate same-formula physical-screen, phase-closure and conservative-refinement gates.

Across c02, recovery is 0/3 with scores 22.89/24.34/17.27. The three independent paths fail at complementary points covered by the same compact intervention: one enumerates R-3 but drops it by fingerprint rank, one excludes substituted B-site chemistry and leaves generated models untested, and one restricts the entire search to Mn-W-O before proliferating decoys. The generic residual ranker is not a safe v1 helper because the intended route ranked poorly in replicate 1 and in the research-only raw-mixture test. Freeze v1 as guidance-only.
