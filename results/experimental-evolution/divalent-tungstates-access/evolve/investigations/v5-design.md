# v5 design draft: checkpoint-only control

Status: cycle-2 control ready to freeze after the completed b003 prospective comparison.

v5 isolates the attention-management hypothesis from v4's new topology-coverage rule. Cycle-1 v3 showed that reflection-family closure and accepted-model completion can recover c05 3/3 and harder-Zn 2/3, but it also regressed c03 despite exact inventory 3/3. The ordinary failures were not caused by missing candidates; they ended with weak coordinate/profile convergence after substantial optional work. Solver trajectories also spend early attention reading autonomous-research, generator, and scorer sources that are not specimen evidence.

The intervention therefore asks for an early complete physical checkpoint, strict reflection-family closure, immediate termination of library search at closure, and staged ordinary refinement from the best saved state. It preserves generic cross-chemistry transfer but does not prescribe formula/topology enumeration. This should minimize regressions on full-reference c02/c03 and preserve P0's own scientific judgment. Its contrast with v4 is deliberate: any relative improvement on tier-1/tier-2 Mn3WO6 measures the value of explicit topology-family coverage, while relative improvement on c03 measures that rule's attention cost.

The completed b003 comparison scored 46.58†/41.94†/48.73 (mean 45.75), selected exact R-3 3/3, and recovered 0/3. Two runs chose flagged WO3 for negligible checkpoint gains; the clean exact-inventory run remained profile-limited. Frozen predictions are: at least 2/3 recovery on both c02 and c03; exact inventory 3/3 and at least 2/3 recovery on harder-Zn; b003 exact R-3 3/3, full physical inventory at least 2/3, at least one recovery, and mean at least 50.00; no case mean more than 15 below P0; complete valid submissions 18/18; and a nonnegative aggregate delta. Mn3WO6 topology recovery and the noise-adjusted promotion threshold are explicitly not predicted under this control.
