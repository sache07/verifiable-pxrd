## Risks to test prospectively

- A mandatory ledger can recreate v1/v4 attention cost and prototype sprawl.
  Restrict the gate to already enumerated serious candidates and stop at
  physical closure.
- Registry alternatives can multiply branches. Activate only after peak
  positions index but accepted-reflection intensities remain systematically
  wrong.
- Residual indexing may have aliases. Use a symmetry-consistent observed
  metric as a common seed, not as proof of topology or stoichiometry. Index
  the full linked family and retain serious alternative indexings until equal
  development separates them.
- A physical-contact audit must flag rather than repair structures and must
  avoid embedding case IDs, source IDs, coordinates or hidden thresholds.
- Stronger terminality language must not encourage unsupported phase additions;
  center/wing residuals still call for source/registry/profile review, not
  automatic new phases.
