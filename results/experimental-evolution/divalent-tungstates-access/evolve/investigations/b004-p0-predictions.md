# Prospective b004 P0 transfer predictions

Recorded after `b004-transfer` was accepted but before any of its three jobs
completed or scored. The case and realization were curated and frozen before
this rollout.

## Mechanism expectations

- The exact published major Ba3W2O9 and physical monoclinic WO3 references are
  available. Both should be selected in all three solves; a failure to find the
  major is recognition/search failure rather than missing-reference transfer.
- The 5 wt% retained BaCO3 has a 2.64-noise-unit omission signal and several
  family members. It should survive a common-state addition in at least two of
  three solves, but earlier calcite evidence makes inconsistent retained-source
  closure a realistic risk.
- The heavy-atom major and high-count pattern should make formula/fraction
  recovery easier than fine oxygen-coordinate and nuisance completion. Exact
  inventory need not imply a noise-floor fit.

## Frozen case gates

1. Ba3W2O9 and WO3 selected 3/3.
2. BaCO3 selected and exact physical three-phase inventory retained at least
   2/3.
3. At least one formal recovery and case mean at least 60.
4. No solve may use a physically flagged source as a numerical substitute for
   one of the three available clean references.

These gates diagnose P0 on a new case; they do not alter the fixed package
promotion rule or claim learned transfer because P0 is the incumbent.

## Outcome

All gates pass. Scores are 86.51, 62.88, and 78.36 (mean 75.92); hidden D is
0.203, 1.021, and 0.405. Every solve selects exact physical COD 1533878
Ba3W2O9, COD 2311041 WO3, and COD 9010928 BaCO3, with maximum fraction errors
of 0.13, 1.07, and 0.86 percentage point. Two solves formally recover and none
reaches the floor. The source-residue mechanism is reliable here; remaining
variance is profile, calibration, and light-atom completion.
