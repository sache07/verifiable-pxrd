## Minimal behavioral change

1. Only when one coherent family remains genuinely unassigned, form a short
   reaction-balanced formula list and preserve symmetry-diverse compatible
   parents from substituted chemistries; do not restrict the search to the
   sample elements. For each serious topology preserve at least two physical
   starts when available. Convert candidates to the target composition and
   give them identical cell and coordinate adaptation before choosing and
   before adding texture or other nuisance terms. Early fit or substitution
   count is not decisive. Stop this search once physical closure is reached.
2. Before the sole local polish, require replay stability and finish supported
   center/wing profile corrections for accepted phases. Roll back any term
   outside the supplied laboratory ranges, pinned at a tested bound, or
   inseparable from a correlated alternative. After polishing, do only
   read-only geometry/replay/export checks: adding phases or refining
   coordinates, cells, profile, calibration, background, scales or nuisance
   terms means the checkpoint was not terminal.
3. Raise only the helper's fixed coordinate stabilization cap from three to
   five, as licensed by `ptest-v7-morepasses-04`; preserve every existing
   convergence, rollback, fraction and geometry gate.

The likely v7 package is v6's physical-closure core plus focused
equal-depth-parent and terminal-operation gates, with the licensed five-pass
helper. Do not ship coordinate-transplant code or donor
answers. A small multi-project comparison wrapper is optional only if it can
verify identical inventory/topology and select among solver-created candidate
projects; the solver must still construct the independent parent starts.
