## Cross-case mechanism

The dominant failures occur before fine least squares: incomplete reference/prototype enumeration, absent hard geometry rejection, premature phase closure, and planned candidate branches that are interrupted yet treated as negative results. Once the inventory is right, uncontrolled nuisance refinement creates a second failure mode through correlated terms and numerical branch failure. Package v1 should therefore encode one short causal sequence: enumerate same-formula and topology/stoichiometry analogues broadly; construct target-composition candidates; hard-screen geometry; complete comparisons from a shared conservative fit state and mark failed/unrun branches unknown; map coherent residuals before declaring closure; then stage cell/coordinates/profile complexity with rollback and require broad residual support for anisotropy, texture or roughness. A small helper may make candidate construction/screening reproducible, but case formulas, IDs and hidden effects must remain outside the reusable package.
