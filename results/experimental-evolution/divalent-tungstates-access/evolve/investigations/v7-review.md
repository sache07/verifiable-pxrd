# v7 package review

## Package

`helpers/staged_polish.py`. The helper SHA-256 is
`954ecebf0bbb6445cdac938860abb83f8c0c55ba4789fcdd9c87af0a8349d6fe`.

## Learned change

The guidance keeps v6's stop-at-physical-closure core but narrows the missing
phase intervention. Only while one coherent family remains genuinely
unassigned, the solver forms a short reaction-balanced formula list, retains
symmetry-diverse parents from substituted chemistries, preserves two physical
starts for a serious topology when available, and compares candidates after
identical cell/coordinate adaptation. This targets c06 and harder Mn without
restoring v4's broad always-on coverage.

Before the sole helper invocation, the accepted model must be replay-stable
and complete in profile as well as inventory. Calibration/profile terms that
are outside the lab ranges, pinned at a tested bound, or inseparable from a
correlated alternative are rolled back. After invocation, only read-only
geometry, replay, and export checks are allowed. This targets premature helper
use on c02/c03/harder Mn and the nuisance-bound variance on harder Zn, b003,
and b004.

## Evidence and exclusions

The 18-project five-pass test scored 18/18 with ten improvements, eight
unchanged results, no worsening and no physical flags; all prior outcomes were
retained, and the causal c03 target reproduced D 3.558 to 0.330. This licenses
only the constant change.

Held-out b004 P0 transfer selected exact physical Ba3W2O9/WO3/BaCO3 inventory
3/3 and recovered 2/3 at mean 75.92. It therefore does not justify an extra
source-specific instruction. Its D spread of 0.203 to 1.021 despite exact
inventory instead supports the completion and nuisance-correlation gates.

The package does not contain source CIFs, case identities, hidden cells,
coordinates, fractions, truth-derived numerical targets, donor projects,
coordinate transplantation, or case lookup rules. It does not mandate broad
prototype search after closure. The behavioral changes remain unvalidated
until the fresh six-case rollout; only the five-pass executable has procedure
evidence.
