# Powder XRD phase analysis

The crystalline model contains four phases. Weight fractions are normalized over those modeled crystalline phases:

| Phase | Structural assignment | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| CaWO4 | Scheelite, I 41/a | 69.85% |
| WO3 | Monoclinic gamma-WO3, P 21/n (No. 14) | 16.90% |
| Ca3WO6 | Distorted double-perovskite-like intermediate, P 1 | 12.53% |
| CaO | Lime, Fm-3m | 0.72% |

Scheelite CaWO4 is established by its full peak series, including the strong features near 18.6, 28.6, 31.3, 34.0, 39.0 and 47.0 degrees 2theta. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the characteristic 23–24.5 degree cluster and many weaker high-angle lines. Trace CaO is supported principally by the 111/200 reflections near 32.1 and 37.4 degrees.

The initially unassigned series near 19.35, 22.04, 30.65, 36.54, 45.1 and 50.8 degrees is accounted for by Ca3WO6. A Ba3WO6 double-perovskite analogue was converted to Ca3WO6, its two split half-occupied Ca pairs were merged into full sites, and its cell/profile were refined. This model both outperformed reduced CaWO3/tungsten-bronze alternatives and gives an overall Ca:W molar ratio of 1.008, consistent with the 1:1 precursor charge.

The independently replayed final project gives Rwp = 5.261% and Rp = 3.951% over all 8301 measured points. Remaining mismatch is concentrated at the most intense, overlapping scheelite peaks; no coherent residual series supports a fifth crystalline phase.

The Ca3WO6 formula and phase contribution are well supported, but its exact P1 atomic coordinates are not uniquely determined by this overlapped powder pattern and should be regarded as an analogue-derived working structure. Its closest modeled O–O contact (1.80 Å), and a short O–O contact in the imported WO3 model (1.52 Å), warrant chemical caution; the WO3 oxygen sublattice is less reliable than its phase identity and lattice. CaO is near the quantitative detection limit, so its 0.72% fraction has substantially larger systematic uncertainty than the formal scale error (roughly ±0.5 wt% is realistic). The major-phase fractions are likely uncertain at about 1–2 wt% from structural, texture and profile correlations. No amorphous fraction was determined.
