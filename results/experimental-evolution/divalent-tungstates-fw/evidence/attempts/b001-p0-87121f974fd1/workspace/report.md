# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

The pattern is described by three crystalline phases: wolframite-type ZnWO4 (P 2/c), wurtzite ZnO (P 63 m c), and monoclinic WO3 (P 21/c). The refined crystalline-phase fractions are 72.17 wt% ZnWO4, 7.33 wt% ZnO, and 20.50 wt% WO3. These values are normalized over the modeled crystalline material; an amorphous fraction cannot be determined from this scan without an internal standard.

The final fit has Rwp = 2.916%, Rp = 2.238%, Rexp = 2.687%, and GOF = 1.085 (8301 points, 30 refined parameters). A fresh native GSAS-II replay regenerated the atomic structure factors and reflection lists with no physical-review flags.

## Evidence

ZnWO4 accounts for the dominant wolframite reflections, including the low-angle peaks near 15.8 and 19.2 degrees and the dense 24-37 degree group. Because the exact Zn-W-O entry was withheld from the supplied search library, its starting model was made from the isostructural MgWO4 model COD 9013902 by Mg-to-Zn substitution, followed by cell and symmetry-constrained coordinate refinement.

Residual peaks at about 31.8, 34.5, and 47.6 degrees identify wurtzite ZnO (COD 9004178). The characteristic WO3 cluster at about 23.4-24.7 degrees and additional peaks near 33.6-34.5 degrees select the monoclinic P21/c model COD 2311041. WO3 required a modest [001] March-Dollase correction (ratio about 1.29). The residual ZnO:WO3 mole ratio is 1.02:1, independently consistent with partial conversion of a nominal 1:1 ZnO/WO3 charge.

## Uncertainty

The phase inventory and wolframite/wurtzite/monoclinic assignments are well supported by multiple non-overlapping reflections. Fractions for the minor ZnO and textured WO3 phases are correlated with preferred orientation and overlapping peaks; a practical uncertainty of roughly 1-2 wt% is more realistic than the least-squares precision. The ZnWO4 topology and heavy-atom sites are reliable, but its oxygen coordinates inherit some analogue-model uncertainty. WO3 oxygen coordinates are weakly constrained by X-ray contrast and were regularized to the documented reference-mismatch range; its refined Uiso reached the allowed lower bound, so that displacement parameter is not independently reliable. No amorphous or unidentified crystalline fraction is quantified.
