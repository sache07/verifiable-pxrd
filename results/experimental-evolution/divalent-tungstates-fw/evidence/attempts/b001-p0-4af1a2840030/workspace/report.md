# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is adequately described by one crystalline phase: scheelite-type CaWO4 (space group I 41/a), with a crystalline-phase-normalized weight fraction of 1.000. The refined cell is a = b = 5.24425 A and c = 11.37963 A. The structural model was initialized from COD 7219229 and refined in GSAS-II; the submitted CIF contains the refined cell, oxygen position, and common isotropic displacement parameter.

The assignment is supported by the complete strong-peak sequence at approximately 18.63, 28.72, 29.04, 31.43, 34.18, 39.20, 47.10, 49.10, 54.29, 57.89, and 59.47 degrees 2theta. Resolved high-angle partners are reproduced by the stated Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 doublet. Reference-pattern checks found no unassigned peak set for residual CaO, monoclinic WO3, Ca3WO6, or the alternate monoclinic CaWO4 polymorph. This is also chemically consistent with reaction of CaO and WO3 at 850 degrees C.

The native atomic-model replay over all 8,301 points gives Rwp = 3.5469%, Rp = 2.7246%, weighted chi-square = 8279.21, and GSAS-II goodness of fit = 0.9995. The difference curve is noise-like, and no specimen roughness, preferred orientation, or anisotropic-strain term was statistically justified. The Ca/W framework and scheelite symmetry are reliable; the oxygen coordinates are less strongly constrained by laboratory X-ray data. Angular zero, specimen displacement, polarization, and displacement parameters are correlated nuisance quantities.

The reported 1.000 fraction is normalized only over detected crystalline phases. Trace secondary phases below the profile residual, strongly overlapping components, and amorphous material cannot be quantified reliably from this scan, so no fraction is assigned to them.
