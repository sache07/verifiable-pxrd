# Powder XRD phase analysis

The scan is described by four crystalline phases. The quantitative Rietveld result, normalized over those phases, is:

| Phase | Structure | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| CaWO4 | scheelite, I4_1/a | 0.63345 (63.35 wt%) |
| WO3 | monoclinic, P2_1/c | 0.27304 (27.30 wt%) |
| CaO | rocksalt, Fm-3m | 0.02681 (2.68 wt%) |
| CaCO3 | calcite, R-3c | 0.06671 (6.67 wt%) |

## Evidence

Scheelite CaWO4 is identified by the coherent series at approximately 18.69, 28.80, 31.65, 34.19, 47.21, and 57.82 degrees 2theta. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the diagnostic 23.21/23.66/24.44 degree triplet and the additional lines near 33.34, 41.57, 48.28, and 55.97 degrees. Full-profile tests favored the P2_1/c WO3 model over the triclinic and higher-symmetry alternatives; a modest [111] March-Dollase correction and [100] uniaxial microstrain were supported by the split WO3 peaks.

The weak but reproducible CaO reflections near 32.26, 37.42, and 53.88 degrees require a rocksalt CaO component. Calcite is supported most clearly by its residual precursor lines near 29.48 and 36.05 degrees, with its other lines overlapping the tungstate/oxide pattern. Removing either minor phase leaves systematic residual peaks.

The refined cells are a = 5.25984, c = 11.34190 A for CaWO4; a = 7.31234, b = 7.55268, c = 10.54173 A, beta = 133.0936 degrees in the submitted P2_1/c WO3 setting; a = 4.81792 A for CaO; and a = 4.99250, c = 17.07552 A for calcite. Small symmetry-preserving coordinate adjustments were made for CaWO4 and WO3. All submitted structures pass the geometry/occupancy screen.

The final full-range fit has Rwp = 2.7913%, Rp = 2.0385%, goodness of fit = 1.0085, and Durbin-Watson = 1.996. Independent regeneration from `final.gpx` reproduced the same curve and phase fractions with no profile-review flags.

## Uncertainty

The phase identities and four-phase inventory are well supported. Formal scale uncertainties understate correlations with background, roughness, preferred orientation, and peak broadening; a practical uncertainty is roughly 1-2 percentage points for each major phase and about 0.5 percentage point for each minor phase. The detailed oxygen coordinates, especially in the minor calcite phase, are less reliably determined than the phase type and lattice. The fractions are normalized only over modeled crystalline material: the scan does not provide an absolute amorphous fraction. No additional crystalline component is resolved above the counting-noise residual.
