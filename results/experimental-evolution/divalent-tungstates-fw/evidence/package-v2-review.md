# v2 prospective review

## Intervention

v2 remains guidance-only. It retains v1's successful anti-leakage, physical-source, ordered-analogue, trace-deletion, selective-coordinate, and replay gates. It replaces the over-conservative stability rule with one compact principle: early source and one-at-a-time effect tests create shortlists; final selection uses cumulative, restarted models judged by the stability of the forward curve, fractions, and structures rather than by coefficient independence.

The new text specifically requires:

- final matched swaps for near-tied same-composition sources and analogue parents after inventory/nuisance stabilization;
- inclusion of composition/topology-nearest analogue parents even when their unadapted sticks are not closest;
- a complete nuisance pass covering joint as well as separate angular correction, common profile/asymmetry, roughness/polarization, and every phase's size/strain/texture;
- re-equilibration and retesting of plausible earlier failures after major model changes; and
- acceptance of a correlated nuisance subspace only when independent starts reproduce its predictions without frozen or physically invalid variables.

No case answer, hidden value, source ID, coordinate, fraction, or simulator-derived bound is present in the package.
