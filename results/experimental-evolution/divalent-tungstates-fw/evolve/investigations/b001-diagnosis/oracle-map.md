# b001 oracle mechanism map

Private research note; do not place case-specific values or lookup rules in a learned package.

| Case | Inventory mechanism | Dominant nuisance/effect mechanism | Sequence expected to approach the noise floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| c01 | Single exact CaWO4 | No zero/specimen effects; only instrument/background and modest reference mismatch (0.035% cell, O coordinates) | Retrieve scheelite; establish correct Cu Kα doublet and smooth background; refine scale/profile and cell; then oxygen coordinates/Uiso with stability checks; save/export the same final GPX. |
| c02 | Exact BaCO3 + WO3, near 46:54 wt% | High background floor; -0.140° zero plus -713 µm displacement, surface roughness, broad Lorentzian profile; both cells differ by 0.37–0.49% | Fit both phases before interpreting residuals; refine background/scale, common angular terms and cells in a staged/correlated way; add roughness and phase size/texture only after stable indexing; verify fractions and full-grid replay. |
| c03 | Exact CaWO4/WO3 plus 6.75% CaCO3 and 2.83% CaO | Four phases; -313 µm shift, roughness; very small domains; anisotropic strain/texture in carbonate and WO3 | Identify/refine the two majors, inspect structured residuals for both weak precursor/decomposition phases, add them without renormalizing away missing mass; stabilize scale/cell/angular terms; then introduce phase-specific size/strain/texture and surface roughness. |
| c04 | Hidden 72% ZnWO4, exact ZnO + WO3 | Missing principal; modest shift/zero; ZnWO4 strain, WO3 texture, exponential background | Treat the strong unindexed inventory as missing reference, retrieve wolframite relatives, substitute Zn into tier-1 MgWO4 and refine its cell before detailed profile terms; add residual ZnO/WO3; refine shift/profile/strain/texture; keep the adapted CIF and GPX synchronized. |
| c05 | Exact major CaWO4/WO3 plus hidden 13.9% Ca3WO6 | Missing secondary ordered phase; roughness; small CaWO4/WO3 domains; anisotropic WO3 strain/texture | Complete obvious phases first, use residual peak positions and Ca-rich local chemistry to seek ordered A3BO6/double-perovskite relatives; adapt tier-1 Ca2CoWO6 by Co->Ca and allow the ~4% cell adjustment; then refine its fraction with specimen terms. |
| c06 | Hidden 66.0% PbWO4 plus exact PbCO3/WO3 | Missing principal with 5.65% analogue cell gap; -794 µm displacement; PbCO3 microstrain/texture; low counts and weak residuals | Recognize scheelite framework, adapt CaWO4 by Ca->Pb and refine cell aggressively but physically; search residuals for both cerussite and WO3; refine common shift/zero, phase profiles and texture, preserving all three phases through final replay/export. |

Cross-case prediction: inventory incompleteness should dominate c03/c05/c06; analogue construction should dominate c04–c06; profile/replay discipline should remain measurable even in c01. A package should therefore emphasize a short residual-driven loop, explicit framework substitution when chemistry predicts an absent phase, and a final GPX/CIF/fraction consistency gate. Exact numerical nuisance values above diagnose mechanisms but are not reusable guidance.
