# v4 prospective review

## Intervention

v4 remains guidance-only. It preserves v3's anti-leakage, physical-source, residual-led inventory, cumulative nuisance, predictive-stability, deletion, and replay rules. It narrows one unresolved analogue-selection defect: credible framework parents now receive equal restrained, blockwise coordinate adaptation before final parent selection. Early fixed-coordinate Rwp or scale collapse cannot eliminate a chemically/topologically credible parent; failed branches must still be restaged. Coordinate blocks must repeat across nuisance starts and pass physical screening.

This separates framework merit from the accidental coordinate quality of a particular published parent. It also says how to resolve equivalent lineages: if they converge to the same physical structure and profile, choose the simpler or chemically nearer provenance rather than a negligible fit difference. No case answer, source identifier, hidden coordinate, fraction, nuisance value, or lookup rule is included.
