## Post-result diagnosis

The score decomposes to only 3.96 profile points but 81.82 structural points. Thus the catastrophic headline regression is profile underfit, not inventory loss. The worker reached lower intermediate replayed fits when intensity corrections were active, but rejected them because repeated alternations changed the individual polarization coefficient. It then fixed polarization to an assumed standard value, omitted roughness and strain, fixed common profile terms, retained only a six-term background, and restored all coordinates to their parents. The final project is physical and internally replayable but remains at the early source-screen basin.

v6 over-corrected v5 in two ways. First, moving structure recovery before profile completion encouraged arbitrary displacement caps and extensive custom trust-step machinery; the package's physical/restart language was interpreted as requiring exact stationary endpoints rather than predictive stability of the curve and structure. Second, its shorter analogue wording was not followed: many parents were screened, but only the early winner received connected coordinate treatment. This loses the behavior v5 had already demonstrated.

The successor should revert to v5's full cumulative profile and explicit equal-finalist language. The only retained v6 insight is narrow: after profile stabilization, coordinate testing needs a completeness ledger over all defensible independent ligand/site blocks; a transient short contact from an isolated move should trigger a connected restrained retry. It must explicitly forbid arbitrary parent-displacement caps and avoid changing the successful inventory/profile ordering.
