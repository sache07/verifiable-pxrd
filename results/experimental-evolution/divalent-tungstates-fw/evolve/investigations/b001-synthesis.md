# b001 cross-case synthesis

The baseline overturns the initial prediction that inventory incompleteness would dominate. P0 consistently found weak phases and built withheld products: every c02/c03/c04/c06 run has the correct intended inventory, and all three c05 runs recover hidden Ca3WO6 (one also retains false trace CaO). The large score variance arises mainly **after** phase discovery, when source, nuisance, profile and structural branches are selected. Best attempts already reach the noise floor on five cases, so the intervention should preserve their scientific search while preventing unstable late choices.

## Cross-case mechanism

Three capabilities should remain untouched: multi-line residual completion, chemically guided framework substitution, and independent GPX replay/export. They solve the intended hard problems. Four late-stage behaviors explain most avoidable loss:

1. Simulator leakage: LAB/runtime/scorer values were repeatedly converted into coordinate targets, nuisance bounds, allowed axes, Uiso equality, or expected perturbations. These are facts about synthetic generation, not experimental evidence.
2. Lowest-Rwp selection without stability: zero/shift/cells co-drifted, parameters hit boundaries or froze, correlations exceeded 90%, and tiny effect gains were retained. Near-noise observed Rwp still gave poor hidden-profile agreement.
3. Source/parent asymmetry: candidates were compared with unequal structural freedom; review-trigger sources were repaired after fitting despite passing alternatives, and analogue parents were sometimes ranked by sticks rather than the same restrained whole-pattern model.
4. Soft final audits: solvers detected invalid contacts or implausible nuisance values but disclosed them instead of rejecting the branch.

## Package decision

Draft v1 is guidance-only (419 words). It makes simulator blindness, pre-fit physical screening, equal low-flexibility source/parent comparison, multication-parent eligibility, trace-phase retesting, fixed-structure nuisance staging, second-start stability, evidence for added effects, and a hard final physical/replay gate explicit. It also forbids chemistry-fixed fractions. No helper is justified yet: P0 already executes every needed operation; the failure is ordering and scientific model selection. Expected effects are unchanged c01 performance, reduced c03/c04 variance, rejection of c02/c06 review or correlated branches, and better c05 parent/profile selection.
