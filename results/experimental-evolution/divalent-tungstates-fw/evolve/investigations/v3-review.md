The revision closes the common c04/c05 analogue-selection gap:

- preserve several chemistry- and topology-nearest same-framework parents through the final cumulative comparison even when their crude early Rwp values are not near-tied;

- treat a failed, bound-hitting, or nonconvergent credible-parent swap as inconclusive, restaging it from lower flexibility rather than silently dropping the parent.
