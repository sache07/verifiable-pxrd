# v9 design

v9 is a minimal replacement for v8. It preserves verbatim the prospectively
validated CIF-health workflow, explicit inactive-HAP values, representative
numerical profile check with a required correction, final replay/schema guard,
and both executable helpers. No new tool or automatic post-processing step is
added.

Five concise guards address only the three v8 failures. Reported precursor
amounts and ratios are fixed evidence rather than free balance coefficients.
The leading balance-motivated analogue must be constructed, justified,
health-screened and scale-tested under matched nuisance terms before rejection.
A weak new phase requires a positive replay-stable scale and at least two
independent non-coincident residual families, so global fit gain or one
overlapped line cannot establish it. Bound nuisance terms are reset before
their residuals motivate inventory additions. Texture is restricted to
no-texture versus symmetry-distinct low-index axial/diagonal March-Dollase
comparisons; correlated high-order harmonics are rejected.

These rules are generic and contain no case identity, source ID, hidden
fraction, coordinate, fitted parameter or lookup rule. They restore protections
present in v6 rather than encoding v8 outcomes. The initial v9 screen will use
only unresolved b001-c03/c05/c06. The three v8 successes are set aside and will
be run as a validation screen only if the unresolved mechanisms pass.
