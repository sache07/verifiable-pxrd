# Phase analysis and refinement

The crystalline pattern is modeled with three phases: 76.32 wt% wolframite-type MnWO4, 19.61 wt% monoclinic WO3, and 4.07 wt% tetragonal hausmannite Mn3O4. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases only.

MnWO4 is established by the characteristic P 2/c line set at approximately 15.1, 18.1, 23–24, 29.6/30.0, 35.7, 37.0, and 40–41 degrees 2theta and by the Mn-W-O synthesis context. The residual WO3 precursor is required by its diagnostic low-angle triplet near 22.7, 23.1, and 24.0 degrees and matching lines near 26.2, 28–29, 33–34, and 41–42 degrees. Weak but coherent reflections at the expected shifted positions for Mn3O4 support the third phase. Trial scales for MnO, Mn2O3, and Mn3WO6 refined to zero, whereas Mn3O4 remained significant.

The final joint GSAS-II refinement used both Cu K-alpha wavelengths, a refined common zero offset, a 12-term Chebyshev background, empirical shared peak-shape terms, phase scales and cells, and isotropic size broadening for WO3. MnWO4 coordinates were refined; the broader/minor WO3 and Mn3O4 atomic coordinates were held to reference models while their cells and fractions were refined. Refined cells are:

- MnWO4, P 2/c: a = 4.79504 A, b = 5.70226 A, c = 4.97190 A, beta = 91.1297 degrees.
- WO3, P 21/n: a = 7.28970 A, b = 7.53910 A, c = 7.68195 A, beta = 90.9065 degrees.
- Mn3O4, I 41/amd: a = 5.76091 A, c = 9.49176 A.

The final fit has Rwp = 5.58%, Rp = 3.93%, Rexp = 2.48%, and goodness-of-fit = 2.25 using Poisson weights inferred from the counts. The covariance-only fraction e.s.d.'s are about 0.14–0.21 percentage points, but they understate model uncertainty. Background/profile sensitivity suggests a more realistic uncertainty of roughly +/-3 percentage points for the two major fractions and +/-1 point for Mn3O4. No amorphous fraction can be determined without an external standard.

The MnWO4 topology and all three phase identities are reliable. The exact low-symmetry distortion of the broad WO3 component is less secure: P 21/n is the best chemically and diffraction-consistent room-temperature model, but closely related triclinic/pseudomonoclinic WO3 cannot be distinguished decisively here. Some systematic difference intensity remains around sharp MnWO4 peaks, consistent with unresolved anisotropic line broadening; consequently the refined oxygen coordinates and formal statistical fraction errors should not be overinterpreted.
