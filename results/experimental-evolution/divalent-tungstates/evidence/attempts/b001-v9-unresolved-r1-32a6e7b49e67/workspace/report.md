# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

The pattern is best modeled with three crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases:

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| CaWO4 | scheelite, `I4_1/a` | 78.60 wt% |
| WO3 | monoclinic, `P2_1/c` | 11.73 wt% |
| Ca3WO6 | monoclinic `P2_1/c` analogue | 9.67 wt% |

The final full-range refinement has Rwp = 2.863%, Rp = 2.230%, Rexp = 2.247%, and goodness-of-fit = 1.274. The internal counting-statistical uncertainties on the fractions are 0.055, 0.032, and 0.042 wt percentage points, respectively, but model/systematic uncertainty is larger, especially for Ca3WO6.

## Phase evidence and balance

The CaWO4 model is the scheelite COD 7219229 structure. Its alternative monoclinic record gave a clearly poorer diffraction match. The selected WO3 model is COD 2311041. A triclinic WO3 competitor gave a nearly indistinguishable profile fit but refined toward monoclinic metric symmetry; the room-temperature monoclinic model was therefore retained. A reviewed WO3 record with a 1.575 A O-O contact was not justified by its negligible fit gain.

The third phase is assigned as Ca3WO6. It was constructed from the health-passing Ca3OsO6 parent COD 1562666 by the isovalent Os6+ to W6+ substitution, then its cell and scale were refined. Before its cell was released, its scale was positive and unchanged on project reopening/replay, and it explained independent residual families near 19.29, 22.01, 30.71-32.18, 37.53, 45.13, 57.02, and 65.78 degrees 2theta. Adding it reduced Rwp from 9.397% to 6.333% at fixed cell and to 5.211% after cell refinement, before the final profile optimization.

No precursor amounts or CaO:WO3 ratio were reported, so a numerical precursor balance could not be imposed or varied. The fixed chemical context gives charge-neutral formulas Ca_m W_n O_(m+3n); Ca2WO5, Ca3WO6, Ca3W2O9, and Ca5W2O11 were considered. The final crystalline fractions independently imply Ca:W = 0.994 and O:W = 3.994 on a molar basis. Thus they nearly close an equimolar CaO + WO3 balance, but this is supporting evidence rather than an input constraint. The expected equimolar relation, moles(WO3) = 2 moles(Ca3WO6), is met within about 4.6%.

CaO remained on the watch list. Its final matched-nuisance test gave a replay-stable 0.21 wt% scale, but it improved only overlapped shoulders: its 37.12 degree line coincides with Ca3WO6/WO3 intensity and the residual there is already negative, while its other lines do not supply two independent non-coincident residual families. It was therefore not retained. Ca3W2O9 refined to approximately 0.002 wt% with uncertainty much larger than its value, and Ca2WO5 pinned at zero scale.

## Profile model and checks

The refinement used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and fixed 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson count weights, eight background coefficients, a nonnegative common U/W/X profile, refined polarization, and one angular correction: 340.34 micrometres specimen displacement. A constant zero correction was inferior. CaWO4 required an apparent isotropic coherent size of 0.0928 micrometres and a replay-stable [110] March-Dollase ratio of 1.2648. No texture was retained for WO3 or Ca3WO6. Surface roughness was rejected because it was 96.6% correlated with the dominant phase scale; strain, SH/L, extinction, and other inactive terms were reset to neutral values.

Representative observed/calculated FWHM checks in degrees 2theta are 0.21810/0.21762 at 18.43 degrees, 0.16722/0.16652 at 23.42 degrees, 0.26789/0.26826 at 39.03 degrees, and 0.47884/0.49718 at 81.99 degrees. Low-, middle-, and high-angle positions agree to about 0.00-0.01 degrees for the isolated checks. The remaining goodness-of-fit above unity is dominated by localized intensity/overlap mismatch rather than a coherent missing peak family.

All submitted CIFs pass the final occupancy/contact health screen. Their shortest relevant coordination distances are chemically plausible: CaWO4 W-O 1.763 A and Ca-O 2.47 A; WO3 W-O 1.714-2.158 A; and Ca3WO6 W-O 1.924-1.939 A and Ca-O 2.25-2.60 A, with no short O-O contacts.

## Remaining uncertainty

The Ca3WO6 formula and phase family are strongly supported by the residual families, replay-stable scale, and emergent Ca/W balance, but its submitted coordinates are an isovalent structural analogue rather than an independently solved structure. Only cell, scale, and profile-related parameters were refined; atomic coordinates were not refined from this multiphase powder pattern. Its exact polymorph is therefore less certain than the CaWO4 and WO3 assignments. Fractions exclude any amorphous material and should be interpreted as normalized crystalline fractions.
