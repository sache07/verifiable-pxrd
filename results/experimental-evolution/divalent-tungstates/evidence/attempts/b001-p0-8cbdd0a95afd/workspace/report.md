# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is adequately described by one crystalline phase: scheelite-type CaWO4, space group I 41/a (No. 88). Its weight fraction is 1.0000 after normalization over the modeled crystalline phases. This normalized value should not be read as proof of absolute sample purity or as a measurement of any amorphous content.

The assignment is supported by the CaO + WO3 reaction context and by the complete scheelite reflection sequence. Examples are the observed/calculated peak positions at 18.607/18.601, 28.702/28.691, 29.028/29.011, 31.419/31.403, 34.157/34.138, 39.167/39.153, 47.077/47.060, and 49.055/49.050 degrees 2theta. All 82 allowed reflections in the fitted range are accounted for, with no systematic unmatched peaks. The refined tetragonal cell is a = 5.249016 A and c = 11.386324 A (V = 313.7179 A^3).

The final GSAS-II refinement used all 8301 points from 9.9034 to 100.0946 degrees 2theta, a Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 model with the reported 0.5 intensity ratio, and Poisson weights (sigma(I) = sqrt(I)) because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied. It converged without singular values at Rwp = 2.3241%, Rp = 1.7872%, Rexp = 2.2809%, and goodness of fit = 1.0204. The close match to the counting-statistics limit and the nearly unit-width standardized residual distribution support the one-phase model.

Plausible impurities were tested rather than assumed. Monoclinic WO3 refined to 0.022(37) wt% and is not detected; its conservative mean-plus-3-sigma upper bound is about 0.14 wt% for that structural model. CaO gave a borderline 0.16(7) wt% scale (only 2.3 sigma), a poor phase R factor, and no decisive unique peak; it was therefore not retained, with a conservative upper bound of about 0.4 wt%. A Ca3WO6 trial collapsed to the zero-scale boundary, so its fraction cannot be quantified reliably, but its diagnostic peaks are absent. Trace phases below these pattern- and model-dependent limits remain possible.

The Ca and W special-position coordinates were fixed by symmetry, while the oxygen coordinates and cell were refined from the powder data. Atomic displacement parameters were retained from the reference structure. Because no calibrated instrumental profile was supplied, size/microstrain terms are empirical profile parameters and should not be interpreted as an independently measured crystallite size or strain.
