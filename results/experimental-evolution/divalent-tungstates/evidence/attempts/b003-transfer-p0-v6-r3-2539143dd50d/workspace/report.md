# Crystalline phase analysis

## Result

Three crystalline phases are required. Weight fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; they do not include any amorphous component.

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, °) | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| NiWO4, wolframite | P 2/c (No. 13) | a = 4.59935, b = 5.66259, c = 4.91110, beta = 90.5022 | 0.6023 (60.23 wt%) |
| WO3, monoclinic | P 21/c (No. 14) | a = 7.68175, b = 7.54439, c = 10.50789, beta = 136.0250 | 0.2989 (29.89 wt%) |
| NiO, rocksalt | F m -3 m (No. 225) | a = 4.16481 | 0.0988 (9.88 wt%) |

The GSAS-II formal standard uncertainties on the fractions are 0.00050, 0.00040, and 0.00055, respectively. These are counting-statistical values and understate the systematic uncertainty.

## Evidence

The precursor chemistry and 700 °C treatment are consistent with formation of NiWO4 plus unreacted oxides. The observed pattern contains independent diagnostic reflections for all three phases: NiWO4 at approximately 15.62, 19.27, 30.85/31.03, and 36.58° 2theta; the characteristic monoclinic WO3 triplet at 23.14, 23.56, and 24.38° (with another group near 33.28–34.16°); and rocksalt NiO at 37.34, 43.42, 63.07, and 75.55°. No coherent set of residual peaks requires another crystalline phase.

Starting structural models were based on COD 5910277 (NiWO4), 2311041 (WO3), and 1010093 (NiO). The refinement used all 8,301 points from 9.9034 to 100.0946° with the supplied Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio fixed. Since pointwise uncertainties were absent, Poisson counting weights were assigned from the measured counts. Phase scales and cells, zero, selected common profile terms, effective polarization, phase broadening, and the X-ray-sensitive heavy-atom coordinates were refined. Oxygen coordinates were retained from the reference structures.

The final fit has Rp = 1.819%, Rwp = 2.372%, Rexp = 1.448%, and goodness of fit = 1.643. Remaining residual structure is concentrated around the strongest overlapping peak groups and is consistent with profile-shape limitations rather than an omitted phase.

## Uncertainty and limitations

The instrument profile was not calibrated and the effective polarization was not supplied. Reasonable alternative profile treatments changed the fractions by about one absolute percentage point, which is a more realistic uncertainty scale than the formal errors. NiO contributes only six distinct reflection families in the fitted range, so its crystallite-size and strain broadening cannot be separated reliably; the submitted model uses size-only broadening. Two refined WO3 tungsten x coordinates are about 97% correlated, and the oxygen coordinates and displacement parameters are not independently determined by this multiphase laboratory X-ray pattern. The phase identities, unit cells, and approximate 60:30:10 crystalline weight ratio are nevertheless robust.

An external standard would be required to quantify amorphous material, so the reported fractions apply only to the modeled crystalline portion.
