## Evidence and model selection

CaWO4 is fixed by the strong scheelite families at approximately 18.43°, 28.54°, 31.27°, 34.01°, 39.03°, 46.93°, 54.11°, 57.70°, 59.29°, and 76.05° 2theta. The 298 K scheelite source model (COD 7219229) fits, whereas a high-pressure fergusonite CaWO4 record gives a grossly inferior fit. Monoclinic WO3 is independently supported by its diagnostic 22.94°/23.42°/24.23° triplet and multiple higher-angle families. The selected neutron structure (COD 2311041) has a physically complete distorted WO6 environment; its beta near 136° is the valid native, nonconventional P 21/c cell setting. Both retained structures are stoichiometric with fully occupied sites; exported geometry gives CaWO4 W–O = 1.755 Å and Ca–O = 2.383–2.549 Å, and WO3 W–O = 1.713–2.157 Å. Alternative room-temperature monoclinic, low-temperature triclinic, and high-temperature orthorhombic/tetragonal/hexagonal records were challenged under the same nuisance model; none supplied a physically preferable improvement.

The accepted baseline uses the supplied Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and ratio, a six-term smooth Chebyshev background, one refined zero correction (-0.2007°), and a common nonnegative profile with U = W = 0 fixed and X = 9.29 refined. Isolated low-, middle-, and high-angle families show no remaining systematic position, FWHM, or asymmetry trend. Polarization was refined because it corrected a coherent cross-phase intensity trend. Repeated asymmetry did not support refining SH/L, which remained fixed at 0.002. Roughness was rejected as a highly correlated scale/background surrogate. For CaWO4 only, replay-stable [110] March-Dollase texture (1.262), [110] uniaxial Lorentzian size (0.117/0.189 µm), and isotropic Gaussian microstrain (4271 µstrain) were supported across multiple isolated families. WO3 size/strain terms collapsed to neutral bounds and were removed. The CaWO4 oxygen position and W Uiso were refined; extra Ca/O displacement parameters were not retained.

## Remaining uncertainty

CaO was kept on the weak-phase watch list. A fixed-cell trial produced a formal value near 1.2 wt%, but it improved only overlapped shoulders, worsened several other expected CaO regions, and a free-cell trial drifted to an implausible lattice parameter. It is therefore not reliably identified and is excluded from the quantitative inventory; roughly 1–2 wt% trace CaO cannot be ruled out. Other challenged watch-list phases refined to zero scale. The covariance-only fraction esds in `result.json` are much smaller than source/profile systematics; about 1–2 wt% absolute is a more realistic uncertainty on either reported fraction.
