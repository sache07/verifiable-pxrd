# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline model contains three phases (fractions normalized over these crystalline phases): MnWO4 (wolframite, P 2/c) 80.93 wt%, monoclinic WO3 (P 21/n) 14.69 wt%, and hausmannite Mn3O4 (I 41/amd) 4.37 wt%. Formal least-squares fraction s.u.'s are 0.15, 0.07, and 0.16 wt%, respectively; model/profile systematics are larger (about 1 wt% absolute, especially for the minor Mn3O4 fraction).

## Evidence

Wolframite MnWO4 explains the dominant paired reflections at about 29.58/30.03 degrees and the lines at 15.11, 18.06, 23.29/23.80, 35.7, and 53.1 degrees. A one-phase fit left a coherent residual family near 22.7, 33, 41.5, 49.5, and 55.5 degrees; monoclinic WO3 reduced Rwp from about 10.8% to about 4.5% and fit that full family substantially better than the tested orthorhombic, tetragonal, or triclinic WO3 alternatives. Weak independent residuals near 31.8 and 59.3 degrees support tetragonal Mn3O4; adding it lowered chi-square by about 1000 in the controlled comparison. The reconstructed precursor molar ratio from the three fractions is close to 3 WO3 : 1 Mn3O4, consistent with partial conversion to MnWO4.

The final full-range fit has Rp = 3.070%, Rwp = 4.200%, Rexp = 2.490%, and goodness of fit = 1.691 (reduced chi-square 2.858). Poisson weights (1/counts) were used because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied.

## Structural model and uncertainty

The refined MnWO4 cell is a = 4.80067, b = 5.70895, c = 4.97767 A, beta = 88.8709 degrees; its atomic coordinates were refined. The WO3 and Mn3O4 cells were refined, but their internal coordinates were retained from reference structures because unconstrained coordinate refinement is not reliable at 14.7 and 4.4 wt%. The metadata provide no calibrated instrumental profile; peak-shape terms and specimen displacement are therefore correlated with absolute cell metrics, so the very small formal cell s.u.'s underestimate calibration uncertainty. No amorphous content or undetected trace phase is quantified, and the reported fractions are normalized only over the modeled crystalline phases.
