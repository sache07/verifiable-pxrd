## Evidence and model selection

The chemistry is consistent with the reported CaO + WO3 precursor pair heated at 850 °C. No precursor amounts were supplied, so no reagent ratio or mass-balance constraint was imposed. Scheelite CaWO4 accounts for the dominant families, including the strong peaks near 18.43, 28.54, 31.27, 34.01, and 46.93° 2theta. The sharp triplet at approximately 22.94, 23.42, and 24.23° and many independent higher-angle lines select monoclinic WO3.

Coherent residual families near 18.71–19.30, 21.96–22.02, 30.7–32.2, and 36.6° required a calcium-rich, charge-balanced phase. A Ca3WO6 model was made by the isovalent W6+-for-Os6+ substitution in a Ca3OsO6 parent (COD 1562666), followed by cell refinement. A structurally distinct Ca3UO6-derived parent collapsed to zero scale. The submitted Ca3WO6 model therefore explains the diffraction families and composition, but is an analogue rather than a direct database determination.

Trace CaO retained a positive replay-stable scale. Its 111, 200, 220, and 311 families occur near 31.91, 37.04, 53.49, and 63.75°; at least the 111 and 220 evidence is independent of strong coincident reflections from the other phases. Its fraction remained about 0.28–0.30% under accepted background/profile variations.

Source challenges did not support additional polymorphs. A fergusonite CaWO4 record collapsed to the scale floor when added and fit very poorly as a replacement. An orthorhombic Pnma WO3 competitor remained much worse after equivalent elaboration (Rwp 8.10% versus 2.90%); tested triclinic and tetragonal WO3 records were also poorer or produced unphysical profile values. Same-formula coexistence was therefore rejected.

## Refinement and profile quality

The final full-range GSAS-II replay used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson counting weights because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, a 14-term smooth Chebyshev background, one zero-angle correction, and nonnegative U/W/X profile terms. Supported additions were sample roughness, CaWO4 [110] March-Dollase texture (ratio 1.2617), and symmetry-compatible CaWO4 [001] uniaxial size broadening (0.0934(8) micrometres basal and 0.0869(8) micrometres axial). Phase strain, extra axial-divergence, polarization, and other phase texture/width terms were unsupported, unphysical, or unstable and were pruned.

Final statistics are Rwp = 2.8951%, Rp = 2.2353%, GOF = 1.2914, and chi-squared = 13780.9 for 8301 observations and 38 variables. Representative observed/calculated FWHM pairs are 0.2185/0.2172° at 18.43°, 0.1683/0.1662° at 24.23°, 0.2483/0.2487° at 28.54°, 0.3496/0.3500° at 57.70°, and 0.4672/0.4717° at 76.05°. Regional Rwp values are 2.30%, 2.97%, and 3.35% for the low-, middle-, and high-angle ranges, respectively.

## Remaining uncertainty

All four final CIFs pass the supplied geometry/occupancy health screen. That screen does not establish polymorph identity. In particular, the exact Ca3WO6 atomic arrangement cannot be determined reliably from this powder pattern alone: its cell and fraction are well supported by multiple families, but its fixed fractional coordinates inherit the Ca3OsO6 analogue. The 0.285% CaO result is near the practical trace-phase limit; its presence is supported, but its fraction should not be interpreted to the formal uncertainty alone. The quoted standard uncertainties are least-squares values and omit source-model, background, microabsorption, and specimen-preparation systematics.
