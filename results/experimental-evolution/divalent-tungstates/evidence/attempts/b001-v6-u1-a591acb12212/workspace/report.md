# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline product is **scheelite CaWO4** (space group *I* 41/*a*, No. 88). Its refined tetragonal cell is a = 5.248704 ± 0.000033 Å and c = 11.385632 ± 0.000062 Å. The crystalline-phase-normalized weight fraction is 1.000; this normalization does not measure amorphous material.

## Evidence

The room-temperature, fully occupied scheelite record COD 7219229 accounts for all major and weak observed families, including the peaks near 18.61, 28.70, 29.03, 31.43, 34.17, 47.08, 57.84, and 76.13° 2theta. The refined geometry remains chemically normal (four W–O bonds about 1.76 Å and eight Ca–O bonds about 2.47 Å). The high-pressure monoclinic CaWO4 polymorph is inconsistent with both the 900 °C ambient-pressure synthesis context and the observed peak positions.

The final full-range replay used a six-coefficient smooth Chebyshev background, one zero correction, and a nonnegative common U/W/X profile. The unknown effective polarization refined to 0.6983; specimen roughness, phase size/strain, texture, extinction, and Babinet terms were neutral. Observed/calculated FWHM values agree at representative isolated low-, mid-, and high-angle families (about 0.155/0.155° at 18.61°, 0.220/0.223° at 47.08°, and 0.212/0.218° at 82.05°). The final statistics are Rwp = 2.3504%, Rp = 1.8170%, Rexp = 2.2809%, GOF = 1.0313, R(F²) = 3.2217%, and Durbin–Watson = 1.867.

## Alternatives and uncertainty

Ca3WO6 and ambient monoclinic, triclinic, and orthorhombic WO3 records all refined to zero scale under the same common profile. CaO is the only marginal watch-list candidate: a two-phase trial gave 0.18(7) wt%, but almost all improvement came from one weak 200-region residual near 37.25°; the other expected CaO families did not improve coherently, and BIC favored the one-phase model. CaO was therefore not quantified as a phase; a model-conditional trace-level upper bound is roughly 0.4 wt% (3 sigma).

Atomic coordinates and isotropic displacement parameters were retained from the screened scheelite source while the cell was refined. ADP trials were strongly correlated with the unknown polarization (maximum correlation about 0.98), so more detailed atomic refinement is not reliable from this pattern. Likewise, instrumental Lorentzian broadening and crystallite size are indistinguishable without a calibrated instrument profile; no crystallite size is reported. Trace phases below the stated sensitivity and amorphous content remain possible.
