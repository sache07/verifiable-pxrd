# PXRD phase analysis

The modeled crystalline material contains three phases:

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| WO3 (monoclinic gamma-WO3) | P 1 21/c 1 (No. 14) | 60.00 wt% |
| Cu3WO6 | P a -3 (No. 205) | 33.82 wt% |
| CuO | C 1 2/c 1 (No. 15) | 6.17 wt% |

Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases. They do not measure amorphous material or any component not represented by a crystalline model.

## Evidence

The WO3 assignment accounts for the characteristic low-angle triplet at approximately 23.21, 23.66, and 24.45 degrees 2theta and its many higher-angle split reflections. Its very intense first member and the corresponding higher-order reflections require strong [10-1] fiber texture in the submitted nonstandard P21/c cell; the refined March-Dollase coefficient is 0.7166.

Cu3WO6 is supported by reflections at approximately 15.75, 20.37, 33.08/34.30, 58.19, 63.88, and 70.79 degrees, including peaks not explained by WO3. Minor CuO is supported jointly by its reflection set near 35.5, 38.7, 48.9, 53.4, 58.4, and 61.5 degrees, much of which overlaps the two major phases. Adding CuO reduced Rwp from 4.44% to 3.31% and refined to a conventional CuO cell. Candidate CuWO4, Cu2O, and Cu4O3 scales refined to zero. A triclinic WO3 alternative gave a poorer fit and nonphysical displacement parameters, so the monoclinic model was retained.

The final GSAS-II refinement used the supplied 9.903-100.095 degree profile, Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths of 1.540598/1.544426 angstrom with the reported 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson weights inferred from the raw counts, a 15-term Chebyshev background, and a pseudo-Voigt profile. Phase scales and cells, zero offset, common profile coefficients, selected heavy-atom coordinates and isotropic displacement parameters, and WO3 preferred orientation were refined. Oxygen coordinates were held at reference values because unconstrained oxygen refinement produced chemically impossible W-O distances.

The final agreement factors are Rwp = 3.307%, Rp = 2.571%, Rexp = 2.409%, and goodness of fit = 1.377 for 8301 points and 49 refined parameters.

## Remaining uncertainty

The covariance-only fraction ESDs are 0.13, 0.10, and 0.16 wt% for WO3, Cu3WO6, and CuO, respectively, but these understate model uncertainty. The instrument profile was not independently calibrated, WO3 is strongly textured, and the minor CuO reflections overlap heavily. A more realistic practical uncertainty is about +/-2 wt% absolute for each major phase and +/-1-2 wt% for CuO. The phase identities and unit cells are well supported; detailed oxygen positions are not independently determined by this dataset.
