# Phase analysis and quantitative refinement

The pattern is described by three crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over those modeled crystalline phases:

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| CaWO4 | Scheelite, `I 41/a`, COD 7219229 | 78.603 ± 0.078 wt% |
| WO3 | Room-temperature monoclinic, `P 21/c`, COD 2311041 | 11.583 ± 0.049 wt% |
| Ca3WO6 | Monoclinic `P 21/c` prototype derived by isovalent W6+ for Os6+ substitution in Ca3OsO6, COD 1562666 | 9.814 ± 0.057 wt% |

The scheelite and monoclinic WO3 assignments are supported by complete low-, middle-, and high-angle reflection families and are chemically consistent with the WO3/CaO precursor mixture heated at 850 °C. The Ca3WO6 model accounts for coherent families near 19–22°, 31–32°, 37°, 45°, and 49–57° that remain in the two-phase fit. No exact Ca3WO6 database entry was available: several stoichiometric analogues were screened, the Ca3OsO6 parent was physically acceptable, and an alternative Ca3UO6 parent refined to zero scale.

Refinement used all 8,301 unmodified observations, the reported Cu Kα1/Kα2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson count weights, a smooth six-term Chebyshev background, one common zero correction, and a nonnegative common W/X profile. Low-angle roughness, CaWO4 [110] March–Dollase texture, and complementary isotropic CaWO4 size/strain terms were retained by checkpointed trials. SH/L asymmetry, specimen displacement, polarization refinement, anisotropic broadening, and broadening or texture for the minor phases were unsupported or unstable and were removed. The final background is smooth and positive, and isolated peaks across the scan show consistent positions, widths, and asymmetry.

The required staged heavy-atom test accepted both WO3 W sites and one Ca site in the Ca3WO6 model. Their largest moves were 0.076, 0.066, and 0.050 Å; the gain survived reopening, no variables froze, and the final minimum interatomic distances are 1.762 Å (CaWO4), 1.675 Å (WO3), and 1.923 Å (Ca3WO6). Other heavy sites failed the 0.003 percentage-point Rwp gain threshold and remain fixed.

Final fit statistics are Rwp = 3.887%, Rp = 3.108%, Rexp = 2.247%, goodness of fit = 1.734, and χ² = 24,841.81 for 8,301 observations and 35 refined variables. The last maximum shift/esd is 0.0066.

## Remaining uncertainty

The reported fraction uncertainties are formal GSAS-II values and understate model uncertainty: no pointwise uncertainties or internal standard were supplied, the CaWO4 scale and roughness remain correlated, and no amorphous fraction can be measured. In particular, the Ca3WO6 oxygen framework/polytype is not uniquely determined by this powder pattern; its identification and fraction are more model-dependent than the formal ±0.057 wt% suggests.

CaO, Ca(OH)2, CaCO3, and a Ca2WO5 analogue were re-challenged against the final common profile. Hydroxide and carbonate scales collapsed to zero. Fixed-cell CaO and Ca2WO5 trials gave only 0.15(5) and 0.11(4) wt%, respectively, with Rwp gains of 0.0018 and 0.0015 percentage point; CaO lacked consistent companion-family support and the Ca2WO5 cell-refined trial was unstable. These traces cannot be identified or quantified reliably and are not included. Consequently, unidentified crystalline material at roughly the 0.1–0.3 wt% level cannot be excluded.
