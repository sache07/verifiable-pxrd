# Phase analysis and refinement

The modeled crystalline assemblage is:

| Phase | Structure | Weight fraction (wt%) |
|---|---|---:|
| PbWO4, stolzite | I 41/a (COD 9009811) | 58.09(15) |
| WO3, monoclinic | P 21/c (COD 2311041) | 20.92(10) |
| Pb3O4, minium | P 42/m b c (COD 9012124) | 16.33(14) |
| PbO, massicot | P b c m (COD 2310433) | 4.65(9) |

Fractions are normalized over these four crystalline phases. The final full-range replay gives Rwp = 4.150%, Rp = 3.102%, Rexp = 2.485%, and goodness of fit = 1.673 (8301 points, 30 refined variables). All phase scales are positive, no variables are frozen, and the final maximum shift/esd is 0.002.

## Evidence and physical inventory

The broad stolzite backbone accounts for the dominant families near 27.8, 33.2, 45.1, 51.6, 55.6, and 72.3 degrees 2theta. Sharp monoclinic WO3 is diagnosed by the 23.53/24.02/24.85 degree triplet and repeated middle- and high-angle families. Minium accounts for families near 26.8, 31.2, and 32.6 degrees, while massicot accounts for the residual families near 29.6, 30.9, 38.3, and 53.9 degrees. The largest remaining positive residuals coincide with reflections of retained phases rather than forming an unassigned peak family.

The selected records have the required occupied compositions, correct symmetries, no partial-occupancy disorder, and plausible geometry. Exported minimum distances include W-O = 1.777 A and Pb-O = 2.615 A in stolzite, W-O = 1.716 A in WO3, Pb-O = 2.131 A in minium, and Pb-O = 2.168 A in massicot; no short O-O contact is present. The stolzite and minium parents are synthetic 290-293 K records; the WO3 and massicot parents are neutron-diffraction structures. Refined cells were exported, while atomic coordinates were retained from these screened parents.

Raspite, an older distorted stolzite entry, three alternate WO3 polymorphs, a low-temperature Pb3O4 polymorph, and litharge PbO were screened with the same provisional nuisance schedule and failed by unique-peak mismatch, implausible geometry/import behavior, negligible scale, or inferior fit. Searches for Pb2WO5/Pb3WO6 cross-chemistry prototypes did not produce a candidate matching the diagnostic reflections. The refined assemblage also gives Pb/W = 1.010 on a molar elemental basis, consistent with the precursor balance.

## Profile decisions and uncertainty

After the inventory stabilized, the profile was restarted with roughness, texture, extinction, displacement, and unused HAP terms neutral. A single zero correction refined to +0.44049 degrees. Common X was independently calibrated from resolved WO3 lines and fixed at 5.693 centidegrees; nonnegative U/W and bounded SH/L branches sought bounds or were unstable. Phase-by-phase tests selected isotropic size broadening for PbWO4, Pb3O4, and PbO and isotropic strain for WO3. A global polarization branch hit its bound and was rejected. All symmetry-distinct texture tests returned March-Dollase factors of 0.989-1.022 with negligible improvement, so the final model has no texture.

The parent structures and four phase identities are robust, but the precision of the fractions is lower than the formal least-squares uncertainties. The data supplied no pointwise uncertainties, so Poisson count weights were used; microabsorption in this Pb/W mixture, unknown effective polarization, source-coordinate errors, and profile/scale coupling can produce several-wt% systematic error, with the 4.65 wt% massicot fraction least secure. Atomic-coordinate changes cannot be determined reliably from this mixture and were not refined. No amorphous fraction, unidentified crystalline fraction, or absolute total crystallinity can be determined without an internal standard; all reported fractions are crystalline-normalized.
