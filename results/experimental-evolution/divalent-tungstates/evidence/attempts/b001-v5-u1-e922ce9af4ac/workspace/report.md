# Crystalline phase analysis

The Cu Kα pattern is described by four crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized over these phases only.

Parenthetical uncertainties are one-standard-deviation least-squares values in the last shown digits. The full-range fit from 9.9034° to 100.0946° 2θ gives Rwp = 3.0249%, Rp = 2.3146%, and GOF = 1.2192 for 8,301 points.

## Evidence

The chemistry is consistent with reaction of the reported WO3 and Pb3O4 precursors at 500 °C to form scheelite PbWO4, with residual oxides. PbWO4 is supported by its 27.81°, 33.16°, 45.13°, and 55.63° families. The resolved WO3 triplet at approximately 23.53°, 24.02°, and 24.84°, together with the 33.7–34.6° and 41.9–42.3° groups, selects monoclinic γ-WO3. Pb3O4 has distinctive reflections near 14.66°, 26.81°, 31.22°, 52.52°, 63.61°, and 66.40°. Massicot is required by a consistent set near 29.53°, 30.78°, 33.08°, 38.22°, 49.64°, 51.21°, 53.57°, 56.52°, 60.73°, and 63.40°; adding it reduced the provisional Rwp from 4.60% to 3.44% before the final common-profile pass.

All retained source records are ordered, fully occupied structures with physically reasonable contacts. Alternate records and polymorphs were challenged with the same nuisance model. Older source records for each major phase fitted less well; raspite PbWO4, triclinic/orthorhombic WO3, low-temperature orthorhombic Pb3O4, and litharge PbO failed their diagnostic reflections or gave substantially poorer fits. A PbO2 challenge refined to only 0.043(24) wt% (about 1.8σ) and was excluded; the data do not reliably establish PbO2, with an approximate two-sigma crystalline upper limit of 0.1 wt% under this model.

## Refinement model and uncertainty

The submitted `final.gpx` is the reopened, three-times replayed full-range project used for the CIFs, fractions, and `fit.csv`. It uses the reported Cu Kα1/Kα2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, an eight-coefficient smooth background, one refined zero correction, and a common fixed U/W/X/SH-L profile established from resolved low-, middle-, and high-angle families. Zero-only fitted slightly better than specimen-displacement-only; because no calibration standard was supplied, the 0.44708° zero correction makes the absolute lattice parameters more systematically uncertain than their very small formal e.s.d.s suggest.

Cells and phase scales were refined; source atomic coordinates and full occupancies were retained because this overlapped multiphase powder pattern does not determine independent coordinates or site occupancies reliably. Supported phase broadening terms were size-only for PbWO4 (about 59 nm) and Pb3O4 (about 33 nm), and strain-only for WO3 (about 1,118 µstrain) and PbO (about 10,904 µstrain). Only Pb3O4 retained weak [110] March-Dollase texture, 0.9587(73), reproduced from two interior starts. Other texture axes returned effectively random values.

A shared surface-roughness correction was strongly supported, but its two free coefficients were 99.43% correlated with a phase scale. They were therefore conditioned at their jointly fitted interior values (A = 0.16371, B = 1.19063) for the final scale refinement. The final active parameters are interior, no covariance correlation exceeds 0.90, and the last replay had maximum shift/e.s.d. 0.001. Formal fraction uncertainties consequently understate model uncertainty; a practical uncertainty of roughly one percentage point is more realistic, especially for the broadened 5 wt% PbO phase. Amorphous content cannot be determined without an internal standard, so no absolute bulk fractions outside the modeled crystalline fraction are claimed.
