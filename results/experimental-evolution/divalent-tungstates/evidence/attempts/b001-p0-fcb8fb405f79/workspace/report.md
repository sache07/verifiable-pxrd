# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is best described by three crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over these phases only; amorphous material, if present, is not included.

| Phase | Structural model | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| CaWO4 | scheelite, *I* 41/*a* (COD 7219229) | 87.50 wt% (formal s.u. 0.17 wt%) |
| WO3 | monoclinic, *P* 21/*c* (COD 2311041) | 11.09 wt% (formal s.u. 0.12 wt%) |
| CaO | rocksalt, *F m* -3 *m* (COD 1563196) | 1.41 wt% (formal s.u. 0.13 wt%) |

## Evidence

The dominant scheelite CaWO4 model accounts for the characteristic reflections near 18.43, 28.54, 31.27, 34.01, 39.03, 46.93, 54.12, 57.72, and 76.04 degrees 2-theta. Monoclinic WO3 is required by the resolved 22.95/23.42/24.23 degree triplet and reflections near 26.43, 33.10/33.43, 41.30/41.76, and 49.83 degrees. A small but coherent rocksalt CaO contribution accounts for weak intensity near 32.16, 37.18, 53.34, 64.38, 67.42, and 79.14 degrees. This assemblage is chemically consistent with partial reaction of the supplied CaO and WO3 precursors to CaWO4.

The Rietveld fit used the full 9.90--100.09 degree range, Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and intensity ratio from the metadata, a 12-term Chebyshev background, refined zero offset, phase scales and cells, and an empirical profile. An isotropic size term and fourth-order cylindrical spherical-harmonic preferred orientation correction were used for the dominant CaWO4 phase. Atomic coordinates were retained from the cited reference structures because the laboratory pattern does not support stable independent coordinate refinement. The final statistics are Rwp = 9.403%, Rp = 6.514%, Rexp = 2.243%, goodness-of-fit = 4.192, and reduced chi-squared = 17.576 (8,301 points; 30 refined parameters). Poisson 1/I weights were used because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied.

## Uncertainty

The tabulated standard uncertainties are covariance-derived and understate systematic error. The instrumental profile was not calibrated, several peaks overlap, and CaWO4 shows preferred orientation. Realistic compositional uncertainty is approximately 1--2 wt% for the two major phases. The CaO assignment is supported by several weak reflections, but its approximately 1.4 wt% fraction is semi-quantitative; a systematic uncertainty of roughly 0.5--1 wt% is more realistic than its formal uncertainty.

Small residual features remain near 19.29, 22.02, and 30.72 degrees. Alternative WO3 polymorphs, tungsten suboxides, and calcium tungstate/oxide candidates did not yield a defensible fourth phase. The identity and fraction associated with those residual features therefore cannot be determined reliably from this pattern. The selected *P* 21/*c* WO3 model is parsimonious, but a closely related pseudo-monoclinic/triclinic description cannot be distinguished uniquely at this resolution.
