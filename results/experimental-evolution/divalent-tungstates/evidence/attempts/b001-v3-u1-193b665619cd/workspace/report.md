# Phase analysis and Rietveld refinement

## Result

Three crystalline phases are supported. Weight fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; no amorphous fraction was measured.

| Phase | Structural model | Refined cell (Å, °) | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| CaWO₄, scheelite | *I*4₁/*a* (No. 88) | *a* = 5.24504, *c* = 11.38197 | 78.81(5) wt% |
| WO₃, monoclinic | *P*2₁/*c* (No. 14) | *a* = 7.68663, *b* = 7.53575, *c* = 10.50980, β = 136.0688 | 11.15(3) wt% |
| Ca₃WO₆, ordered double-perovskite type | *P*2₁/*n* (No. 14) | *a* = 5.53608, *b* = 5.78422, *c* = 8.01619, β = 90.0463 | 10.04(5) wt% |

Parenthetical uncertainties are formal GSAS-II one-sigma values in the last shown wt% digits. The final full-range fit (9.903–100.095° 2θ, 8301 points) has Rwp = 2.747%, Rp = 2.117%, Rexp = 2.247%, and GOF = 1.225. A fresh replay of the saved project converged with maximum shift/e.s.d. 0.0029.

## Evidence

CaWO₄ accounts for the dominant scheelite family at approximately 18.43, 28.54, 31.27, 34.01, 39.03, 46.93, 54.11, 57.71, 59.29, and 76.05° 2θ. COD 7219229 is an ordered, full-occupancy 298 K powder-Rietveld structure. Its ambient-pressure *I*4₁/*a* model was preferred over the high-pressure *C*2/*c* polymorph because the latter's diagnostic splitting is absent.

WO₃ is required by the 22.94/23.42/24.23° triplet and associated lines near 33°. The retained full-occupancy *P*2₁/*c* model is COD 2311041, a published neutron structure. Under the same common profile and nuisance schedule, monoclinic candidates gave Rwp ≈ 13.27% before later corrections, an orthorhombic *Pnma* model gave 14.62%, and a triclinic model gave only a marginal 13.24% while its cell collapsed toward pseudo-monoclinic metrics with extra degrees of freedom. Thus WO₃ and its fraction are well supported, but the powder data do not uniquely distinguish *P*2₁/*c* from a very weakly distorted *P*1̅ description.

After fitting CaWO₄ and WO₃, coherent unmatched peaks remained at approximately 18.71, 22.02, 30.71–32.13, 36.59–37.51, 45.13, 50.81, 55.20, and 56.98° 2θ. No exact Ca₃WO₆ entry was available, so several same-stoichiometry cross-chemistry parents were screened. The ordered 293 K *P*2₁/*n* single-crystal structure of high-pressure-grown Ca₃OsO₆ (COD 1562666) was chemically closest and matched this complete family; larger/lower-symmetry Ca₃UO₆-type parents were not preferred. Isovalent octahedral Os⁶⁺→W⁶⁺ substitution gives charge-balanced Ca₃WO₆. Adding it and reconverging background, all scales, displacement, cells, and common profile reduced Rwp from 8.19% to 2.88%. One supported isotropic Ca₃WO₆ microstrain term, 2234(89) µstrain, reduced Rwp further to 2.75%; a size-only branch was unstable and rejected. It improved diagnostic windows at low, middle, and high angle, with calculated FWHM increasing physically from 0.155° at 18.71° to 0.336° at 92.62°. The modeled Ca/W molar ratio is 1.006, consistent with a near-equimolar CaO+WO₃ inventory.

## Refinement and physical checks

The measurement was modeled with the reported Cu Kα₁/Kα₂ wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio. A specimen-displacement correction was selected over zero shift from its angular dependence; the two were not co-refined. The accepted common profile is nonnegative and low-dimensional (U and W only). X was pruned after becoming insignificant and correlated with CaWO₄ size; V, Y, Z, unused strain terms, and other phase-size terms remain neutral. Low-angle asymmetry refinement went negative and was rejected. Surface roughness was finite and stable; CaWO₄ required a reproducible [110] March–Dollase term and an isotropic size of 0.0790(3) µm. No retained term hit a bound, no parameter remained frozen, and the largest final correlation was 0.895 between WO₃ cell-metric terms.

Atomic coordinates were held to the screened source/prototype values while cells were refined. All submitted CIFs are fully occupied and have no sub-1.1 Å contacts. Refined first-shell W–O distances are 1.763 Å in CaWO₄, 1.714–2.158 Å in WO₃, and 1.923–1.940 Å in Ca₃WO₆. These are chemically plausible. The Ca₃WO₆ coordinates remain an analogue-derived model rather than an independently refined atomic structure.

## Remaining uncertainty

Pointwise uncertainties and a calibrated instrumental profile were not supplied, so Poisson counting uncertainties (σ = √counts) were used. The quoted fraction errors are therefore formal and likely smaller than systematic uncertainty from background, roughness, preferred orientation, profile choice, and the Ca₃WO₆ analogue.

An ordered 293 K rocksalt CaO neutron model (COD 1563196) was also trialed. It refined to about 0.15(4) wt%, but did not improve a consistent family of diagnostic reflections and overfit its strongest 200 line; it was excluded as an unreliable trace-level result. No other coherent unmatched peak family remains. Trace CaO below reliable quantification cannot be ruled out, and amorphous material—if present—is outside the reported normalization.
