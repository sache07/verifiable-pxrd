# Phase analysis and refinement report

The pattern is modeled as three crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized over those phases only:

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| Nickel tungstate, NiWO4 | wolframite type, P2/c | 60.301(36)% |
| Tungsten trioxide, WO3 | monoclinic, P21/c | 29.470(27)% |
| Nickel oxide, NiO | rocksalt, Fm-3m | 10.229(38)% |

## Evidence

The inventory is chemically consistent with reaction of the reported WO3 and NiO precursors at 700 °C: NiWO4 is the product, while WO3 and NiO remain as unreacted crystalline precursors. Each phase has multiple coherent peak families. Examples are the split NiWO4 111/11-1 pair at 31.02/30.82°, the WO3 families at 23.12, 23.54, and 24.36°, and the NiO 111, 200, 220, and 400 peaks at 37.35, 43.40, 63.06, and 95.41° 2θ. Refitting after phase omission raises Rwp from 1.744% to 33.674% without NiWO4, 35.660% without WO3, and 6.366% without NiO. Thus even the smallest NiO contribution is not a background or scale surrogate.

The full-occupancy starting records were COD 5910277 for NiWO4, COD 2311041 for neutron-derived WO3, and COD 1010093 for NiO. Several physically plausible WO3 records were compared under a common restrained profile. After the NiWO4 structure was corrected and the common profile rebuilt, the P21/c record gave Rwp 1.857%, compared with 2.184% for a triclinic P-1 record and 3.982% for the alternate P21/n record. P21/c was therefore retained. A fixed substituted ZnWO4-parent model fit NiWO4 poorly, but refining its symmetry-allowed coordinates converged to the same coordinates as the direct NiWO4 start. The final octahedra are physically plausible: Ni-O = 2.04–2.11 Å and W-O = 1.79–2.11 Å in NiWO4; WO3 has six-coordinate W with W-O = 1.72–2.16 Å.

## Refinement and residual checks

The final GSAS-II model uses the supplied Cu Kα1/Kα2 wavelengths and ratio, Poisson counting weights, a smooth six-coefficient Chebyshev background, one zero correction, refined polarization, and a nonnegative common W/X profile with U = V = Y = 0. Phase-specific size/strain terms were admitted only after neutral one-phase challenges and replay tests. Roughness, SH/L refinement, and preferred orientation were rejected or pruned. The model was repeatedly saved, reopened, and refined over 10–100°; the last maximum shift/esd is 0.0026. Final statistics are Rwp = 1.744%, Rp = 1.372%, and goodness of fit = 1.208. Low-, middle-, and high-angle Rwp values are approximately 1.70%, 1.72%, and 1.79%, with no coherent unmodeled peak family.

## Remaining uncertainty

The reported parenthetical uncertainties are formal covariance estimates and are smaller than the likely model/systematic uncertainty. No internal standard or calibrated instrumental profile was supplied, so absolute crystallite-size/strain values are not considered reliable and the crystalline fractions cannot quantify amorphous material. The NiWO4 internal coordinates are refined from this pattern; WO3 and NiO internal coordinates are retained from published structures because this laboratory powder pattern does not determine them independently. P21/c is clearly preferred among the tested WO3 records, but a closely related pseudomonoclinic/triclinic description cannot be excluded absolutely. No additional weak crystalline phase is supported strongly enough to report.
