# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

The crystalline model contains three phases (fractions normalized over these modeled crystalline phases):

- MnWO4, wolframite type, P2/c: **75.83 ± 0.27 wt%**.
- WO3, monoclinic, P2_1/c: **17.48 ± 0.11 wt%**.
- Mn3O4, tetragonal hausmannite, I4_1/amd: **6.68 ± 0.30 wt%**.

The final full-range GSAS-II refinement gives Rwp = 4.647%, Rp = 3.482%, Rexp = 2.490%, and goodness of fit = 1.870. The quoted fraction uncertainties are covariance-derived one-sigma values and do not include model-selection or microabsorption systematics.

## Evidence and model selection

The precursor chemistry (WO3 + Mn3O4, 700 °C) and the observed 15.1°, 18.1°, and split 29.6/30.0° families identify the major product as P2/c MnWO4. No exact MnWO4 record was present in the supplied structure corpus, so same-stoichiometry analogues were screened. A P2/c CuWO4 wolframite parent with the chemically conservative Cu2+→Mn2+ substitution gave the best physically adequate model. Although that source record was measured at high pressure, only its wolframite topology and fractional-coordinate motif were transferred; the cell was independently refined at the measured pattern conditions. The resulting Mn-cell geometry remained octahedral, with Mn–O distances of approximately 2.10–2.22 Å and W–O distances of approximately 1.77–2.12 Å. Alternate FeWO4 coordinate records fit worse or produced poorer geometry.

The coherent residual families at 22.7–23.8°, 32.8–33.7°, and about 41.5° require monoclinic WO3. Two equivalent monoclinic records fitted substantially better than triclinic, orthorhombic, or tetragonal alternatives. The weak tetragonal Mn3O4 phase is supported by partly independent families near 31.84°, 37.47°, 58.12°, and 59.38°, plus the overlapped 35.68° family. Orthorhombic Mn3O4 refined below its uncertainty. MnO went to zero scale; Mn3WO6 and Mn2O3 only borrowed trace scale from overlapped lines and lacked their strongest unique families, so they were excluded.

The shared Cu K-alpha1/alpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio were fixed to the metadata. The accepted baseline uses one zero correction (-0.4240°), a six-term smooth Chebyshev background, nonnegative X-only common width (X = 5.618; U = V = W = Y = Z = 0), and FCJ asymmetry (SH/L = 0.0883). The effective polarization refined to 0.735. Unused size, roughness, H-strain, extinction, and texture terms were neutralized. Isotropic microstrain was retained only for WO3 (4578) and Mn3O4 (5638); anisotropic strain, size broadening, and axial/diagonal texture trials were not replay-stable or did not improve multiple families.

Refined cells are MnWO4 a = 4.79506 Å, b = 5.70226 Å, c = 4.97182 Å, beta = 91.1289°; WO3 a = 7.68259 Å, b = 7.53781 Å, c = 10.50625 Å, beta = 136.0516° (the source P2_1/c setting has a non-reduced beta near 136°); and Mn3O4 a = 5.75679 Å, c = 9.49691 Å.

## Remaining uncertainty

Mn3O4 is weak, broadened, and partly overlapped, so its approximately 6.7 wt% fraction has appreciably larger systematic uncertainty than its formal one-sigma value; the strain model changes its integrated scale more than the covariance error suggests. WO3 and Mn3O4 atomic coordinates were therefore not refined. Fractions are crystalline-only: an amorphous contribution, if present, cannot be quantified without an internal standard. No additional crystalline phase is supported reliably by the remaining residual.
