# PXRD phase analysis and refinement

The crystalline portion is modeled as wolframite ZnWO4, monoclinic WO3, and wurtzite ZnO. Fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases.

The final fit has Rwp=3.2737%, Rp=2.5703%, Rexp=2.7646%, and goodness-of-fit=1.1862 (8,301 points, 29 refined variables). Because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, these values and the parenthesized fraction uncertainties use Poisson counting weights inferred from the counts. The fraction uncertainties are formal, conditional standard uncertainties; absorption, microabsorption, background choice, and structural-model errors likely make the practical uncertainty about 1–3 absolute wt%. No amorphous fraction or absolute crystalline content can be obtained from this scan alone.

## Evidence and model selection

ZnWO4 explains the 18.90 degree peak, the intense 30.44/30.68 degree pair, and the 38.31, 48.69, 50.21, and 53–54 degree families. WO3 is required by the 23.16/23.62/24.43 degree triplet and its 33–34 degree, 41.9 degree, and 49–51 degree families. ZnO is independently identified by its full wurtzite series at 31.79, 34.47, 36.30, 47.59, 56.59, 62.90, 67.96, and 69.09 degrees.

The phase inventory was reconverged after each addition using the same fixed, modest profile: Rwp fell from 8.09% for ZnWO4 alone to 4.34% after adding WO3 and to 3.71% after adding ZnO. Before ZnO was added, all of the diagnostic ZnO reflections remained as coherent positive residuals. Final profile refinement and one supported ZnWO4 width term reduced Rwp to 3.27%. The residual contains small intensity/shape errors at modeled peaks but no convincing unmatched peak family requiring a fourth crystalline phase.

WO3 polymorphs were compared with identical background, profile, scale, displacement, and cell treatment before adding phase broadening or texture. P21/c COD 2311041 gave Rwp=3.683% versus 3.728% for triclinic P-1 COD 2106293 and 5.288% for orthorhombic Pnma COD 2107312. In the diagnostic 32.7–34.8 degree region, local Rwp was 4.99%, 5.94%, and 16.47%, respectively. The P21/c assignment is therefore favored, and its room-temperature neutron source is chemically appropriate, but the small P21/c versus P-1 difference means that a very weak lower-symmetry distortion is not decisively excluded by this laboratory pattern.

Candidate records were screened before fitting. All selected models are fully occupied and undisordered. Their refined-cell geometries remain physical: ZnWO4 has W–O=1.797–2.140 A and Zn–O=2.026–2.225 A; WO3 has W–O=1.715–2.160 A; ZnO has Zn–O=1.975–1.988 A. Nominal alternate ZnWO4 records COD 2101679 and 2101690 were rejected because their occupied compositions are Cu-substituted, not ZnWO4. The old WO3 record COD 1528915 was not used because rounded coordinates produce an implausible O–O contact near 1.58 A; the physically sound neutron record COD 2311041 represents the same room-temperature monoclinic phase.

## Refinement scope and uncertainty

The Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and reported 0.5 intensity ratio were fixed. A single specimen displacement of -47.42(1.14) micrometres was selected over a constant zero offset from the angular dependence; the zero term was fixed at zero, and the two correlated corrections were never refined together. The constrained global width model has V=Y=0 and positive U, W, and X, giving a nonnegative FWHM that rises smoothly from 0.112 degrees at 10 degrees to 0.186 degrees at 100 degrees.

Cells, all phase scales, a 10-term smooth background, and the common profile were refined. Only ZnWO4 required a supported isotropic size term, giving an apparent coherent-domain size of 0.1267(26) micrometres; this is not a direct particle-size measurement. A strain-only alternative fit worse and drove a global width term to its bound. A WO3 size trial was poorly determined (about 65% relative uncertainty) and was excluded. Unused microstrain values are zero, and the fixed WO3/ZnO size values are 10 micrometres, negligible on this profile. A mild WO3 [010] March-Dollase term, 0.9758(29), is supported by the 020-family intensity residual; no texture term was used for ZnWO4 or ZnO.

Atomic coordinates and displacement parameters were retained from the screened source structures. Refining them from this overlapped three-phase laboratory pattern was not reliable, so the submitted CIFs refine the cells but do not claim new atom positions. The final project has no frozen variables, no SVD-truncated variables, maximum shift/esd=0.133, and no parameter correlation above 0.90. The product/residual-precursor fractions imply an overall Zn/W molar ratio of about 1.15, consistent with Zn-rich feed and incomplete formation of ZnWO4 at 600 degrees C.
