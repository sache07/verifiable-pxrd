# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline model contains three phases: CaWO4 84.30 wt%, WO3 12.40 wt%, CaO 3.29 wt%. Fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases; an amorphous fraction cannot be obtained without an internal standard.

## Evidence

Scheelite CaWO4 (I41/a, No. 88) accounts for the dominant 18.43, 28.54/28.81, 31.27, 34.01, 39.03, 46.93, 54.11, and 57.70 degree lines. Monoclinic WO3 (P21/n, No. 14) is required by the characteristic 22.94/23.42/24.23 degree triplet and the 26.44, 33.10/33.43, and 41.31/41.76 degree splittings. Weak CaO (Fm-3m, No. 225) improves the 32.18 and 37.53 degree regions and several overlapping higher-angle lines. Its presence is also chemically self-consistent: the refined residual WO3:CaO mass ratio is approximately the ratio expected for equal molar amounts of the two unreacted precursors.

Alternative low-temperature WO3 cells were tested. The selected monoclinic model fit better than triclinic and orthorhombic candidates while retaining a plausible cell. A mild CaWO4 [001] March-Dollase correction was supported by the 004/200 intensity mismatch and lowered Rwp relative to an untextured model. Refined cells are CaWO4 a=5.24310, c=11.37462 A; WO3 a=7.29010, b=7.53251, c=7.68456 A, beta=90.9367 degrees; and CaO a=4.81989 A.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The GSAS-II Rietveld fit used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson counting weights, a refined zero offset, phase scales and cells, an 18-term Chebyshev background, and empirical profile/size broadening. Atomic coordinates were retained from COD 7219229 (CaWO4), 1528915 (WO3), and 1563196 (CaO); coordinate refinement was not stable enough to justify changes. The final statistics are Rp=7.424%, Rwp=10.588%, Rexp=2.247%, and GOF=4.721.

The formal covariance errors are smaller than the real uncertainty because no pointwise standard uncertainties or calibrated instrument profile were supplied and residual preferred orientation/profile mismatch remains. Plausible profile variants gave approximately CaWO4 82.6-84.3 wt%, WO3 12.4-13.1 wt%, and CaO 3.1-4.2 wt%. Thus sensible rounded estimates are 84 +/- 2 wt%, 12.5 +/- 1 wt%, and 3.5 +/- 1 wt%, respectively. CaO is the least certain phase because it is minor and most of its stronger lines overlap; its structure is credible, but its fraction should not be quoted more precisely than about one absolute wt%.
