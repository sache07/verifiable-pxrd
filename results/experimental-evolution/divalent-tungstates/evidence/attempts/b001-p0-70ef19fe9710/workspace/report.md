# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by three crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases only.

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| PbWO4, scheelite | I 41/a (88) | 60.99 wt% |
| WO3, monoclinic gamma phase | P 21/n (14) | 23.38 wt% |
| Pb3O4, tetragonal minium | P 42/mbc (135) | 15.64 wt% |

The assignments are supported by distinct peak families after refinement of a common +0.43915 degrees 2-theta zero offset. Scheelite PbWO4 accounts for the dominant broad series at 27.81, 33.16, 45.12, 55.63, and 72.35 degrees. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the sharp 23.53/24.02/24.84 degree triplet and associated lines. Pb3O4 is required by coherent residual peaks at 26.80, 31.25, 32.54, 52.54, 58.51, and 66.48 degrees. A triclinic WO3 model fit substantially worse (Rwp about 5.78% versus 4.65%). Trial additions of litharge PbO and raspite PbWO4 refined to zero; massicot PbO and PbO2 refined below 0.2 wt% without unique supporting peaks and were not retained.

The final GSAS-II Rietveld fit used the stated Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 doublet over 10-100 degrees, an empirical background, refined phase scales and cells, and phase-dependent broadening. The PbWO4 oxygen coordinates were refined; atomic coordinates for WO3 and Pb3O4 were retained from COD models 1528915 and 1537983 because their overlapped/broadened intensities do not support reliable coordinate refinement. Isotropic displacement parameters were fixed and should not be interpreted. Final statistics are Rwp = 4.653%, Rp = 3.049%, Rexp = 2.486%, and GOF = 1.876.

Covariance-only fraction uncertainties are about 0.15-0.18 wt%, but they are not realistic absolute errors because pointwise uncertainties and a calibrated instrument profile were not supplied. Profile/polarization choices, strong absorption contrast, possible microabsorption, and specimen displacement give an estimated model sensitivity of roughly 1-2 wt% absolute. Broad noncrystalline background features were fitted empirically, so no amorphous fraction is determined; crystalline phases below roughly 1 wt% cannot be established reliably from this dataset.
