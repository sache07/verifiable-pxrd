# Bounded SH/L challenge preregistration

## Claim under test

Both c04 packages fixed SH/L at its minimal placeholder despite strong
reproducible low-angle asymmetry; v2 rejected it largely because of covariance
with specimen displacement. Starting from each v1 endpoint, plus the structurally
improved v2 c04 endpoint, retain the entire submitted model and release only a
bounded common SH/L term before full reconvergence. The excellent v1 c01 fit is
the principal regression control; the other v1 cases test specificity.

## Predictions and decision rule

- The mechanism is supported if both c04 endpoints obtain a material fresh-
  replay D reduction with an interior, stable SH/L value and physical replay.
- If only c04 improves while c01 remains near its noise-floor fit, v3 should
  prescribe a conditional low-angle asymmetry challenge, not an always-on term.
- Broad improvements would justify testing SH/L routinely before phase-width
  attribution. Stored-Rwp-only gains, bound hits, physical flags, or c01
  regression reject that stronger rule.
- High covariance alone does not reject a term; it must also lack a reproducible
  residual signature or fail fresh replay. No fitted SH/L value may enter the
  package.
