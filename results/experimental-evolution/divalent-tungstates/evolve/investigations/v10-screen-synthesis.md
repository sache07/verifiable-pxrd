## Mechanism and next intervention

The source-health, fixed-balance, constructed-analogue, weak-phase, coexistence, saved-boundary and schema gates all behave as intended. The remaining common failure is temporal: v10 treats the refinement schedule as a path of final decisions, while width, intensity, geometry, texture and source ranking are coupled coordinates. A branch that loses before a phase/source/texture change is never reopened, and a late lower-symmetry source can win by absorbing the residual basis mismatch.

The next package should preserve the chemistry/output clauses but replace the profile schedule with saved-checkpoint coordinate descent. After any accepted inventory, source, geometry, shared-intensity, texture or broadening change, reopen common U/W/X and phase size/strain from neutral values; compare size, strain and their combination from the same state, challenge anisotropy where family widths differ, then re-challenge polarization/roughness/geometry. Repeat until both retained terms and source ranking are stable. Give every serious source/polymorph its own symmetry-appropriate converged loop. Lower symmetry needs diagnostic split-family evidence and an advantage that survives the equal loop, not Rwp alone. Bounds still require restoring saved values before replay.
