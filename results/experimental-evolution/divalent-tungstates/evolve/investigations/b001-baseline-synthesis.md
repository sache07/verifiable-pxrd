## Emerging cross-case hypothesis

P0's scientific search and project-consistency checks are strong, but the first mixture exposes a coupled inventory/reference-quality failure. The package should still stay narrow: make every saved profile parameter explicit; physically screen candidate records before interpreting a failed scale test; and revisit coherent residuals after reconverging each inventory change. Major withheld phases are being adapted successfully; the generic analogue search is justified specifically by the replicated hidden-minor-phase failure in c05.
