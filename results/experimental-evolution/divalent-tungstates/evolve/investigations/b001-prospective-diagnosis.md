# b001 prospective diagnosis (written before P0 results)

This records expected solution paths and failure modes before seeing rollout outcomes. Realized numerical nuisance values are deliberately not treated as knowledge of future samples.

| Case | Sequence expected to reach the noise floor | Prospective divergence to test |
|---|---|---|
| c01 | Retrieve ambient scheelite CaWO4; construct one-phase GSAS-II project; establish background/scale and global calibration/profile; refine cell and physically warranted atom/profile terms; export project-consistent CIF/fraction/profile. | Basic project/export failures, wrong CaWO4 polymorph, or stopping after qualitative phase ID. |
| c02 | Search the Zn--W--O, Zn--O, and W--O inventories before refinement; include ZnWO4, monoclinic WO3, and weak ZnO; refine global terms before separate phase cells, scales, and only then phase-specific broadening; revisit residual peaks before export. | Omit 7% ZnO, use one shared broadening model, overfit background, or leave final.gpx inconsistent with exports. |
| c03 | Identify SrCO3 and WO3 first; recognize the remaining strong pattern as scheelite despite absent SrWO4; obtain CaWO4/SrMoO4/PbWO4 framework, substitute Sr/W, rescale/refine its cell without lowering symmetry, then fit phase-specific texture/strain and fractions. | Accept Sr3W2O9 or another same-chemistry decoy, report an unresolved major phase, or identify SrWO4 verbally without creating/refining an atomic model. |
| c04 | Identify WO3 and inspect residuals; reject Mn3WO6 as a complete explanation; adapt ZnWO4/MgWO4/FeWO4 wolframite by cation substitution and cell refinement; keep searching for weak broadened Mn3O4; refine global then phase-specific profile terms. | Fail to broaden the reference search after exact MnWO4 absence, or stop after fitting the major adapted phase and miss Mn3O4. |
| c05 | Fit CaWO4 and WO3 but treat coherent residual peaks as missing crystalline intensity; use overall Ca:W balance and local Ca-rich chemistry to search A3BO6/double-perovskite relatives; adapt a Ca3XO6 or Ca2BWO6 framework to Ca3WO6, then add/refine the minor phase rather than absorbing it into profile/background. | Stop at the two obvious phases, choose a CaWO4 polymorph to explain residuals, or use a compositionally implausible library decoy instead of adapting a prototype. |
| c06 | Enumerate PbWO4, Pb3O4, PbO, and WO3 from the precursor/product/decomposition inventory; preserve four scales through staged refinement; use separate size/strain where peak-width residuals demand it; verify the weakest PbO against residual peaks and export all four phases consistently. | Omit PbO, collapse Pb3O4/PbO into one oxide, destabilize the fit by freeing all profile terms at once, or save a stale project. |

Cross-case prediction: P0 should solve c01 reliably and often identify the major exact-reference phases. The systematic gaps, if present, should be (i) residual-driven completeness for weak phases, (ii) explicit analogue construction when a plausible exact-composition result is absent, and (iii) a disciplined refinement/export loop that preserves a verified atomic project. Package guidance should only target gaps demonstrated by the three-rollout evidence.
