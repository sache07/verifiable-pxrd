# v1 design review

v1 is intentionally narrow. It packages three mechanisms supported by b001: (1) physical-screen alternate source records before rejecting phases, compare polymorphs before flexible nuisance terms, then reconverge the whole inventory; (2) expose cross-chemistry parents when exact composition lookup fails; and (3) treat saved HAP defaults and analytic width validity as explicit scientific choices.

Evidence: c01 strain diagnostics improved D from 0.356/0.491 to 0.025/0.056 and the independent successful control used 41 ppm strain to reach D 0.049. Screened PbO recovered 5.412/5.121 wt% in c06 r1/r2 and reduced D to 0.652/1.009; independent c06 r3 naturally used the same physical sources/reconvergence logic, retained 4.57 wt% PbO, and raised structural score to 82.09. The tier-1 A3BO6 adaptation recovered 8.35/9.94 wt% in c05 r1/r2 and reduced D from 24.574/17.217 to 11.921/1.952. A blanket profile hard reset failed, so no automatic refinement helper is shipped.

The analogue helper reads only the current solver-visible filtered index and optionally its CIF root. It ranks reduced stoichiometric matches by retained chemistry, optional space group, optional cell mismatch, and source quality. It contains no case identifiers, reference files, answers, coordinates, fractions, or lookup exceptions. On the curator's full index with the hidden source excluded, it ranked the independently established tier-1 c05 parent first; it also exposed plausible scheelite and wolframite parents without chemical-system-specific code.

All 18 b001 P0 solves are complete and inspected. Their overall mean is 50.04; only the clean control exceeds 80 on average. c05 missed the withheld minor phase in all three runs, c06 independently replicated the proposed source/reconvergence fix in its successful third run, and the c01 profile mechanism has both diagnostic and natural replication. v1 is therefore ready for a six-case prospective development screen.
