The pattern indexes as a nearly metrically degenerate primitive hexagonal cell:

- **Space group:** \(P6mm\) (No. 183)
- **Cell:** \(a \approx 5.414\) Å, \(c \approx 4.679\) Å
- **Contents:** As on \(1a\) and \(3c\), giving As\(_4\) per cell
- **Refined positional separation:** \(z(3c)\approx 1/6\)

The missing 110 reflection and the intensity sequence are reproduced by the \(1a+3c\) arrangement. Splitting of reflections such as 301/103 and 220/113 determines the small deviation from the exact metric degeneracy.
