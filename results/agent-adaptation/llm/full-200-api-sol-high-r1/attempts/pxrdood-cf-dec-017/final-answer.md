The pattern indexes as an ordered **ThMn₁₂-type** structure, tetragonal \(I4/mmm\) (No. 139), with \(Z=2\). Refined approximate cell parameters are \(a=8.372\) Å and \(c=4.941\) Å. The strong diagnostic sequence includes 110 at 14.95°, 101 at 20.86°, 211/220 at 29.92/30.17°, 002 at 36.34°, and 321/400 at 43.01/43.20°.

The intensity distribution strongly places Ir on 8j. Co on 8f and Ga on 8i is preferred; their interchange is the only meaningful surviving alternative because Co and Ga have relatively similar Cu-Kα scattering.
