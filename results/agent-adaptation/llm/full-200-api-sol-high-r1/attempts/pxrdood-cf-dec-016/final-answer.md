The peak splitting is systematic rather than a Cu Kα doublet: for example, the features near 42.43/42.59° index as tetragonal 400/004. The pattern is therefore best described as a slightly tetragonally distorted, cation-ordered derivative of the cubic Th₃P₄ structure.

Best indexing:

- Space group: **I-42d**, No. 122
- \(a = 8.511(2)\) Å, \(c = 8.485(3)\) Å
- \(c/a = 0.9969\)
- \(Z=4\)
- Yb occupies the fourfold site, consistent with Yb²⁺.
- Nd³⁺ and Dy³⁺ statistically share the eightfold site 1:1.
- The sulfur coordinate is approximate; \(u\approx0.0825\) in the parent Th₃P₄ description.

Representative assignments include 112/211 at 25.68°, the split 310/301–103 group at 33.26/33.36°, 400/004 at 42.43/42.59°, and split 420- and 422-type groups near 47.8° and 52.7°.
