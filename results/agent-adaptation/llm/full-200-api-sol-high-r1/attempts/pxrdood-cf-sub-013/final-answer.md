The pattern is best indexed as an orthorhombic **TiNiSi-type** phase, space group **Pnma (No. 62)**, with \(Z=4\). The approximate cell is \(a=7.577\), \(b=3.792\), \(c=7.586\) Å. The near relationships \(a\approx c\approx2b\) produce the pronounced pseudo-cubic peak grouping and several unresolved overlaps.
