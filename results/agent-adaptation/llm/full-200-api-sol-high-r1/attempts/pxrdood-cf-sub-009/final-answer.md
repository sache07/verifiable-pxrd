## Primary solution

The pattern indexes as **body-centered tetragonal**, with \(a=4.1439\) Å and \(c=10.2259\) Å. The relative intensities, particularly of 101/103-type reflections, favor **ordered Ir/Ru occupation** of the parent ThCr\(_2\)Si\(_2\) transition-metal site. The resulting space group is noncentrosymmetric **\(I\bar{4}m2\) (No. 119)**. Ir/Ru site interchange represents the indistinguishable inverse-domain model.

A disordered centrosymmetric \(I4/mmm\) model with Ir/Ru sharing 4d gives the same peak positions, but is disfavored by the observed intensities of odd-\(l\) reflections such as 101 and 103. Swapping Ir and Ru between 2c and 2d is an inverse-domain choice and cannot be distinguished from this powder pattern.
