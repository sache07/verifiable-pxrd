An origin-shifted but identical CIF can place Mg at \(1a\), Ni at \(1d\), and H at \(2f\). This is not a distinct structural alternative. The Mg/Ni framework is highly secure; as usual for Cu X-ray data, the hydrogen assignment is intrinsically less sensitive, but the observed weak-reflection intensity relationships support the \(2e\) site over the other two-hydrogen special positions.
