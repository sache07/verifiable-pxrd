The pattern is consistent with an **O3-layered, half-lithiated nickel oxide**, Li\(_{0.5}\)NiO\(_2\) = LiNi\(_2\)O\(_4\), rather than a cubic spinel. It indexes on a slightly distorted triclinic cell in **\(P\bar1\)**. Diagnostic assignments include 001 at 18.22°, 200 at 38.66°, 20−2 at 44.40°, 201 at 48.44°, 202 near 63.49°, and 020 near 65.61°. The splitting around 43–45° rules out the higher-symmetry monoclinic parent as an exact description.
