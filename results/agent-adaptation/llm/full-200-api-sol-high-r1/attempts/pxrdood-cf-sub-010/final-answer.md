The heavy-atom substructure is well constrained: Re occupies a Pnma 4c site near \((0.079,\,1/4,\,0.096)\). Cu X-ray data cannot reliably distinguish O from F because their scattering factors are nearly identical. The defensible primary model therefore has a statistically mixed O/F anion site. This should be regarded as the **average powder structure**. Several split or additional reflections—most visibly around 26.1–26.3° and 37.7–38.8°—suggest weak O/F ordering, a small symmetry-lowering distortion, or a closely related minor domain. The present Cu pattern does not determine a unique ordered O/F alternative; neutron diffraction, anomalous diffraction, or chemical constraints would be needed to choose among such subgroups.
