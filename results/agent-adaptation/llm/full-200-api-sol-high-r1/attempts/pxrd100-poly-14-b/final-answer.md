The pattern indexes as a primitive hexagonal cell with \(a \approx 3.7516\) Å and \(c \approx 12.480\) Å. The \(00l\) condition \(l=2n\), together with the intensity distribution, supports the 2H-delafossite-type space group \(P6_3/mmc\) (No. 194), \(Z=2\).

Pb occupies the octahedral 2a site, Au the linearly coordinated 2c site, and O the 4f site with \(z\approx0.090\). The oxygen coordinate is less precisely determined than the lattice parameters because Pb and Au dominate the Cu-radiation intensities.
