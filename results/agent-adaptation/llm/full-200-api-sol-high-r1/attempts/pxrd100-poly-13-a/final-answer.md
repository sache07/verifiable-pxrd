Refined lattice parameters are approximately \(a=7.3675\) Å and \(c=17.4470\) Å. Nb occupies the unique quarter of the octahedral sublattice, while Cr occupies the remaining three quarters. This model gives sensible approximate coordination distances: Ag–S ≈ 2.4 Å and Cr/Nb–S ≈ 2.47–2.48 Å. A polar \(R3m\) description is formally difficult to exclude from ordinary powder intensities, but no symmetry-breaking feature requires it; the centrosymmetric \(R\bar{3}m\) model is therefore the preferred structure.
