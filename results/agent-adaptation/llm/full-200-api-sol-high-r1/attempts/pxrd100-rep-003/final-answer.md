The pattern indexes as a **CuPt-type ordered zincblende derivative**, trigonal **R3m (No. 160)**. Alternating Al and Ga cation layers along cubic ⟨111⟩ generate the low-angle superstructure reflections, especially 003 at 13.35° and 101 at 25.72°. Refined hexagonal-cell parameters are approximately \(a=4.0577\) Å and \(c=19.8815\) Å.
