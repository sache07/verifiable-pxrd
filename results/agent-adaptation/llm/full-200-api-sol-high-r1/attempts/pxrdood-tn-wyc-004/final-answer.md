The pattern is **not consistent with a single Hf phase**. The most securely identified component is hexagonal **ω-Hf**, based on the characteristic peak pair near 36.9–37.5° and the reflections at 29.73, 53.5, 61.7, and 80.5°. Hexagonal α-Hf and probably a small amount of β-Hf are also present.

Several very intense peaks—notably 18.50, 26.02, 28.31, and 40.31°—remain unexplained by these phases. Many can be indexed using a complex primitive-cubic cell with \(a\approx14.09\) Å, but its Hf atomic arrangement is not uniquely recoverable from this one-dimensional pattern. Thus the following primary CIF should be regarded as the most secure identified phase, not a complete whole-pattern solution.
