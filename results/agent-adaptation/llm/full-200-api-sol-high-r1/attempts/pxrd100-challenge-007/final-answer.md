## Structure solution

The reflections index on a body-centered cubic cell with \(a \approx 9.421\) Å. The extinction pattern, particularly the strong 211, 220, 310, and 321 reflections together with absent 222 and 400 reflections, identifies space group \(I\bar{4}3d\) (No. 220). The composition and multiplicities give the **anti-Th\(_3\)P\(_4\)** structure type:

- Nd/Sm statistically share the 16c rare-earth site in a 1:3 ratio.
- Sb occupies 12a.
- \(Z=4\).
- The positional parameter of the rare-earth site is approximately \(x=0.070(3)\); its precision is limited without a full profile refinement.

Nd/Sm ordering into a lower-symmetry derivative cannot be excluded by ordinary Cu X-ray data because their scattering factors are very similar. However, no superstructure reflections support such ordering, so the statistically mixed \(I\bar{4}3d\) model is the primary and only warranted solution.
