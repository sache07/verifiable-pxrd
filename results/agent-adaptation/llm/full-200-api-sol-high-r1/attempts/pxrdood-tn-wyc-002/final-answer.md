The pattern is best indexed as a long-period, approximately hexagonal Lu polytype:

- \(a \approx 3.604\) Å
- \(c \approx 48.38\) Å
- 18 Lu layers per cell, \(V/Z_{\rm atom}\approx30.2\) Å³
- approximate 18H-type stacking

The strong basal reflections near 28.8°, 50.65°, and 59.1° are consistent with approximately 100, 110, and 200. The non-fundamental intensities require a relaxed 18-layer stacking superstructure rather than ordinary hcp Lu or ideal 9R Sm-type Lu.
