The pattern indexes as an **F-centered cubic** cell with \(a \approx 6.3586\) Å. The reflection sequence is 111, 200, 220, 311, 222, 400, 331, 420, 422, 333/511, and 440. The strong 200-type superstructure reflections require Yb and Ir on one pair of opposite sublattices and Al and Ga on the other. Weak odd reflections support full quaternary ordering in the **LiMgPdSn-type structure**, space group **F-43m (No. 216)**.
