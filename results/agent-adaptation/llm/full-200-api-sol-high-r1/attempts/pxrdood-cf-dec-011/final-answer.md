The pattern is best indexed by a **primitive cubic cell** with \(a \approx 10.105\) Å. The systematic absence of \(h00\) reflections with odd \(h\), together with the composition and a 40-atom cell (\(Z=4\)), points to the ordered chiral space group **\(P2_13\) (No. 198)**. Mg and Be are ordered on separate \(4a\) sites; Y and Ni each occupy one \(4a\) and one \(12b\) orbit.

Several peaks are split or displaced, particularly near 24.5, 35.3–35.5, and 37.5°, suggesting a minor closely related phase, compositional inhomogeneity, or anisotropic strain.
