The pattern is consistent with an **O3-layered LiMO₂ framework** rather than cubic disordered rocksalt. The principal layered reflections are accompanied by strong superstructure peaks. The low-angle peaks index particularly well as:

- 12.72°: 010  
- 18.07°: \(1\bar{1}0\)  
- 18.17°: 003  
- 18.44°: 100  
- 22.23°: 013  
- 25.3–26.2°: \(1\bar{2}0\), 020 and 110  

This gives an approximately fivefold basal-plane supercell with:

\[
a \simeq 5.185\ \text{Å},\quad
b \simeq 7.499\ \text{Å},\quad
c \simeq 14.634\ \text{Å},\quad
\gamma \simeq 111.98^\circ .
\]

The exact Mn/Co/Ir registry is not uniquely recoverable from this laboratory powder pattern alone. The primary CIF below is therefore an idealized, chemically exact ordered model in \(P1\), with one representative surviving cation-ordering arrangement. Oxygen and lithium coordinates were initialized from the O3 parent structure.
