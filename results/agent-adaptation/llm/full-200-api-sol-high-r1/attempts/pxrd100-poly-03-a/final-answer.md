## Primary solution

The pattern indexes as a face-centered cubic cell with \(a=6.8941(3)\) Å. The systematic intensity alternation—strong 220/400/422 and nearly cancelled 200/222/420—is diagnostic of the fluorite structure:

- Space group: \(Fm\overline{3}m\) (No. 225)
- Ce: 4a, \((0,0,0)\)
- Se: 8c, \((\tfrac14,\tfrac14,\tfrac14)\)
- \(Z=4\)
