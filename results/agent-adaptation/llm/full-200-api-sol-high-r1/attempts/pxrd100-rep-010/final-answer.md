The pattern indexes best as a **triclinic, centrosymmetric FePSe phase**, space group \(P\overline{1}\) (No. 2), with \(Z=2\). The cell is pseudo-monoclinic and consistent with an ordered arsenopyrite/ullmannite-related P–Se dimer structure.
