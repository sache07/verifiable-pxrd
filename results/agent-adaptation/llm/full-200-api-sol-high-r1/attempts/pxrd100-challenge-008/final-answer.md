The peak splitting is consistent with a monoclinically distorted, rock-salt B-site-ordered double perovskite. Indexing gives approximately:

- Space group: **P2₁/n** (No. 14)
- \(a=6.113\) Å, \(b=6.151\) Å, \(c=8.703\) Å
- \(\beta=90.67^\circ\)
- \(Z=2\)

The structure is best written as **Ba₂La(Ru₀.₅Sb₀.₅)O₆**. La occupies one octahedral sublattice, while Ru and Sb statistically share the other. Separate long La–O and short (Ru,Sb)–O octahedra were imposed in the oxygen-coordinate model. The cell was determined from the peak positions; oxygen coordinates should be regarded as chemically constrained rather than independently determined with high precision.
