The pattern indexes as a primitive tetragonal cell with \(a \approx 4.120\) Å and \(c \approx 9.660\) Å. The diagnostic reflections include 001 at 9.15°, 002 at 18.36°, 100 at 21.55°, 101 at 23.46°, 003 at 27.68°, 102 at 28.46°, 110 at 30.67°, and 111 at 32.07°.

The intensity relationships favor polar **P4mm (No. 99)** with \(Z=1\): Er and Br occupy the two fourfold axes, Bi occupies the twofold edge site, and oxygen occupies a fourfold mirror site. The heavy-atom arrangement is well constrained; the oxygen coordinates are less precise from Cu powder data alone and should be finalized by Rietveld refinement.
