The pattern is consistent with an **orthorhombic, ordered-rocksalt-derived structure**, indexed with approximately

\[
a=9.762\ \text{Å},\quad b=5.049\ \text{Å},\quad c=5.280\ \text{Å}.
\]

The strong 18.16° line and its harmonic near 36.80° index as 200/400. The peaks near 33.93° and 35.53° correspond to 002 and 020. A **Pnma, Z = 4** model accounts for the remaining peak clusters.

Li positions and the Mn/Co ordering cannot be determined very accurately from Cu X-ray powder data alone: Li scatters weakly, while Mn and Co have similar X-ray scattering factors. Thus, the coordinates below should be treated as a prototype-constrained average model suitable as a starting point for Rietveld refinement, rather than a definitive single-crystal solution.
