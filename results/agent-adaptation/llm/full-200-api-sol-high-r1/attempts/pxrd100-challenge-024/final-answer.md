The pattern is most consistently indexed by an **orthorhombic NbOF₃-type cell**, approximately

\[
a=13.23\ \text{Å},\quad b=5.41\ \text{Å},\quad c=4.24\ \text{Å},
\]

with \(Z=4\). The low-angle reflections are consistent with 200, 210, 201, and an unresolved 011/400 pair. The surviving space-group assignment is **Pna2₁ (No. 33)**; centrosymmetric Pnma is disfavored because the observed 201 reflection violates its \(h0l\) condition.

The O/F assignment cannot be established reliably from Cu X-ray data because their scattering factors are almost identical. The assignment below places O on the shortest terminal Nb–X site and should therefore be regarded as a chemically restrained starting model for Rietveld refinement, rather than a fully unconstrained powder solution.
