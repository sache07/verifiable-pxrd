The pattern is best indexed by an **orthorhombic** cell. The apparently tetragonal sequence at 15.83°, 17.50°, 21.80°, and 27.56° is the \(010/011/012/013\) series. A consistent indexing is:

- \(a \approx 7.5305\) Å
- \(b \approx 5.5946\) Å
- \(c \approx 11.8890\) Å
- \(V \approx 500.9\) Å³
- \(Z=4\), calculated density \(\approx 4.81\) g cm\(^{-3}\)

The absence of \(h0l\) reflections with odd \(h\), together with observed odd \(00l\) and \(0k0\) reflections, favors **Pma2, No. 28**. Representative indexing includes 001 at 7.43°, 002 at 14.89°, 010 at 15.83°, 011 at 17.50°, 012 at 21.80°, 003 at 22.42°, 200 at 23.61°, 201 at 24.78°, 013 at 27.56°, and 202 at 28.02°.

The lattice and space group are considerably more secure than the Li/F coordinates; the latter should be treated as a chemically constrained starting model for Rietveld refinement.
