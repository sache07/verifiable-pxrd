The lattice is well constrained by the peak positions, but the specific O/N ordering is not unique: Cu Kα diffraction has very little contrast between O and N. Permutations of the four N atoms over the twelve anion positions therefore remain surviving alternatives, including a fully occupationally disordered model with each anion position occupied by O \(2/3\) and N \(1/3\). Neutron diffraction or chemically constrained total-scattering data would be required to select the O/N arrangement unambiguously.
