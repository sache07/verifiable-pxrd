The pattern is best described as **polytypic AgBiO₂**, dominated by a hexagonal **2H delafossite-type phase, P6₃/mmc**, with a substantial **3R, R-3m** stacking variant. The diagnostic basal pairs are:

- 2H: 14.38° (002), 28.98° (004)
- 3R: 14.24° (003), 28.72° (006)

The strong 29.37° and 51.88° reflections are consistent with the 2H 100 and 110 reflections. Several additional lines, especially near 24–27°, indicate stacking disorder or a lower-symmetry distorted component, so the CIFs below represent average structures. Oxygen coordinates are chemically restrained estimates because they cannot be uniquely refined from the overlapping powder pattern alone.
