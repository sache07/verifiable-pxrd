- \(a=b=3.714\) Å
- \(c=12.289\) Å
- \(Z=1\)

Representative indexing is 001 at 7.19°, 002 at 14.41°, 003 at 21.68°, 100 at 27.72°, 101 at 28.67°, 102 at 31.37°, 104 at 40.58°, and 110 at 49.02°. The best chemically reasonable solution is a cation-ordered, wurtzite-derived polar structure in **P3m1**.
