# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

Three crystalline phases are required by the pattern. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases:

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction | Refined cell (Å, °) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| monoclinic WO3 | P 21/c | 0.5556 (55.6 wt%) | a=7.70186, b=7.54616, c=10.55374, beta=136.1368 |
| scheelite CaWO4 | I 41/a | 0.0821 (8.2 wt%) | a=5.24826, c=11.39789 |
| calcite CaCO3 | R -3 c | 0.3623 (36.2 wt%) | a=4.99381, c=17.09311 |

The WO3 cell is an equivalent nonstandard monoclinic setting inherited from the reference structure, not a separate polymorph. GSAS-II formal one-sigma fraction uncertainties are 0.25, 0.08, and 0.29 percentage points for WO3, CaWO4, and calcite, respectively. Those values reflect counting statistics only. A more realistic uncertainty from profile/texture-model sensitivity is approximately ±3, ±2, and ±3 absolute percentage points, respectively.

## Evidence and refinement

The precursor chemistry supports WO3, CaCO3, and their reaction product CaWO4. The observed 23.42/23.90/24.67° triplet and the 33.57/34.46° group identify monoclinic WO3. Calcite accounts for the strong 29.71° line and lines at 43.46, 47.78, and 48.78°. Scheelite CaWO4 is required for the remaining family including 18.90, 31.72, 47.52, and 54.43°. After all three phases are included, there is no coherent unmatched peak family suggesting another crystalline phase at appreciable abundance.

Starting structures were COD 2311041 (WO3), 7219229 (CaWO4), and 1547347 (calcite). The Rietveld model used the reported Cu K-alpha doublet and Kalpha2/Kalpha1 ratio, Poisson weights because pointwise uncertainties were absent, a 15-term Chebyshev background, refined phase scales and cells, a common instrumental profile, and a calcite [104] March-Dollase texture correction (refined ratio 0.779). Atomic coordinates were retained from the reference structures; the powder data establish the structure types and lattice metrics but do not justify unconstrained independent coordinate refinement. The refined 2theta zero offset is +0.3503°.

The final fit has Rwp=9.274%, Rp=6.727%, Rexp=2.394%, and goodness-of-fit=3.882 over 10–100° 2theta. A texture-free model gives Rwp=9.392% and fractions of 53.6/7.9/38.5 wt%, which is the main basis for the systematic uncertainty above. Attempts to release independent size and microstrain terms lowered Rwp but produced boundary or nonphysical values and were rejected.

## Remaining uncertainty

The phase identities are reliable; the fractions are moderately reliable. No calibrated instrument profile was supplied, and zero/profile parameters are correlated, so the lattice precision and formal fraction errors are optimistic. The reported fractions exclude amorphous material and any crystalline phase below the practical detection limit (roughly 1–2 wt%, depending strongly on scattering power and peak overlap). Absolute amorphous content cannot be determined without an internal standard.
