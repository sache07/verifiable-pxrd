# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled by three crystalline phases: wolframite-type MgWO4, monoclinic WO3, and rocksalt MgO. The refined crystalline-phase weight fractions are:

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, degrees) | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| MgWO4 | P 2/c | a=4.69243, b=5.67682, c=4.93083, beta=90.7751 | 78.266(177)% |
| WO3 | P 21/n | a=7.30569, b=7.54009, c=7.69353, beta=90.8380 | 13.975(89)% |
| MgO | F m -3 m | a=4.21401 | 7.759(187)% |

Fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. Parenthesized uncertainties are GSAS-II formal one-standard-deviation values and do not include model or sample-preparation systematics.

## Evidence

The dominant reflections at approximately 15.93, 19.24, 24.21, 24.92, 30.81, and 31.11 degrees 2-theta form the characteristic MgWO4 pattern. The resolved WO3 triplet near 23.43, 23.93, and 24.67 degrees, together with its 33.6–34.5 degree cluster, selects the room-temperature monoclinic P21/n model over higher-symmetry WO3 polymorphs. Residual peaks near 37.30, 43.21, and 62.54 degrees are accounted for by MgO; adding MgO lowered Rwp from 10.72% to 10.12%, and refining its cubic cell lowered it to 9.87%. This phase assemblage is also chemically consistent with formation of MgWO4 from the stated MgO and WO3 precursors with both precursors remaining.

Reference structural models were COD 9013902 (MgWO4), COD 1528915 (WO3), and COD 9000499 (MgO). Lattice metrics and phase scales were refined; atomic coordinates, occupancies, and displacement parameters were retained from those reference structures because the laboratory powder data do not independently constrain a full atomic redetermination.

The final GSAS-II Rietveld fit used 10–100 degrees 2-theta, the supplied Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson counting weights, a 14-term Chebyshev background, and refined zero and pseudo-Voigt/Caglioti profile terms. Final statistics are Rwp=9.871%, Rp=7.023%, Rexp=2.864%, goodness-of-fit=3.453, and chi-square=98403.9. The refined zero offset is +0.3017 degrees. An axial-divergence refinement was rejected because it improved Rwp by only 0.02 percentage point while correlating 97% with zero offset.

## Remaining uncertainty

Pointwise uncertainties and a calibrated instrumental profile were not supplied, so Poisson weights were assumed and the formal fraction uncertainties are likely optimistic. The elevated goodness-of-fit indicates residual profile/model error. The WO3 assignment is secure at the monoclinic phase level, but closely related monoclinic settings/distortion models are not uniquely distinguishable from these data alone. MgO is supported by its available reflections but is the least constrained phase because several peaks overlap the tungstate/tungsten-oxide patterns. No amorphous content or unmodeled crystalline material can be quantified from this refinement; the reported fractions therefore apply only to the modeled crystalline fraction.
