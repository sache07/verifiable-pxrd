# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline pattern is modeled by monoclinic WO3 (P 21/c, No. 14) and tetragonal BaO2 (I 4/m m m, No. 139). The refined crystalline-only fractions are **64.70 wt% WO3** and **35.30 wt% BaO2**. These fractions sum to 100% over the modeled crystalline material; amorphous content is not measurable from this dataset alone.

## Evidence

The monoclinic WO3 model accounts for the diagnostic 23.42/23.92/24.66 degree triplet and its higher-angle families, while BaO2 accounts for the paired features near 26.33/27.07 degrees and lines at 33.54, 42.98, 46.64, 47.97, and 55.3-55.9 degrees. A trial BaWO4 phase refined to about 0.13(15) wt% and its characteristic approximately 32 degree line is absent, so it was not retained. The refined cells are WO3: a=7.69648, b=7.53826, c=10.55616 A, beta=136.0983 degrees; BaO2: a=3.81597, c=6.85185 A.

The final GSAS-II Rietveld fit converged with Rp=5.849%, Rwp=7.784%, Rexp=3.644%, and goodness of fit=2.140. Since pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, counting uncertainties were taken as sqrt(counts). A Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 model used the reported wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio; the refined zero offset is 0.2967 degrees.

## Uncertainty and limits

The covariance-matrix standard uncertainties of the fractions are only 0.13 and 0.13 percentage points, but they do not include model systematics. The peaks are broad, the instrument profile was not calibrated, and WO3/BaO2 overlap strongly near 26-34 degrees; a more realistic compositional uncertainty is about 2-3 wt% absolute. Oxygen occupancy in the peroxide was not refined, so the data support the BaO2 structure type but do not reliably distinguish ideal BaO2 from a slightly oxygen-deficient peroxide. Minor crystalline phases below roughly 0.5-1 wt% and any amorphous material cannot be excluded reliably.
