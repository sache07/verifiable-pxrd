# Phase analysis and refinement summary

The pattern is modeled with three crystalline phases:

| Phase | Space group | Refined weight fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| SrWO₄ (scheelite) | I4₁/a | 76.84 wt% |
| Sr₃W₂O₉ | C2/c | 12.26 wt% |
| WO₃ (monoclinic) | P2₁/n | 10.90 wt% |

Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; no amorphous fraction was determined. The final fit gives Rwp = 14.685%, Rp = 10.631%, Rexp = 3.536%, and GOF = 4.162 over 10.05–99.90° 2θ.

## Evidence

Scheelite SrWO₄ is unambiguous and accounts for the dominant series, including the strong reflections near 18.33, 27.99, 33.39, 45.47, 47.75, 51.98, 56.08, and 72.99° 2θ. A C2/c Sr₃W₂O₉ model accounts for a second set including the prominent features near 29.76, 32.62, and 44.0–44.6°; adding it reduced Rwp from about 19.49% to 17.38% in controlled model comparisons. The remaining triplet at approximately 23.46, 23.97, and 24.73°, together with associated higher-angle reflections, is matched by room-temperature monoclinic WO₃. Adding that model reduced Rwp further, and P2₁/n WO₃ fit slightly better than the tested orthorhombic, tetragonal, and alternative monoclinic/triclinic models. SrCO₃ and SrO₂ refined to effectively zero, while SrO was below 1 wt% and did not materially improve the fit, so they were not retained.

The refined cells are a = 5.42380 Å, c = 11.97152 Å for SrWO₄; a = 9.64444 Å, b = 5.53342 Å, c = 13.45000 Å, β = 103.598° for Sr₃W₂O₉; and a = 7.30973 Å, b = 7.54703 Å, c = 7.69762 Å, β = 90.770° for WO₃. Atomic coordinates were retained from the reference structural models; lattice parameters, phase scales, background, zero offset, and profile terms were refined.

## Uncertainty and limitations

The formal one-standard-deviation fraction errors from the least-squares covariance matrix are about 0.20–0.25 wt%, but these understate the true uncertainty. Pointwise uncertainties and a calibrated instrumental profile were unavailable, the peaks show substantial broadening/intensity mismatch, and the minority phases have relatively high phase R factors. A realistic systematic uncertainty is several weight percent, particularly for separating Sr₃W₂O₉ from WO₃. The Sr₃W₂O₉ identification is less secure than SrWO₄ because its refined cell differs from the starting reference and some reflections overlap strongly; it should be regarded as the best available structural match rather than a definitive structure solution. The monoclinic assignment of WO₃ is favored but not uniquely proved over closely related distorted WO₃ polymorphs. Trace phases below roughly 1–2 wt% cannot be excluded reliably.
