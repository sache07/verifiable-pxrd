# Powder diffraction phase analysis

## Result

The modeled crystalline product is single-phase wolframite-type manganese tungstate, **MnWO4**, space group **P 2/c** (No. 13). Its weight fraction is **1.000** after normalization over the modeled crystalline phases. This is a relative crystalline-phase fraction: the pattern does not support an absolute amorphous fraction, and trace crystalline impurities below the practical detection level cannot be excluded.

The final GSAS-II Rietveld fit over 9.9034–100.0949 degrees 2theta gives **Rwp = 9.592%**, **Rp = 7.083%**, **Rexp = 3.461%**, and **GOF = 2.772** using counting-statistical weights because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied. The refined monoclinic cell is a = 4.83009(16) A, b = 5.75623(18) A, c = 4.99535(16) A, beta = 91.2158(21) degrees, V = 138.8548(89) A3.

## Evidence

The characteristic line set at approximately 18.63, 23.85, 24.31, 30.07, 30.53, 31.31, 36.22, 37.45, 40.53, and 41.13 degrees 2theta, together with the higher-angle reflections, is indexed by the MnWO4 wolframite cell. The chemistry is consistent with reaction of the reported Mn3O4 and WO3 precursors at 900 degrees C.

Plausible competing phases were checked against the full pattern. Mn3WO6 predicts intense reflections near 19.97, 20.50, 28.77, 35.27, and 46.65 degrees that are not present as a coherent set. Residual Mn3O4 would give diagnostic strong lines near 33.6, 41.8, and 45.0 degrees; common WO3 polymorphs would give strong 22.6–24.5 and 33–34 degree clusters. Their diagnostic reflections are absent, and adding such phases is not justified by the difference profile.

The starting topology and coordinates came from COD 1520200 (MnWO4, P 2/c). That entry is a 2.4 GPa structure, so the cell was refined freely and expands to an ambient-pressure-like value. Mn and W symmetry-allowed y coordinates were refined; oxygen coordinates were retained from the reference because unconstrained oxygen refinement from this laboratory X-ray pattern produced chemically implausible W–O distances and is therefore not reliable.

## Remaining uncertainty

No calibrated instrument profile or measured pointwise uncertainties were supplied. A reflection-geometry specimen-height shift of -410 micrometres and empirical U/V/W peak-width terms were refined; the U and V terms are highly correlated (about 98%). Consequently, the quoted cell standard uncertainties are formal least-squares values and understate systematic uncertainty. The GOF above 1 and structured intensity residuals likewise indicate profile/coordinate limitations, but there are no unexplained sharp peaks requiring another crystalline phase.

Because only one crystalline phase is warranted, its normalized crystalline fraction is exactly 1.000 and has no meaningful independent phase-fraction standard uncertainty. A small impurity or amorphous component cannot be quantified reliably from these data alone.
