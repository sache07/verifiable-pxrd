# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline product is predominantly triclinic copper tungstate (CuWO4, space group P -1), with a minor monoclinic WO3 precursor residue (space group P 21/n). Quantitative phase analysis normalized over these crystalline phases gives 97.87 wt% CuWO4 and 2.13 wt% WO3. The formal GSAS-II standard uncertainty is 0.06 wt% for each complementary fraction, but a practical uncertainty of roughly 0.5 wt% is more realistic for the trace WO3 because its strongest lines overlap CuWO4. Amorphous material, if present, is not quantified.

## Evidence

The complete low-angle and split-peak sequence of ambient cuproscheelite is reproduced, including the CuWO4 reflections near 15.61, 19.32, 23.24/23.89/24.43, 26.27, 28.98, 30.44, and 31.94/32.44 degrees 2theta. Refinement gives a CuWO4 cell of a = 4.71212, b = 5.84722, c = 4.88528 A, alpha = 91.6550, beta = 92.5346, gamma = 82.7488 degrees, consistent with triclinic ambient CuWO4. The Cu and O coordinates were refined while W and isotropic displacement parameters were held to the starting structural model.

Minor WO3 is supported by residual shoulders/peaks near 23.43 and 24.69 degrees and additional weak lines. Adding the P 21/n WO3 model lowers Rwp materially and gives a stable positive scale; its refined cell is a = 7.30602, b = 7.54705, c = 7.69181 A, beta = 90.8773 degrees. Because WO3 is only about 2 wt%, its atomic coordinates were not refined. A CuO trial refined to zero within uncertainty (about 0.03(12) wt% before clipping) and was therefore excluded.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The GSAS-II fit used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and fixed 0.5 intensity ratio, a 12-term Chebyshev background, and refined zero/profile terms, phase scales, both cells, CuWO4 Cu/O coordinates, and axial-divergence asymmetry. The final fit over 10-100 degrees has Rwp = 6.999%, Rp = 5.109%, Rexp = 2.434%, and goodness of fit = 2.884. The largest final shift/s.u. is below 0.006, with no singular values; zero and axial-asymmetry parameters remain strongly correlated, so cell standard uncertainties are likely optimistic. The phase identities are reliable, but the exact trace-WO3 fraction should not be interpreted more precisely than the practical uncertainty above.
