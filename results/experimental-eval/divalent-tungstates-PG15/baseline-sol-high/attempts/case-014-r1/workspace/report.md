# Phase analysis report

The crystalline pattern is modeled as scheelite **SrWO4** (space group I 41/a) plus residual room-temperature monoclinic **WO3** (space group P 21/n). The refined crystalline-only fractions are **87.83 wt% SrWO4** and **12.17 wt% WO3**. The covariance-matrix counting-statistical standard uncertainty is 0.28 percentage point for either fraction, but the practical uncertainty is larger (roughly a few wt%) because no calibrated instrumental profile, standard reference, or supplied pointwise uncertainties were available.

## Evidence

SrWO4 accounts for the dominant scheelite reflection series, including the intense features near 28.00, 33.39, 45.44, 51.98, 56.08, and 57.34 degrees 2theta. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the independent low-angle group near 23.45, 23.97, and 24.73 degrees and additional features around 34, 42, 50, 55, and 62 degrees. Alternative SrCO3 and Sr3W2O9 models predict strong peaks that are absent and refined unstably or to negligible useful contribution. Orthorhombic WO3 gave a slightly poorer profile match than monoclinic WO3, while the latter is also the expected room-temperature polymorph.

The final fit has Rwp = 18.10%, Rp = 12.65%, Rexp = 3.54%, and goodness-of-fit = 5.12. A Bragg-Brentano sample-shift term (-571.1 micrometres in the GSAS-II convention) was required by the systematic 2theta-dependent peak offset. Because that correction is large and no external calibrant was supplied, refined cell parameters carry additional systematic uncertainty beyond their least-squares errors.

## Remaining uncertainty

Atomic coordinates were held at literature values (COD 1546036 for SrWO4 and COD 1528915 for WO3); the data support phase identity and unit-cell/scale refinement but do not reliably determine subtle coordinate or displacement-parameter changes. Broad residual intensity may include imperfect profile/background modeling or an amorphous contribution. The reported fractions are therefore normalized only over the two modeled crystalline phases and do not quantify amorphous material. Poisson counting uncertainties (sqrt(counts)) were used because the input supplied none.
