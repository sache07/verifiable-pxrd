# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline pattern is assigned to wolframite-type **MnWO4**, monoclinic **P 2/c**. Its calculated reflections account for the diagnostic observed peak sequence at approximately 15.67, 18.63, 23.85, 24.31, 30.07/30.53, 31.31, and 36.22 degrees 2theta and for the higher-angle pattern. This is also the chemically expected reaction product from Mn3O4 and WO3 after heating at 900 °C.

The final GSAS-II refinement used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and intensity ratio, an 18-term Chebyshev background, specimen displacement, lattice parameters, scale, and a constrained positive pseudo-Voigt profile. It converged with Rwp = 7.290%, Rp = 5.221%, RBragg = 4.067%, and GOF = 2.110. The refined MnWO4 cell is a = 4.83027(12) A, b = 5.75645(13) A, c = 4.99561(12) A, beta = 91.2173(16) degrees, V = 138.8729(65) A3. Atomic coordinates and displacement parameters were retained from the starting P 2/c reference because unconstrained coordinate refinement was sensitive to the uncalibrated instrument/intensity response.

No second crystalline phase is determined reliably. Tests of monoclinic/triclinic WO3 and Mn3WO6 drove their scales to zero. A hausmannite Mn3O4 trial gave only a weak nominal 0.7(3) wt% contribution, changed Rwp by just 0.003 percentage point, and lacked a consistent set of unique residual peaks; it was therefore not retained. The reported MnWO4 fraction is consequently 100% after normalization over modeled crystalline phases, not an absolute bulk fraction. Sub-percent precursor residues and amorphous material cannot be excluded, and the absence of a calibrated instrument profile limits the precision of microstructural and atomic-coordinate conclusions.
