# Quantitative phase analysis

The modeled crystalline assemblage is **triclinic WO3 (P -1), 67.96 wt%**, and **wolframite CoWO4 (P 2/c), 32.04 wt%**. Fractions are normalized only over these two modeled crystalline phases; an amorphous fraction cannot be obtained from this pattern without an internal standard.

## Evidence

The WO3 assignment accounts for the characteristic cluster at approximately 23.17, 23.67, and 24.41 degrees 2theta and its corroborating reflections near 26.66, 29.00, 33.35/33.59, 34.22, 41.46/41.96, 50.01, and 55.98 degrees. CoWO4 is required by the strong 30.71-degree reflection and matched peaks near 19.09, 36.44, 38.61, 44.39, 54.15, 61.80, 65.15, and 81.64 degrees. This is chemically consistent with incomplete reaction of the reported CoO and WO3 precursors at 900 degrees C. No separate CoO or Co3O4 phase was justified: their strongest diagnostic reflections would leave unmatched peaks, especially near 42.5 degrees for CoO and 31.3/36.9 degrees for Co3O4.

Closely related WO3 models were explicitly compared. With the same constrained profile treatment, triclinic P -1 gave Rwp = 6.77%, versus 7.15% for the best monoclinic P21/c reference. The triclinic splitting therefore improves the full profile, though the two distortions are sufficiently similar that the exact WO3 symmetry remains the principal structural uncertainty.

## Refinement and uncertainty

GSAS-II refinement used the reported Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson weights derived from the raw counts, a 12-term Chebyshev background, refined phase scales and unit cells, and a constrained common pseudo-Voigt profile. Atomic coordinates, occupancies, and displacement parameters were retained from the reference structures because this multiphase laboratory pattern does not determine them independently. Refined cells are:

- WO3: a = 7.29725 A, b = 7.52572 A, c = 7.68084 A, alpha = 89.7353 degrees, beta = 90.7592 degrees, gamma = 90.1852 degrees.
- CoWO4: a = 4.66290 A, b = 5.67896 A, c = 4.94332 A, beta = 89.9457 degrees.

Final statistics over 10-100 degrees 2theta are Rwp = 6.774%, Rp = 5.098%, Rexp = 3.283%, and GOF = 2.067. The formal GSAS-II one-sigma fraction uncertainty is 0.15 wt% for each phase, but it excludes profile-model, reference-structure, absorption, and preferred-orientation systematics. The phase-model comparison shifts the CoWO4 result by about 1-2 wt%, which is a more realistic practical uncertainty. The phase identities and approximate two-to-one WO3:CoWO4 mass ratio are reliable; finer precision is not.
