# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline pattern is modeled as **triclinic WO3 (P -1)** plus **bixbyite Mn2O3 (Ia-3)**. The GSAS-II Rietveld refinement gives **73.49 wt% WO3** and **26.51 wt% Mn2O3**, normalized over these modeled crystalline phases. The final profile statistics are Rp = 5.09%, Rwp = 7.09%, Rexp = 2.66%, and Rwp/Rexp = 2.66.

## Evidence

The WO3 assignment is fixed by the intense 23.4/23.9/24.7 degree triplet and the resolved 33-35 degree multiplet. A triclinic P -1 reference (COD 2106293) fit better than the tested room-temperature monoclinic P21/n alternative (screening Rwp about 7.1% versus 8.3%). Its refined cell is a = 7.30243 A, b = 7.53449 A, c = 7.69066 A, alpha = 89.9968 deg, beta = 90.6816 deg, gamma = 89.9800 deg.

Mn2O3 is required by the bixbyite reflections, especially the contribution near 33.2 degrees and corroborating peaks near 38.5, 45.1, 55.1, and 65.8 degrees. Adding Ia-3 Mn2O3 lowered Rwp from about 10.7% for WO3 alone to about 7.1%. The refined cubic cell is a = 9.41021 A. Alternative MnWO4 and Mn3WO6 models refined to effectively zero fraction and failed to account for their expected strong diagnostic peaks near 30-31 degrees and 20-21/35 degrees, respectively.

## Uncertainty and limits

The covariance-matrix standard uncertainty on each fraction is about 0.25 percentage points, but a more realistic uncertainty is roughly 1-2 wt% because no calibrated instrumental profile or internal standard was supplied and the result has modest sensitivity to background/profile choices. The fractions apply only to the modeled crystalline material; amorphous content cannot be quantified from these data alone. Oxygen coordinates were retained from the reference structures because Cu X-ray powder data do not constrain them reliably; only the cells and heavy-atom coordinates were refined. Small residual peak-shape mismatch remains around the strongest WO3 peaks, so the exact low-symmetry WO3 distortion is less certain than the phase identity and total WO3 fraction. A minor crystalline phase below roughly 1-3 wt% cannot be excluded.
