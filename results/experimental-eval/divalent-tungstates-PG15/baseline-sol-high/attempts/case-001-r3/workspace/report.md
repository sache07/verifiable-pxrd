# Phase analysis and quantitative refinement

The crystalline pattern is explained by two phases: monoclinic WO3 (space group P 21/n, 46.1 wt%) and orthorhombic BaCO3/witherite (space group Pmcn, 53.9 wt%). Fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases.

## Evidence

The split WO3 peak groups at approximately 23.4, 23.9, 24.6 degrees and 33.6-34.5 degrees, together with its higher-angle lines, identify the room-temperature monoclinic tungsten oxide structure. BaCO3 is required by its distinct lines near 19.8, 28.0, 43.3, 44.5, and 47.1 degrees and by contributions throughout the pattern. This assignment is also chemically consistent with the stated BaCO3 and WO3 precursors and the relatively low 400 degrees C heating temperature.

A trial third phase using scheelite BaWO4 drove its scale to the numerical lower bound. Its diagnostic strong reflection pair expected near 26.5 and 31.9 degrees was not observed with the required relative intensity, so BaWO4 was not retained. Its structure and fraction therefore cannot be determined reliably; any amount present is below what this pattern and model can support.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The final GSAS-II Rietveld refinement used the supplied Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, the 10-100 degree range, Poisson counting weights estimated from the counts, a 12-term Chebyshev background, specimen displacement, phase scales and cells, a global pseudo-Voigt profile, and phase-dependent isotropic microstrain. The refined cells are:

- WO3: a = 7.30492 A, b = 7.53575 A, c = 7.69045 A, beta = 90.7544 degrees.
- BaCO3: a = 5.30637 A, b = 8.91284 A, c = 6.43184 A.

The fit gives Rwp = 8.81%, Rp = 6.72%, Rexp = 3.48%, and goodness of fit = 2.53. Formal least-squares fraction uncertainties are about 0.2 wt%, but profile and weak preferred-orientation choices shift the result by roughly 1-2 wt%. A realistic summary is therefore WO3 = 46 +/- 2 wt% and BaCO3 = 54 +/- 2 wt%. The atomic coordinates were retained from reference structures (COD 1528915 for WO3 and COD 9010928 for BaCO3); the powder data support the phase types and refined cells but do not independently determine all atomic coordinates reliably. No amorphous fraction or absolute crystalline content can be obtained without an internal standard, so the reported fractions apply only to the modeled crystalline material.
