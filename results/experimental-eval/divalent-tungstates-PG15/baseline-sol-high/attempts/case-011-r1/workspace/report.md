# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

Two crystalline phases are required by the pattern.

| Phase | Structural model | Refined cell (angstrom, degrees) | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Monoclinic WO3 | P 21/n, COD 1528915 starting model | a=7.31062, b=7.54043, c=7.69673, beta=90.7591 | 67.22% (formal s.u. 0.22%) |
| beta-MnO2 (pyrolusite) | P 42/m n m, COD 1514110 starting model | a=4.40254, c=2.87528 | 32.78% (formal s.u. 0.22%) |

Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; they do not quantify any amorphous content.

## Evidence

The dominant 23.44/23.93/24.69 degree triplet, together with the 26.93, 33.59-34.48, 47.54, 50.23 and 55-57 degree groups, identifies room-temperature monoclinic WO3. Reflections not accounted for by WO3 at approximately 29.01, 37.68, 42.19, 56.22 and 59.66 degrees match rutile-type beta-MnO2. Local-database screening of the relevant Mn-O, W-O and Mn-W-O structures strongly favored these two models; MnWO4 and alternative MnO2 tunnel/layer polymorphs do not reproduce the observed peak set. No coherent unmatched reflection set supports a third crystalline phase.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The GSAS-II Rietveld model used the reported Cu K-alpha doublet and the 10-100 degree range. A 12-term smooth background, phase scales, zero offset, both unit cells and a common empirical peak profile were refined. Atomic coordinates were retained from the cited starting structures: unconstrained coordinate tests were unstable and are not reliably determined by this multiphase laboratory pattern. Preferred orientation was not included because axis-dependent trials were not uniquely supported.

Fit statistics are Rwp=8.359%, Rp=5.972%, Rexp=2.694% and goodness-of-fit=3.107. Because pointwise uncertainties and a calibrated instrumental profile were absent, Poisson count weights were assumed. The quoted fraction s.u.s are least-squares values only; profile/structure trials indicate a more realistic systematic uncertainty of about +/-1 wt% per phase. Trace crystalline impurities at roughly the few-weight-percent level cannot be excluded.
