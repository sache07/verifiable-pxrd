# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline pattern is explained by two phases:

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction* | Refined cell (Å, °) |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Monoclinic WO₃ | P2₁/n (No. 14) | 71.55(22) wt% | a = 7.30885(29), b = 7.53980(31), c = 7.69705(33), β = 90.6679(34) |
| Co₃O₄ spinel | Fd-3m (No. 227) | 28.45(22) wt% | a = 8.08731(46) |

\*Normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. Parentheses are GSAS-II covariance-based statistical e.s.d.'s and do not include model/systematic error.

## Evidence

The WO₃ assignment is fixed by the characteristic low-angle triplet at 23.465, 23.954, and 24.714° 2θ and the associated lines near 26.94, 34.52, 50.26, and 56.24°. The room-temperature monoclinic model fits materially better than the screened higher-symmetry WO₃ alternatives. The Co₃O₄ assignment is supported independently by the spinel sequence at 31.636, 37.200, 45.143, 59.683, and 65.561°.

CoWO₄ was tested explicitly because it is the expected reaction product from the stated precursors. Its strongest calculated line is near 30.6° before the refined offset, where the observed pattern has no corresponding peak. Adding CoWO₄ to a copy of the final model drove its scale to zero (the unconstrained optimum was negative); the formal 1σ mass-fraction sensitivity was about 0.10 wt%. It is therefore not included as a determined phase. A conservative practical detection limit is about 1 wt%, given profile/model systematics.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The final GSAS-II Rietveld model used the supplied Cu Kα₁/Kα₂ wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, counting-statistical weights (σ = √counts), a 12-term Chebyshev background, and refined phase scales, zero offset, unit cells, and two positive common profile-broadening terms. The refined zero offset is +0.3451°. Atomic coordinates were held to the reference models: Loopstra–Boldrini neutron coordinates for monoclinic WO₃ (transformed to the equivalent near-orthogonal P2₁/n setting) and a room-temperature Co₃O₄ spinel model. Coordinate, occupancy, crystallite-size, and preferred-orientation refinements were not justified by this single uncalibrated powder scan.

The final fit statistics are Rwp = 4.198%, Rp = 2.937%, Rexp = 1.722%, goodness of fit = 2.438, and reduced χ² = 5.942 for 8,301 points and 22 refined variables. Residual structure is concentrated mainly at strong WO₃ reflections rather than forming a separate unmatched peak sequence. The formal fraction errors are consequently optimistic; a more realistic systematic uncertainty is approximately ±2 wt% because no calibrated instrumental profile, internal standard, or independent preferred-orientation/absorption correction was available. Amorphous content, if present, cannot be measured here, so the reported fractions apply only to the modeled crystalline material.
