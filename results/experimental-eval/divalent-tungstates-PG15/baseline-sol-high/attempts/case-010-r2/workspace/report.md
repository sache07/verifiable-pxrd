# Phase analysis and refinement

The crystalline sample is modeled as **73.11 wt% monoclinic WO3** and **26.89 wt% cubic bixbyite Mn2O3**, normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. The formal GSAS-II standard uncertainty is 0.19 wt% for each complementary fraction.

The WO3 assignment is supported by the intense 23.43/23.91/24.68 degree triplet and the groups near 33–35, 50, and 56 degrees 2theta. A WO3-only refinement left a coherent residual sequence near 23.46, 33.26, 38.53, 45.5, 49.6, 55.5, and 66.0 degrees; these are jointly accounted for by bixbyite Mn2O3. Adding Mn2O3 lowered Rwp from approximately 10.2% to 6.7%. MnWO4 was tested but rejected: its characteristic strong reflections near 30.0 and 30.45 degrees are absent, and it does not explain the residual sequence.

The final refinement used Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths of 1.540598/1.544426 A with the reported 0.5 intensity ratio. It refined background, phase scales, both cells, a specimen-height shift, Lorentzian profile terms, and the two independent W positions. Oxygen coordinates in WO3 and all Mn2O3 coordinates were retained from the reference structures because laboratory Cu X-ray data with heavy W do not determine those coordinates reliably. Starting models were COD 2311041 (WO3, P 21/c) and COD 1514230 (Mn2O3, I a -3).

Refined cells are WO3: a = 7.6913 A, b = 7.5350 A, c = 10.5426 A, beta = 136.160 degrees; Mn2O3: a = 9.41094 A. The final profile has Rwp = 6.691%, Rp = 4.855%, Rexp = 2.662%, and goodness-of-fit = 2.513.

The quoted fraction uncertainties are counting-statistical and understate model systematics. No calibrated instrument profile was supplied, the fitted specimen shift is substantial, and overlapping WO3/Mn2O3 reflections correlate scale and line shape. A practical uncertainty of roughly 1–3 wt% is therefore more realistic. Any amorphous material is invisible to this crystalline normalization, and additional crystalline phases below the pattern's detection limit cannot be excluded.
