# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline pattern is explained by two phases: monoclinic WO3 and
tetragonal BaO2. On the modeled-crystalline basis, the refined composition is
65.76 wt% WO3 and 34.24 wt% BaO2. The final GSAS-II refinement gives
Rwp = 8.86%, Rp = 6.56%, Rexp = 3.64%, and reduced chi-squared = 5.91 over
8284 points (10-100 degrees 2-theta).

## Evidence

The characteristic WO3 triplet at 23.42, 23.92, and 24.66 degrees, together
with its reflections near 33.9, 34.5, and 50.2 degrees, selects the monoclinic
model. Monoclinic, triclinic, orthorhombic, and tetragonal WO3 reference
structures were compared; the monoclinic P 21/n model gave the best complete
profile match. Peaks at approximately 27.07, 33.54, 42.98, 46.64, and
55.6 degrees establish residual tetragonal BaO2. These assignments are also
chemically consistent with the BaO2 and WO3 precursors and the low reported
200 degrees C treatment.

The starting structures were COD 1528915 (WO3) and COD 1010113 (BaO2).
GSAS-II refined phase scales, unit cells, zero offset, a restrained common
profile function, and a 12-term Chebyshev background. Atomic coordinates were
kept at the reference values because this laboratory powder pattern does not
support a reliable unconstrained coordinate refinement. The supplied Cu
K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio were fixed.

## Remaining uncertainty

The parenthesized fraction uncertainties are formal covariance estimates only
(about 0.15 percentage point). A more realistic practical uncertainty is about
2 percentage points per phase because no calibrated instrument profile,
internal standard, or preferred-orientation correction was available, and the
peaks are broad. The fractions exclude any amorphous material.

BaWO4 was tested as a third phase. Its scale corresponded to only about
0.25(11) wt% and its diagnostic reflection near 31.9 degrees was not resolved;
it is therefore not reliably detected and was excluded from the submitted
model. A reasonable upper bound is approximately 0.5 wt% on the modeled
crystalline basis. No other strong unmatched crystalline reflections remain.
