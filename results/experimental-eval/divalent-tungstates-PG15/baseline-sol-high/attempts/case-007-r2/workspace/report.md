# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline sample is modeled as 77.31 wt% wolframite-type MgWO4, 13.84 wt% monoclinic gamma-WO3, and 8.84 wt% periclase MgO. Fractions are normalized to the three modeled crystalline phases; they do not include any amorphous material. The final GSAS-II fit over 10.0–99.9 degrees 2theta gives Rwp = 13.23%, Rp = 9.30%, and goodness of fit = 4.63.

## Evidence

The dominant reflections at 15.93, 19.23, 24.21, 30.81/31.11, 36.79, and 41.86 degrees form the P2/c MgWO4 (wolframite) pattern. The peaks at about 23.44, 23.92, and 24.68 degrees, together with the 33.57–34.47 degree multiplet, require room-temperature monoclinic gamma-WO3 (P21/n). Residual lines near 37.31, 43.21, and 62.54 degrees identify cubic MgO (Fm-3m). These assignments are chemically consistent with a 900 degrees C reaction of the stated MgO and WO3 precursors. No additional internally consistent set of unmatched peaks remained after adding these three phases.

Reference structures from COD entries 9013902 (MgWO4), 1528915 (WO3), and 9000499 (MgO) seeded the refinement. Unit cells and phase scale factors were refined; atomic coordinates and displacement parameters were retained from the reference structures because this laboratory powder pattern does not support stable, unique coordinate refinement. The refined cells are:

- MgWO4: a = 4.69310, b = 5.67683, c = 4.93118 angstrom, beta = 90.7713 degrees.
- WO3: a = 7.30587, b = 7.54093, c = 7.69365 angstrom, beta = 90.8486 degrees.
- MgO: a = 4.21416 angstrom.

The common refined 2theta zero correction is +0.3042 degrees. Because no calibrated instrument profile was supplied, the peak-shape terms are empirical.

## Uncertainty

GSAS-II's formal one-standard-deviation fraction uncertainties are 0.25, 0.12, and 0.26 wt% for MgWO4, WO3, and MgO, respectively. These are conditional least-squares errors and are too optimistic: pointwise uncertainties were not supplied (Poisson sqrt(counts) estimates were used), the instrumental profile was uncalibrated, and substantial peak overlap remains. A more realistic interpretation is approximately 77 +/- 3 wt% MgWO4, 14 +/- 2 wt% WO3, and 9 +/- 2 wt% MgO on a crystalline-only basis. The three phase identities are robust, but the exact WO3 distortion/atomic coordinates and fractions at better than these practical bounds cannot be determined reliably from this dataset alone. An amorphous fraction, if present, cannot be quantified without an internal standard.
