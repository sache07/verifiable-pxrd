# Phase analysis and refinement report

The pattern is modeled by two crystalline phases: 98.06 wt% wolframite-type MnWO4 (monoclinic `P 2/c`) and 1.94 wt% monoclinic gamma-WO3 (`P 21/n`). Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; amorphous material and phases below the diffraction detection limit are not included.

## Evidence

The dominant peak sequence (including the groups near 15.7, 18.7, 23.9/24.4, 30.1/30.6, 36.3, 40.6/41.2, and 51–54 degrees 2theta) is indexed by the wolframite MnWO4 structure and is chemically consistent with the MnO + WO3 reaction at 900 °C. A one-phase refinement gave Rwp = 6.923%. Adding monoclinic WO3 removed a coherent set of weak residual intensities and improved Rwp to 6.590%; its scale was about 25 formal standard uncertainties above zero. Alternative orthorhombic and hexagonal WO3 models fit less well.

The final Rietveld fit used the supplied Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and intensity ratio, a 12-term Chebyshev background, refined uncalibrated profile/zero terms, a mild [110] March-Dollase correction for MnWO4 (ratio 1.066), refined MnWO4 lattice and coordinates, and a refined WO3 lattice with literature atomic coordinates held fixed because the phase is weak. Refined cells are:

- MnWO4: a = 4.83635 Å, b = 5.76342 Å, c = 5.00190 Å, beta = 91.2181 degrees.
- WO3: a = 7.3083 Å, b = 7.5490 Å, c = 7.6893 Å, beta = 90.830 degrees.

Fit statistics over 10–100 degrees 2theta are Rwp = 6.590%, Rp = 4.640%, Rexp = 2.760%, and goodness-of-fit = 2.394.

## Remaining uncertainty

The formal phase-fraction standard uncertainty is 0.07 wt%, but it does not include model systematics. No calibrated instrumental profile was supplied, and the refined zero and axial-divergence terms are strongly correlated (about 97%); a realistic uncertainty on the 1.94 wt% WO3 fraction is therefore larger, on the order of several tenths of a weight percent. The WO3 atomic coordinates cannot be determined reliably from its weak contribution and remain at the literature values.

Trial additions of MnO and Mn3WO6 were insignificant, while Mn3O4 refined negative. Mn2O3 gave only a marginal approximately 0.30 wt% contribution (about 3 sigma), changed Rwp by only 0.0036 percentage point, and had no clearly isolated reflections; it was not retained because that trace assignment and fraction are not reliable.
