# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled as a single crystalline phase: triclinic copper(II) tungstate, CuWO4, space group P -1. Its weight fraction is **1.000 (100.0 wt%)**, normalized over the modeled crystalline phases.

## Evidence

The Cu-W-O chemical context and 800 °C treatment support CuWO4 formation. More importantly, the refined triclinic cell indexes the complete observed line set from 9.90–100.09° 2theta, including the strong groups near 19.32°, 28.98–32.43°, 35.95–38.86°, 48.97–57.44°, and 63–68°. Peaks initially suggestive of residual CuO or WO3 are also required reflections of CuWO4; neither precursor provides a unique unmatched line that warrants an additional phase. A multi-phase trial was therefore rejected in favor of the parsimonious one-phase model.

The final Rietveld refinement gives Rwp = 7.354%, Rp = 5.269%, RBragg = 2.293%, and goodness-of-fit = 3.031. The refined cell is a = 4.71094 Å, b = 5.84565 Å, c = 4.88385 Å, alpha = 91.6557°, beta = 92.5342°, gamma = 82.7489°.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 100 wt% value is relative to the modeled crystalline content; an amorphous fraction or a trace phase below the pattern's practical detection limit cannot be quantified without an internal standard. No calibrated instrumental profile was supplied, and the refined Caglioti U/V terms are strongly correlated (97.7%), so crystallite-size or microstrain conclusions are not reliable. Atomic coordinates refined to modest shifts and reproduce the intensities well, but oxygen positions from laboratory X-ray powder data should not be treated as single-crystal-quality determinations. The refined zero offset is about 0.306°, so absolute cell accuracy is also conditional on the wavelength and specimen-displacement assumptions.
