# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

The modeled crystalline material is single-phase wolframite MnWO4, space group P 2/c (No. 13), at a normalized crystalline-phase weight fraction of 1.000 (100 wt%). The refined cell is a = 4.832535, b = 5.759149, c = 4.998243 A, beta = 91.21778 degrees, and V = 139.0761 A3. The full-range Rietveld fit gives Rwp = 4.1197%, Rp = 3.2452%, Rexp = 3.4605%, and goodness of fit = 1.1923 for 8,301 points and 25 refined parameters.

## Evidence and model checks

The Mn3O4 + WO3 starting chemistry and 900 degrees C firing are consistent with MnWO4 formation. A fully occupied, ordered MnWO4 wolframite model accounts coherently for the strong and weak reflection families over the complete 9.90-100.09 degrees 2theta Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 scan. Peak positions, widths, and low-angle asymmetry remain consistent across low-, middle-, and high-angle isolated families. The final saved-project forward replay regenerates the pattern without further optimization and reports no physical-review flags.

COD 1520200 supplied the initial P 2/c topology. Because that record was measured at 2.4 GPa, its source cell was not accepted as an ambient cell: the lattice and atomic coordinates were refined against this pattern. The topology was also challenged with Mn-substituted FeWO4, ZnWO4, and MgWO4 wolframite records under the same nuisance model; the exact MnWO4 topology fit best. The submitted geometry is chemically plausible (density 7.23 g cm-3; nearest W-O 1.788-2.146 A and Mn-O 2.108-2.282 A), with full occupancies and no short contacts. Isotropic Uiso was fixed at 0.005 A2 because separate displacement refinements were not stable enough to justify additional parameters.

The baseline uses one specimen displacement, a smooth eight-term background, a nonnegative common U/W/X profile, axial-divergence asymmetry, and physically bounded roughness/polarization. Isotropic crystallite size (0.303 micrometres) and [001] uniaxial microstrain (equatorial/axial about 1760/1115 ppm) were retained: neutral size and neutral strain controls worsened Rwp to 4.204% and 4.306%, respectively. Symmetry-distinct preferred-orientation trials all returned March-Dollase ratios near one, so texture was pruned.

## Remaining uncertainty

WO3, Mn3O4, Mn2O3, MnO, and Mn3WO6 were re-tested after establishing the common profile. MnO, Mn3WO6, and an alternate Mn3O4 polymorph collapsed to zero scale. Other WO3/Mn-oxide records gave inconsistent 0.2-0.5 wt% estimates with only marginal fit changes; their strongest lines overlap MnWO4, while expected unique families were not coherently present. Those phases therefore cannot be identified or quantified reliably and are not included in the structural model. Crystalline impurities below roughly 0.5 wt% cannot be excluded by this dataset.

The reported 100 wt% is normalized only over modeled crystalline phases. It is not an absolute bulk phase-purity determination, and amorphous material is not quantified.
