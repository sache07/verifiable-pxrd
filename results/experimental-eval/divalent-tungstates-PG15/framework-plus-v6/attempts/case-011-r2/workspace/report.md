# Powder XRD phase analysis

The sample is predominantly an unreacted WO3/MnO2 precursor mixture, consistent with limited reaction at the reported 300 °C, with trace MnWO4 reaction product and a trace Mn2O3-like component. Fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline material; this measurement does not quantify amorphous content.

| Modeled phase | Space group | Weight fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| WO3, triclinic model | P-1 | 72.677 wt% |
| beta-MnO2, rutile | P42/mnm | 26.372 wt% |
| MnWO4, wolframite | P2/c | 0.305 wt% |
| Mn2O3, bixbyite model | Ia-3 | 0.647 wt% |

The major assignments are supported by complete peak families: WO3 accounts for the strong 23.44, 23.93, 24.69, 34.48 and 50.22 degree features, while rutile MnO2 accounts for the 29.01, 37.68, 56.96, 59.66 and 65.12 degree families. The trace MnWO4 scale is supported by partly isolated intensity near 18.76, 30.39/30.72 and 51.86 degrees; the trace Mn2O3 scale is supported most clearly near 33.27 and 55.47/55.61 degrees. Their simultaneous scales remain positive at about 6.6 and 7.5 standard uncertainties, respectively. Mn3WO6 and Mn3O4 refined to zero, and gamma-MnO2 was insignificant under the same common profile.

Several physical WO3 records were compared under one nuisance model. The P-1 record gave the best profile and its refined cell is nearly orthogonal, but a pseudo-monoclinic P21/c interpretation remains credible; therefore the exact WO3 symmetry is not fully resolved. The cubic and slightly orthorhombic Mn2O3 records are indistinguishable at only 0.65 wt%; the higher-symmetry Ia-3 CIF is a representative model, not a reliable symmetry determination. Atomic coordinates for both trace phases could not be refined independently. Their nominal scale uncertainties are about 0.05 and 0.09 wt%, but structural, roughness and overlap systematics are larger, so the trace fractions should be treated as approximate. The two major fractions are more robust, though a roughly 1–2 wt% systematic uncertainty is realistic.

The final refinement used the supplied Cu Kalpha doublet, a smooth eight-term background, one specimen-displacement correction, a nonnegative common U/W/X profile, fixed physically profiled surface roughness, WO3 anisotropic strain, MnO2 isotropic strain and [110] March-Dollase texture. Unsupported size, asymmetry and additional texture terms were removed. All exported structures pass the short-contact/occupancy screen. Independent full-grid replay of `final.gpx` gives Rwp = 5.420%, Rp = 4.064% and GOF = 2.016, with no replay review flags; remaining residuals are mainly intensity mismatches at low and middle angle rather than a systematic peak-position trend.
