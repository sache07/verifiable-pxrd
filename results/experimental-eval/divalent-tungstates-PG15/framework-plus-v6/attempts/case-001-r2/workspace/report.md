# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is best described as mostly unreacted BaCO3 and WO3 with a trace of scheelite-type BaWO4, consistent with incomplete reaction after the reported 400 °C firing. Fractions are normalized over modeled crystalline material; they do not measure any amorphous content.

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| BaCO3, witherite-type | Pmcn (No. 62; Pnma-equivalent setting), COD 9010928 | 55.11 wt% |
| WO3, monoclinic | P21/c (No. 14), COD 2311041 | 44.00 wt% |

## Evidence and model selection

BaCO3 and WO3 account for essentially all strong and medium-intensity families. Under the same background, scale, displacement, and profile treatment, WO3 COD 2311041 gave Rwp 7.63% at the common-profile comparison stage, versus 8.24% for the plausible COD 1528915 polymorph; the other tested WO3 records were poorer or refined to negligible scale. The refined cells are a = 5.30882, b = 8.91580, c = 6.43359 Å for BaCO3 and a = 7.69403, b = 7.53809, c = 10.54114 Å, beta = 136.11289° for the retained WO3 setting.

After reconverging the two-phase baseline, BaWO4 reduced Rwp from about 6.77% to 6.71% with scale alone. Cell and restrained profile/orientation refinement further reduced it to 6.6475%. Its contribution is coherent across partly isolated families near 26.83, 43.37, 49.14, 53.94, 54.87, and 69.41° 2theta. Ba3W2O9 and Ba3WO6 trials produced only approximately 0.26 and 0.13 wt% and materially smaller improvements, so they were not retained.

The sole exact BaWO4 database record (COD 1527204) uses two half-occupied split oxygen sites and creates 1.16 Å alternative O...O contacts. An ordered I41/a prototype was therefore built from the chemically close BaMoO4 structure COD 2300459 by the isovalent Mo6+ to W6+ substitution. Ba, W, and O occupancies are full, the model passes the geometry screen, and the oxygen coordinates were held fixed. Ordered SrWO4, CaWO4, and PbWO4 parents were also inspected; the BaMoO4 parent best preserved the Ba coordination after scaling. The split-site and ordered models fit essentially equally, so the ordered model is preferred on physical grounds rather than Rwp.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The final full-range fit has Rwp = 6.6475%, Rp = 5.1575%, and goodness-of-fit = 1.914 for 8,301 points and 34 refined parameters. It uses the stated Cu Kalpha doublet, an eight-term smooth background, one specimen-displacement correction (-577.3 micrometres), nonnegative common U/W/X profile terms, axial asymmetry, phase scales/cells, restrained size/strain terms, and supported March-Dollase terms. The saved-project atomic replay reproduces Rwp = 6.647473%, all fractions, and the submitted CIFs, with no negative background, peak-width, or displacement-parameter flags.

The BaCO3 and WO3 identifications and their approximate ratio are robust. Their project-only fraction e.s.d.s are about 0.19 wt% each, but do not include reference/profile systematics. BaWO4 is probable rather than definitive: its formal fraction is 0.89(6) wt%, but its broadening was fixed at the physical lower size bound (0.04 micrometres), and scale, size, texture axis, overlap, and prototype coordinates are correlated. Model variants move the estimate by several tenths of a weight percent. Thus its exact fraction and oxygen geometry cannot be determined reliably from this pattern; it should be interpreted only as a trace, roughly 1 wt% crystalline component. Light-atom coordinates in the dominant phases were likewise retained from their references where unconstrained refinement became nonphysical.
