# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

The crystalline pattern is modeled as wolframite MnWO₄ with a small amount of room-temperature monoclinic WO₃. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; amorphous material, if present, is not measured by this normalization.

| Phase | Structural model | Refined cell | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| MnWO₄ | Wolframite, P2/c (No. 13) | a = 4.83203 Å, b = 5.75812 Å, c = 4.99742 Å, β = 91.2189° | 0.97585 (97.59 wt%) |
| WO₃ | Monoclinic, P2₁/c (No. 14) | a = 7.68753 Å, b = 7.54058 Å, c = 10.53068 Å, β = 136.0835° | 0.02415 (2.41 wt%) |

The formal scale-covariance uncertainty of the WO₃ fraction is 0.00058 (0.06 wt%). Profile, background, and polymorph choices dominate that number; a more realistic interpretation is approximately 2.4 ± 0.3 wt% WO₃.

## Evidence and refinement

The MnWO₄ model accounts for the strong families throughout 10–100° 2θ, including the low-angle 15.7°, 18.7°, 23.9–24.4°, and 30.1–31.4° groups and their high-angle continuations. COD 1520200 was the only exact MnWO₄ entry in the frozen inventory, but it is a 2.4 GPa determination. Its wolframite topology was retained while the cell and symmetry-allowed coordinates were refined to ambient-like metrics. The resulting octahedra are chemically plausible: nearest Mn–O distances are 2.096–2.289 Å and W–O distances are 1.795–2.138 Å.

WO₃ is supported by coherent, partly independent families at 23.47/23.92/24.70°, 33.60–34.49°, and 47.59–50.22°, plus higher-angle continuations. The selected parent is the room-temperature neutron model COD 2311041. Its cell and isotropic microstrain were refined, but its atomic coordinates were retained: coordinate refinements at only 2.4 wt% produced implausibly large shifts and were not considered determined by this pattern. Its W–O distances remain plausible at 1.715–2.159 Å.

The common profile has positive U/W/X terms (35.22/3.19/1.24), SH/L = 0.076, one specimen-displacement correction (−617 µm), and a smooth positive 10-term Chebyshev background over the 90° scan. Supported specimen terms are [001] uniaxial microstrain for MnWO₄, isotropic microstrain for WO₃, mild MnWO₄ [110] March–Dollase texture (1.0117), and low-angle roughness. Isotropic size terms and alternative texture axes were replay-unstable or unsupported and were removed. The fresh native GSAS-II replay gives Rwp = 3.9058%, Rp = 2.9841%, Rexp = 2.7588%, and goodness of fit = 1.4157. The replay reports no negative background, peak-width, polarization, or ADP flags.

## Remaining uncertainty

Monoclinic and triclinic WO₃ parents are nearly degenerate at this low fraction. A triclinic P-1 trial changed Rwp by less than 0.01 percentage point, but refinement drove its α and γ angles by roughly 1° toward monoclinic metrics, far beyond a credible source-to-sample perturbation and inconsistent with the room-temperature context. The monoclinic assignment is therefore preferred, while the exact low-symmetry distortion should not be regarded as independently resolved.

MnO, Mn₂O₃, Mn₃O₄, and Mn₃WO₆ were re-challenged after the final common profile was established. MnO and the latter two phases had insignificant or collapsing scales. Mn₂O₃ could fit about 0.2 wt% largely through one weak feature near 33.3°, but it predicted a stronger 55.5° family where no coherent residual exists and improved Rwp by only about 0.003 percentage point. No additional phase or fraction is therefore reported reliably; trace crystalline material below roughly 0.2–0.3 wt% cannot be excluded.
