# Quantitative phase analysis

The crystalline model is **77.11 wt% wolframite NiWO4 (P 2/c)** and **22.89 wt% monoclinic gamma-WO3 (P 21/c)**, normalized over those two modeled crystalline phases. The regenerated full-grid fit gives Rwp = 4.349%, Rp = 3.360%, and goodness-of-fit = 1.253.

NiWO4 is supported by many separated reflections, including the groups near 19.2, 23.9, 30.9, 36.5, and 54.6 degrees 2-theta. The residual peak family near 23.1-24.3, 26.5, 28.8, 33.2-34.1, 49.9, and 55.9 degrees requires a WO3 phase. This agrees with the stated NiO + WO3 precursor chemistry and 900 C treatment. Both submitted CIFs pass the supplied composition, occupancy, symmetry-expansion, duplicate-site, and contact audit.

The WO3 assignment was tested against materially distinct polymorphs. Under the final matched profile model, monoclinic P 21/c gives Rwp = 4.3481%, versus 4.3749% for triclinic P -1 despite the latter using two additional cell variables; orthorhombic and tetragonal candidates were poorer. The P 21/c assignment is therefore preferred, though the closely related WO3 polymorphs make its symmetry less certain than the phase composition itself.

Refinement used a zero offset, minimal global V/W/Z profile terms, polarization, and positive isotropic WO3 microstrain. Specimen displacement was rejected as inferior; joint displacement/zero refinement was 99.99% correlated. Phase size, NiWO4 strain, preferred orientation, axial divergence, added surface roughness, and extra U/X/Y profile terms were unsupported or nonphysical. Removing either phase worsened Rwp to 30.2% (without NiWO4) or 13.9% (without WO3).

A mature-model NiO test refined to only about 0.19(8) wt% and changed Rwp by just 0.0005 percentage point, without independent multi-peak support, so NiO is not reported as an identified phase. This is below reliable identification/quantification for these data. No coherent residual family justified a third atomistic phase.

The quoted fraction uncertainties in `result.json` are covariance-only and understate model/instrument systematics; a few relative percent is a more realistic caution because no instrumental standard, counting uncertainties, or internal standard was supplied. The fractions cannot constrain amorphous material. WO3 coordinates and displacement parameters were kept at the audited source values; only the NiWO4 coordinates were powder-refined, with plausible final Ni-O/W-O contacts.
