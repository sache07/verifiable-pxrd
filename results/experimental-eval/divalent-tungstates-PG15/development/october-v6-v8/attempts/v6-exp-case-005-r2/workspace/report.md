# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled as two crystalline phases: spinel Co₃O₄ (space group *Fd*‑3*m*) and triclinic WO₃ (space group *P*‑1). The refined crystalline-phase-normalized fractions are **25.61 wt% Co₃O₄** and **74.39 wt% WO₃**. Formal covariance-only 1σ uncertainties are 0.20 wt% for each fraction. These do not include model systematics; a more realistic uncertainty is about ±2 wt% absolute because no calibrated instrumental profile or internal standard was supplied.

## Evidence

The Co₃O₄ model accounts for its characteristic peak family near 19.37, 31.64, 37.20, 38.91, 45.14, 59.68 and 65.56° 2θ. WO₃ accounts for the resolved low-angle triplet near 23.46, 23.95 and 24.71° and the many higher-angle reflections. Both exported atomistic models pass the composition, occupancy, symmetry-expansion, duplicate-site and contact audit with plausible Co–O and W–O distances.

Matched screening favored the triclinic WO₃ model (COD 2106293) over a monoclinic *P*2₁/*c* alternative (COD 2311041): with the same minimal W+X profile treatment, Rwp was 3.527% versus 3.617%. A screened *P*2/*c* CoWO₄ model refined to only about 0.13 wt%, had poor phase Rf (~28%), gained credit only from overlaps, and its released cell became unstable; it was therefore rejected. No additional phase had stable positive scale and support from multiple unique peaks.

The final refinement converged without dropped variables (maximum shift/esd 0.034), with Rwp = 3.356%, Rp = 2.395%, Rexp = 1.722% and goodness-of-fit = 1.952 over 10–100° 2θ. Refined cells are *a* = 8.08097 Å for Co₃O₄ and *a* = 7.30319, *b* = 7.53423, *c* = 7.69149 Å, α = 89.8689°, β = 90.6955°, γ = 89.9774° for WO₃. Leave-one-out refinements materially worsened Rwp when the Lorentzian X term, surface-roughness correction, or WO₃ size term was removed.

## Remaining uncertainty

The exact WO₃ symmetry is the main structural uncertainty: *P*‑1 is reproducibly preferred, but the closely related monoclinic model cannot be excluded absolutely at laboratory resolution. Atomic coordinates and displacement parameters were retained from the audited source structures; only cells, phase scales and supported nuisance/profile terms were refined. The fitted WO₃ size (~0.188 µm) should be read as an effective broadening parameter, not a reliable physical crystallite size, because the instrument profile was not calibrated. The reported fractions are conditional on the modeled crystalline material and do not quantify any amorphous content.
