# PXRD phase analysis

The modeled crystalline product contains wolframite NiWO₄ and residual monoclinic WO₃. The refined fractions are normalized over these crystalline phases; no amorphous fraction was measured.

| Phase | Selected model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| NiWO₄ | P2/c wolframite | 0.7615 (76.15 wt%) |
| WO₃ | P2₁/c monoclinic, unconventional equivalent cell setting | 0.2385 (23.85 wt%) |

The final fit has Rwp = 4.214%, Rp = 3.246%, Rexp = 3.479%, and GOF = 1.212 over 10.02–99.98° 2θ. Count-derived Poisson weights were used because the input supplied counts without standard uncertainties.

## Evidence

The dominant peak positions and intensities are explained by a P2/c wolframite NiWO₄ model, consistent with reaction of the stated NiO and WO₃ precursors at 900 °C. A second coherent family of reflections, including the clusters near 23–24.3° and 33–34.2° 2θ, requires WO₃. Removing WO₃ from the mature model raises Rwp from 4.21% to 15.93%, so the second phase is not an overlap-only assignment.

The final cells are NiWO₄ a = 4.59943, b = 5.66171, c = 4.90903 Å, β = 89.9844° (V = 127.834 Å³), and WO₃ a = 7.68746, b = 7.53196, c = 10.52607 Å, β = 136.0895° (V = 422.692 Å³). The latter is an unconventional but symmetry-equivalent P2₁/c setting. The submitted CIFs passed the supplied composition, occupancy, symmetry-expansion, duplicate-site, and contact audit. Refined NiWO₄ contacts remain chemically plausible (shortest W–O about 1.81 Å; Ni–O about 2.05 Å). WO₃ oxygen-coordinate refinement was rejected because it produced >0.93 correlations and an implausible ~1.51 Å W–O contact; its source oxygen topology was retained while the cell and W positions were refined.

Profile tests retained a one-parameter surface-roughness correction and positive WO₃ isotropic microstrain (~1505 µstrain). Their matched removals increase Rwp to 5.61% and 4.34%, respectively. No meaningful March–Dollase texture was found. The final total Gaussian and Lorentzian widths are positive for every regenerated reflection, and the saved profile reproduces exactly in the project audit.

## Remaining uncertainty

P2₁/c and P-1 WO₃ models are close. The P-1 replacement reaches Rwp = 4.206% only with eight additional symmetry-lowering cell/coordinate degrees of freedom; it improves 30–50° but worsens 50–80°, and the BIC penalty does not support the added complexity. P2₁/c is therefore submitted as the smallest interpretable model, but the exact low-symmetry WO₃ variant is not uniquely determined by this powder pattern. The alternative changes the WO₃ fraction from 23.85 to about 24.72 wt%, so a realistic model-dependent uncertainty is roughly ±1 wt% absolute, larger than the formal scale-only standard uncertainty (~0.06 wt%).

A NiO trial refines to only ~0.25 wt% and gains essentially all of its fit improvement in one 43.3° window; its other characteristic reflections do not show matched improvements. It fails the required multi-peak test and is not included. Trace NiO at approximately this level therefore cannot be established reliably.

The angular correction is also not physically unique: zero offset and specimen displacement are strongly correlated when refined together (|r| ≈ 0.999). The smaller zero-only description was retained at −0.0940°. Consequently, the reported cell metrics and fractions should be interpreted within the stated structural/profile-model uncertainty.
