# PXRD phase analysis

The Cu Kα pattern is modeled by three crystalline phases. At 900 °C, formation of zinc tungstate from the stated ZnO and WO3 precursors, with residual amounts of both precursors, is chemically consistent with the refined phase inventory.

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction* |
|---|---|---:|
| Zinc tungstate (wolframite) | ZnWO4, P2/c, COD 1520641 | 74.72(10)% |
| Tungsten(VI) oxide | WO3, P21/c, COD 2311041 | 18.16(8)% |
| Zinc oxide (wurtzite) | ZnO, P63mc, COD 9004178 | 7.12(8)% |

*Fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. Parentheses are formal GSAS-II least-squares ESDs, not total accuracy estimates.

## Evidence and refinement

All submitted atomistic models passed the supplied composition, occupancy, symmetry-expansion, duplicate-site, and contact audit. Their unit cells, phase scales, and the common background/profile model were refined; atomic coordinates and ADPs were retained from the audited source structures. The final refinement converged without dropped variables (Rwp = 5.008%, Rp = 3.812%, Rexp = 3.785%, GOF = 1.323; maximum shift/ESD = 0.005). The saved and independently regenerated profiles agree exactly.

Each phase has multi-peak support across the pattern. Matched leave-one-out refinements increased Rwp to 40.30% without ZnWO4, 14.62% without WO3, and 6.70% without ZnO. The retained nuisance model uses sample displacement, a positive Suortti surface-roughness correction, one global Gaussian V term, and isotropic WO3 microstrain. Removing these terms individually raised Rwp to 11.14%, 12.19%, 9.68%, and 5.26%, respectively. Added March-Dollase texture, Lorentzian terms, W, and axial-divergence terms were rejected as immaterial or strongly correlated; a polarization-only solution was rejected because it refined outside its physical range. Joint zero/displacement refinement was also rejected at 99.98% correlation.

## Remaining uncertainty

WO3 is reliably present, but its exact low-symmetry polymorph is not unique at this pattern's breadth and overlap. The submitted P21/c model is the smallest stable best fit (Rwp 5.008%); a chemically valid P-1 alternative gives Rwp 5.044% and 18.54 wt% WO3 under the same mature nuisance model. The orthorhombic alternative is clearly worse (Rwp 7.158%). Thus, the P21/c assignment is preferred but the monoclinic-versus-triclinic distinction and the last roughly 0.4 wt% of the WO3 fraction are not determined reliably.

No internal or external standard was supplied, so an amorphous fraction cannot be determined. The reported fractions are conditional on the three-phase crystalline model. Formal ESDs also omit structure-choice, absorption/surface, specimen-preparation, and counting-model systematics; the residual serial correlation (Durbin–Watson 1.263) makes them optimistic as absolute uncertainties.
