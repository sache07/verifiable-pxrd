# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline product is predominantly triclinic copper tungstate, with a small amount of residual triclinic tungsten trioxide. Fractions are normalized over the two modeled crystalline phases.

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, °) | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| CuWO4 | P -1 (No. 2) | 4.70878, 5.84343, 4.88205; 91.6556, 92.5336, 82.7508 | 97.5516(56)% |
| WO3 | P -1 (No. 2) | 7.30393, 7.53746, 7.69195; 88.5812, 90.9336, 90.1057 | 2.4484(56)% |

The result is chemically consistent with the CuO + WO3 precursor mixture heated at 800 °C. Triclinic CuWO4 accounts for the dominant peak positions and splittings. The competing monoclinic CuWO4 model fails the complete pattern even after scale and cell refinement (Rwp 47.46%, versus 4.461% for the selected model). The minor P -1 WO3 model explains several distinct residual peaks, notably near 23.48°, 24.66°, and the 33.3–34.8° cluster; replacing it with orthorhombic WO3 worsens Rwp to 4.891%. Mature-model additions of CuO and Cu3WO6 refined to zero scale, while Cu2O was only 0.04 standard deviations from zero, so no third crystalline phase was retained.

The final Rietveld fit has Rwp = 4.461%, Rp = 3.431%, and goodness of fit = 1.839. The GSAS-II project converged without singular variables, regenerated its saved profile exactly, and passed the supplied project audit. Both exported CIFs passed composition, occupancy, symmetry, contact, and duplicate-site checks.

The quoted fraction uncertainties are formal one-sigma covariance estimates. They do not include profile-model, preferred-orientation, absorption/surface, or sampling errors; branch sensitivity indicates that a few tenths of a weight-percent is a more realistic systematic uncertainty for the minor phase. Fractions exclude any amorphous material because no internal standard was supplied. The CuWO4 coordinates were refined and retain plausible Cu–O/W–O geometry. For the 2.45 wt% WO3 phase, the cell, scale, and size were refined but its source atomic coordinates were fixed; its phase identity is supported, while detailed WO3 atomic positions cannot be determined reliably from this pattern alone. Trace phases below the residual/model sensitivity remain possible.
