# PXRD phase analysis

The modeled crystalline fraction contains **72.82 wt% triclinic WO3** (P-1) and **27.18 wt% pyrolusite MnO2** (P42/mnm). Fractions are normalized over these crystalline phases; no amorphous standard was present, so absolute crystalline content cannot be measured.

| Phase | Refined cell (A, deg) | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| WO3, triclinic P-1 | a=7.31073, b=7.54051, c=7.69745; alpha=89.9020, beta=90.7689, gamma=89.9880 | 0.7282(16) |
| MnO2, pyrolusite P42/mnm | a=4.40131, c=2.87531 | 0.2718(16) |

The final whole-pattern fit over 10.00–100.05 degrees 2theta gives Rwp=5.807%, Rp=4.246%, and goodness-of-fit=2.158 using `w=1/I` because the input contained counts but no uncertainties. The calculated profile includes Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 radiation, a refined background and polarization, positive total peak widths, WO3 isotropic microstrain, and a small MnO2 [001] March-Dollase correction. Matched removals of Y, Z, WO3 strain, polarization, and texture raised Rwp to 5.865%, 5.993%, 6.360%, 6.281%, and 5.988%, respectively. The final GPX audit found no numerical or width flags; the last maximum shift/esd was 0.029 with no dropped variables. A final angular branch test retained the baseline: the nominally better zero-plus-displacement branch had 99.93% parameter correlation.

Evidence for WO3 is the resolved low-angle multiplet at approximately 23.44, 23.92, and 24.69 degrees and its many higher-angle lines. Triclinic WO3 remained better than the monoclinic alternative under matched nuisance refinement (mature matched Rwp about 6.11% versus 6.19%). Pyrolusite is independently required by lines near 29.0, 37.7, 57.0, 59.7, 67.5, and 72.5 degrees; omitting it produced a large complete-pattern degradation. Both submitted CIFs pass composition, occupancy, symmetry-expansion, duplicate-site, and contact screening.

No third phase was accepted. MnWO4, Mn3O4, Mn3WO6, hollandite MnO2, reduced WO3, and W3O8 either worsened the fit, collapsed to zero scale, or were unstable. Bixbyite Mn2O3 gave an apparent 1.1 wt% scale, but its small Rwp gain (about 0.03 percentage point) was concentrated in 25–40 degrees and it made strong absent/overpredicted contributions near 23.44 and 66.05 degrees. Its presence and fraction therefore cannot be determined reliably and it is not included.

The quoted fraction standard uncertainties (0.16 percentage point) are covariance-only. Profile/structure branch sensitivity is closer to roughly 2 percentage points, so a realistic summary is WO3 73(2) wt% and MnO2 27(2) wt%. Atomic coordinates and occupancies are the audited COD 2106293 and COD 1514232 models with refined lattice metrics. Coordinate and ADP refinements were unstable or gave no material complete-pattern benefit, so those quantities are not independently determined by this powder pattern.
