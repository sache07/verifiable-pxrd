# PXRD phase analysis

The modeled crystalline assemblage is **76.61(9) wt% NiWO4 (wolframite, P2/c)** and **23.39(9) wt% WO3 (monoclinic, P21/c)**. Parentheses are formal least-squares precision in the last shown digits; realistic fraction uncertainty is larger because the instrument profile was not calibrated, pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, and absorption/model systematics are not included. Fractions are normalized over these crystalline phases only; no amorphous fraction was measured.

## Evidence

NiWO4 accounts for the dominant peak family, including reflections near 19.20, 24.85, 30.87, 36.52, 41.62, 44.70, 52.27, 54.58, and 62.27 degrees 2theta. WO3 is required by a second coherent family near 23.06, 23.54, 24.29, 26.53, 28.82, 33.20, and 34.09 degrees. An atomistic two-phase refinement gives Rwp = 4.2723%, Rp = 3.2903%, Rexp = 3.4788%, and goodness of fit = 1.2307 over all 8,282 points.

The NiWO4 model uses an audited wolframite topology (COD 9013902) with charge-balanced Mg-to-Ni substitution and the NiWO4 cell from COD 5910277; the latter CIF was not imported directly because its atom loop was malformed. Refined NiWO4 coordinates retain plausible octahedral contacts (approximately 1.96-2.03 A for Ni-O and 1.91-2.23 A for W-O), and the exported CIF has no audit flags. The WO3 model is the audited COD 2311041 structure; its cell and phase broadening were refined while its atomic coordinates were retained because coordinate branches were not materially justified.

Matched tests favored monoclinic WO3 over the closely related triclinic P-1 model only slightly (Rwp 4.2723% versus 4.2827%) and with fewer lattice variables. Thus WO3 itself is secure, but its exact low-symmetry polymorph is not determined robustly by this pattern. Orthorhombic WO3 fit substantially worse in screening.

The selected nuisance model uses a 12-term Chebyshev background, the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, refined zero, W and Z profile terms, polarization, and positive isotropic WO3 microstrain. Size, additional U/V/X/Y terms, axial divergence, surface roughness, March-Dollase texture, and WO3 coordinate refinements were rejected because they were negligible, boundary-limited, strongly correlated, or chemically implausible. The angular comparison retained zero correction; specimen displacement was slightly worse, and refining both was 99.99% correlated.

## Remaining uncertainty

A final matched NiO test refined to only 0.22(9) wt% and improved Rwp by 0.0017 percentage point. That signal is overlap-dominated and is not reliable enough to establish residual crystalline NiO, so NiO is excluded from the submitted phase inventory; the data are consistent with a sub-percent trace. No other coherent residual peak family supported an additional atomistic phase.

The reported phase fractions are conditional on the two modeled crystalline phases and should not be interpreted as bulk fractions if amorphous material is present. Counting-statistical weights (1/count) were inferred from the original counts because measurement uncertainties were absent.
