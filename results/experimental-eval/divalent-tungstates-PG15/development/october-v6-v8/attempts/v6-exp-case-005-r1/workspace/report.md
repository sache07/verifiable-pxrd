# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by two crystalline phases: monoclinic WO3 (P 21/c) at **74.9 wt%** and cubic spinel Co3O4 (Fd-3m) at **25.1 wt%**. These fractions are normalized only over the modeled crystalline phases. The formal covariance e.s.d. is about 0.19 wt%-point per phase, but **about ±1 wt%-point** is a more realistic uncertainty because no calibrated instrument profile, measured ADPs, internal standard, or pointwise uncertainties were supplied. Amorphous content therefore cannot be quantified.

## Evidence

WO3 accounts for the characteristic low-angle multiplet near 23.46, 23.95, and 24.71 degrees 2theta and its higher-angle families. Co3O4 is independently required by peaks near 31.64, 37.20, 45.14, 59.68, and 65.55 degrees. In the final nuisance model, removing Co3O4 raises Rwp from 3.2845% to 5.7884%.

Audited atomistic candidates were compared with identical staged nuisance refinements. Monoclinic WO3 (source COD 2311041) gives the better mature full-pattern model than triclinic WO3 (COD 2106293): the best final triclinic trial was approximately 3.301% Rwp, was less stable, and used two additional cell variables. The submitted WO3 CIF retains the valid nonstandard P 21/c setting of the source (beta about 136.154 degrees, V = 423.183 A^3). Co3O4 uses the audited spinel model from COD 9005896.

A chemically valid P2/c wolframite CoWO4 screening model was generated by charge-balanced Fe2+ to Co2+ substitution in COD 9008124. Its refined scale collapsed to 1e-12 and it slightly worsened the fit, so CoWO4 is not supported. The directly indexed Co-O-W library entry was malformed and could not be parsed. No remaining coherent set of residual peaks justified another crystalline phase.

## Refinement and checks

The refinement used the supplied Cu K-alpha doublet, raw-count Poisson weights, a Chebyshev background, phase scales and cells, sample displacement, minimal positive profile terms, polarization, and isotropic microstrain for both phases. The selected displacement is -584.62 micrometres (about +0.297 degrees effective correction at 55 degrees 2theta for the assumed 200 mm radius). A simultaneous zero/displacement branch was rejected because their correlation was 99.97%; the displacement-only model was retained. Size and March-Dollase texture terms were unsupported, negligible, boundary-limited, or unstable.

Atomic coordinates were held at the audited source values. Refining all WO3 coordinates was unstable, gave a 97.5% coordinate correlation, and shortened W-O to 1.575 A; refining the Co3O4 oxygen coordinate gave only a 0.0016 percentage-point Rwp gain. The source WO3 CIF contained zero ADP placeholders, so physically positive fixed screening values were used (W Uiso = 0.003 A^2 and O Uiso = 0.008 A^2); they were not claimed as measurements.

Final statistics over 10-100 degrees 2theta are Rwp = **3.2845%**, Rp = **2.3524%**, Rexp = **1.7221%**, and goodness of fit = **1.9099** (8,284 observations, 23 refined variables). The full exported 9.9034-100.0949 degree grid has Rwp = 3.2825%. The final project converged with no dropped variables and maximum shift/e.s.d. = 0.0082. Both submitted CIFs passed composition, occupancy, symmetry, duplicate-site, and contact audits; `final.gpx` passed profile regeneration and positive-width audits without flags.

Trace crystalline material below the detection/support level of this pattern cannot be excluded, and the reported fractions should not be interpreted as total-sample fractions if an amorphous component is present.
