# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled by seven crystalline components. Weight fractions are normalized over those modeled crystalline phases only; no amorphous fraction was measured.

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| Scheelite PbWO4 | I41/a | 58.84(42)% |
| Fluorite-like Pb-W-O, nominal Pb2WO5 model | Fm-3m | 11.93(49)% |
| Massicot PbO | Pbcm | 8.77(23)% |
| Monoclinic WO3 | P21/c | 8.59(20)% |
| Litharge PbO | P4/nmm | 7.23(23)% |
| Minium Pb3O4 | P42/mbc | 2.37(26)% |
| W3O8 | P21212 | 2.27(24)% |

The refined full-pattern statistics are Rwp = 12.261%, Rp = 8.192%, Rexp = 3.493%, and GOF = 3.510. The final refinement converged with max shift/esd = 0.059, no dropped variables, and a regenerated profile identical to the saved profile.

## Evidence

The dominant scheelite phase is chemically expected from the PbO + WO3 reaction at 500 °C and accounts for many unique reflections across the full range. Both PbO polymorphs and WO3 likewise have multi-peak support. Mature leave-one-out tests increased Rwp to about 14.02% without massicot, 13.79% without litharge, 13.51% without WO3, 13.37% without the fluorite-like component, 12.42% without Pb3O4, and 12.33% without W3O8; removing scheelite caused model failure. The α-PbO2 challenge collapsed to zero scale in the mature model.

Candidate and exported CIFs were screened for composition, occupancy, duplicate sites, symmetry expansion, and contacts. The final GSAS-II project audit reports finite positive widths for every reflection and no numerical/model-integrity flags. Profile terms were retained only after matched deletion tests: global W/X widths, surface-roughness B, phase microstrain for scheelite, massicot, litharge, the fluorite-like phase and Pb3O4, plus a weak scheelite [001] March-Dollase term. Size terms, specimen displacement, correlated zero/shift, and unsupported texture branches were rejected.

## Remaining uncertainty

The cubic component is reliably fluorite-like (a ≈ 5.146 Å), but its chemistry is not determined reliably. Matched charge-balanced Pb2WO5, Pb3WO6 and disordered PbWO4 models give Rwp = 12.246–12.270% and 11.56–12.70 wt%. Its very large effective microstrain and only nine reflection families make the nominal 11.93 wt% Pb2WO5 result conditional; the submitted CIF is a representative topology, not a unique composition.

Monoclinic and triclinic WO3 are also near-tied. P21/c was selected because it converged stably; the triclinic cell remained less stable and did not improve the mature complete-pattern fit. Pb3O4 and W3O8 are low-level assignments whose cells were fixed at literature values after unstable cell-refinement trials. The W3O8 source model is declared P21212, while its rounded coordinates admit an inferred Pbam supergroup. These minor-phase fractions, and all quoted statistical esds, do not include structural-model uncertainty.
