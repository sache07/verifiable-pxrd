# Quantitative crystalline-phase analysis

The selected two-phase Rietveld model is **witherite BaCO3** in Pmcn (space-group No. 62, a setting of Pnma) at **53.27 wt%**, and **monoclinic WO3** in P21/c (No. 14) at **46.73 wt%**. Fractions sum to 100% over the modeled crystalline material. The formal covariance esd is 0.16 wt% for each complementary fraction.

The complete-pattern fit converged with Rwp = 7.0083%, Rp = 5.3339%, and goodness-of-fit = 2.0165, using all 8301 points. Removing either precursor phase gave poor single-phase fits (Rwp about 31.4% for BaCO3 alone and 29.5% for WO3 alone), whereas both accepted structures account for many unique reflections. A BaWO4 candidate was rejected because its strong predicted peaks near 26.45 and 31.85 degrees 2theta are absent. Other oxide and carbonate candidates either predicted unsupported peaks, failed geometry audits, or collapsed to negligible/unstable scale.

For WO3, a fully reconverged triclinic P-1 alternative is close but worse (Rwp 7.0174% versus 7.0083%) and uses three additional lattice parameters; it changes the phase split to 52.57/47.43 wt% (BaCO3/WO3). Thus WO3 is securely present, but the monoclinic-versus-pseudotriclinic symmetry assignment remains the main structural uncertainty. The orthorhombic WO3 alternative is clearly worse (Rwp 11.73%). Atomic coordinates and displacement parameters were retained from audited literature structures because free coordinate/ADP refinements were non-unique or chemically implausible.

The final profile uses a single specimen-displacement correction, global U and X widths, polarization, WO3 isotropic microstrain, and a mild BaCO3 [110] March-Dollase term. Matched removals materially degrade the complete pattern; competing zero/shift, size/strain, extra V/Y/W, roughness, and WO3 texture branches were rejected for correlation, instability, boundary behavior, or region-inconsistent gains. The fitted displacement is substantial, so absolute cell parameters inherit calibration uncertainty.

No internal standard was supplied, and the measurement has no reported pointwise uncertainties. Consequently, the fractions are conditional crystalline-phase fractions: amorphous content cannot be measured, the formal esds understate model/systematic uncertainty, and a practical fraction uncertainty of roughly +/-1 wt% is more realistic than the covariance esd.
