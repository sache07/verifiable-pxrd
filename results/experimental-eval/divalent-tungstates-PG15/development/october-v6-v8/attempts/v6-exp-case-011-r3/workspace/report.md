# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled by two crystalline phases: triclinic WO3 (P-1) at **72.405 +/- 0.177 wt%** and pyrolusite MnO2 (P42/mnm) at **27.595 +/- 0.177 wt%**. Fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases. The fit statistics over 10–100 degrees 2theta are Rwp = 5.850%, Rp = 4.281%, Rexp = 2.694%, and GOF = 2.175.

## Evidence

The chemical context (MnO2 and WO3 heated at 300 degrees C) and the complete peak set both support retained precursor phases rather than a crystalline reaction product. The triclinic WO3 model (COD 2106293) accounts for the dense split-peak families near 23–35 degrees and throughout the high-angle range. Pyrolusite MnO2 (COD 1514110) accounts for independent peaks near 29.0, 37.7, 41.3, 43.1, 57.0, and 59.7 degrees. Removing WO3 or MnO2 raised Rwp to about 38.2% or 16.0%, respectively.

The closest WO3 competitor was carried through the same mature angular, profile, polarization, broadening, and texture model. Monoclinic gamma-WO3 converged at Rwp = 7.60%, versus 5.85% for triclinic WO3; a P21/m alternative remained grossly inconsistent (Rwp about 32.7%). Mature-baseline additions of MnWO4, Mn3O4, and Mn3WO6 had boundary/tiny scales or no material leave-one-out benefit, so none was retained. No coherent set of unexplained peaks required another atomistic phase.

The final model uses a refined zero offset, physically positive Gaussian/Lorentzian width terms, a polarization envelope, isotropic WO3 size and microstrain broadening, and a simple MnO2 [110] March-Dollase term. Matched removal of every retained optional term degraded the complete-pattern fit. Surface roughness, displacement, extra MnO2 broadening, and coordinate refinements were rejected because they were correlated, unstable, boundary-limited, or immaterial. Both submitted CIFs pass the supplied occupancy, symmetry, duplicate-site, and contact audit.

## Remaining uncertainty

The quoted fraction uncertainties are GSAS-II statistical standard uncertainties only. Systematic uncertainty is larger because no calibrated instrumental profile, internal standard, pointwise uncertainties, or amorphous standard was supplied, and MnO2 preferred orientation is significant. Thus the fractions are conditional crystalline-phase fractions; a trace product below the diffraction detection limit or an amorphous component cannot be excluded or quantified. Unit cells are refined, but atomic coordinates are retained from the audited reference structures because coordinate shifts were not robustly supported. The refined WO3 size/microstrain values should be treated as profile nuisance parameters, not calibrated microstructural measurements.
