# PXRD phase analysis

The accepted crystalline model is single-phase wolframite MnWO4, space group P 2/c (No. 13). Its refined cell is a = 4.83066(8) Å, b = 5.75683(8) Å, c = 4.99613(8) Å, beta = 91.2169(10)°, and V = 138.9075(39) Å³. The reported fraction is therefore 1.0000 (100 wt%) after normalization over the modeled crystalline phases.

This assignment is supported by the Mn3O4 + WO3 reaction context and, more importantly, by the positions and intensities of the MnWO4 reflections throughout 9.90–100.09° 2theta. A P 2/c atomistic model (COD 1520200 topology) was audited, refined, exported, and audited again; all sites are fully occupied and the final CIF has no occupancy, duplicate-site, symmetry, or short-contact flags. The complete-pattern fit has Rwp = 5.0997%, Rp = 3.8226%, Rexp = 3.4543%, and GOF = 1.4763.

Mn3O4, Mn3WO6, and structurally valid hexagonal, tetragonal, orthorhombic, monoclinic, and triclinic WO3 models were retested one at a time from the mature baseline. Mn3WO6 and two WO3 branches collapsed to the positive scale floor; Mn3O4 changed Rwp by only 0.00001%. Several WO3 polymorphs acquired very small positive scales, but none passed the absent-prediction test. The best diagnostic branch, triclinic WO3, nominally fitted about 0.46 wt% and lowered Rwp by 0.009%, but its strongest calculated feature at 24.595° was absent and materially worsened that profile region; its gain was driven mainly by a residual near 23.410°. It was therefore rejected rather than reported as a phase. The weak residual scattering may contain an unidentified trace component, but neither its structure nor fraction is reliable.

The final refinement uses the stated Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and ratio, a Chebyshev background, specimen displacement, a minimal W/Z peak-width model, surface-roughness B, and positive isotropic microstrain of 660(52) microstrain. Atomic coordinates and the cell were refined; displacement parameters were fixed at source isotropic-equivalent values because their refinement gave no material benefit. Size was rejected at 99.8% correlation with Z; joint zero/displacement was rejected at 99.993% correlation; added U/V/X/Y widths, axial divergence, polarization, a second roughness term, and preferred orientation were unstable, nonphysical, highly correlated, or non-unique.

The 100 wt% value is conditional: it is normalized only over accepted crystalline phases. Without an internal standard, amorphous content and phases below the data/model detection capability cannot be quantified. Instrument broadening was not supplied independently, so the refined microstrain and specimen displacement should not be interpreted as high-accuracy standalone physical measurements. Count-derived Poisson weights also omit systematic model uncertainty.
