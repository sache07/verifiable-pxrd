# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline product is wolframite MnWO4 with a small monoclinic WO3 remainder. The GSAS-II phase fractions, normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases, are **97.33 wt% MnWO4** and **2.67 wt% WO3**. The final full-range fit has Rwp = 6.800%, Rp = 4.732%, and goodness of fit = 2.470.

MnWO4 is supported by the complete set of strong reflections and by the MnO + WO3 reaction context. The refined P 2/c cell is a = 4.83056, b = 5.75631, c = 4.99573 A, beta = 91.2174 degrees. Because the available starting model (COD 1520200) was measured at 2.4 GPa, its cell and both independent oxygen positions were refined; the resulting Mn-O and W-O contacts pass the supplied CIF geometry audit. A small {110} March-Dollase correction was retained after matched leave-one-out testing.

Coherent residual peaks near 23.45, 33.6, and 34.5 degrees require WO3. A literature monoclinic model (COD 2311041), transformed to the equivalent P 21/n setting, explains these and other weaker/overlapped lines without strong absent predictions. A triclinic WO3 replacement was about 0.074 Rwp percentage points worse under the same scale and size treatment; the other screened WO3 polymorphs were poorer, unstable, or malformed. The WO3 cell and atomic coordinates were therefore held at literature values, while an isotropic coherent-domain size of about 0.092(13) micrometres was supported. MnO, Mn3O4, Mn2O3, and Mn3WO6 were retested from the mature two-phase model and rejected because their gains were overlap-dominated or lacked stable multi-peak support.

The quoted covariance uncertainty on each normalized fraction is about 0.12 wt%, but it does not include model selection, background, absorption, or microstructure systematics. In particular, the WO3 fraction moves by several tenths of a weight-percent when its weak-line broadening is changed; a realistic interpretation is roughly 2-3 wt% WO3 rather than an exact 2.67 wt%. Its detailed oxygen coordinates cannot be determined reliably from this minor, strongly overlapped contribution. No internal standard was supplied, so amorphous content cannot be quantified and is excluded from the normalization. Pointwise uncertainties were not supplied; count-statistical GSAS-II weights were used.

The submitted CIFs, fractions, and `fit.csv` were exported from `final.gpx` after full-grid profile regeneration. Both CIF audits, the project numerical/width audit, and the submission readback audit were run on the final files.
