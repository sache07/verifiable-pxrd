# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

The modeled crystalline product is single-phase manganese tungstate, MnWO₄, in the wolframite structure (space group P 2/c, No. 13). Its weight fraction is **1.0000 (100 wt%) after normalization over the modeled crystalline phases**.

The refined cell is a = 4.83258 Å, b = 5.75926 Å, c = 4.99832 Å, β = 88.7810°, and V = 139.082 Å³. The acute-β cell is an equivalent setting of the more commonly reported obtuse-β wolframite cell. The final whole-pattern statistics are Rwp = 4.1116%, Rp = 3.2006%, Rexp = 3.4605%, and GOF = 1.1910.

## Evidence and model selection

The precursor chemistry (Mn₃O₄ + WO₃, heated at 900 °C), the indexed peak positions across 9.90–100.09° 2θ, systematic absences, and the complete intensity pattern all support wolframite MnWO₄. An ambient-pressure wolframite model was preferred to a 2.4 GPa model in matched minimal refinements (Rwp 12.533% versus 12.639% before the later profile and coordinate stages). The selected model accounts for 146 reflections over the measured range, and the exported structure passes the supplied composition, occupancy, symmetry, duplicate-site, and contact audit.

Source-supported Mn₃O₄, Mn₃WO₆, Mn₂O₃, and WO₃ models were audited and retested one at a time from the mature baseline. Mn₃O₄ and Mn₃WO₆ refined to the nonnegative scale floor. WO₃ gave only a 0.0024 percentage-point Rwp change and predicted strong absent intensity. Mn₂O₃ produced a nominal 0.43(9) wt% overlap fit and a 0.0053 percentage-point Rwp change, but its second- and third-strongest unique reflections were absent; most credit came from one residual near 33.24° and overlaps. It therefore did not satisfy the required multi-peak support and was not included.

The final nuisance model uses the stated Cu Kα1/Kα2 wavelengths and ratio, a smooth 20-term background, one surface-roughness parameter, axial asymmetry, positive U/W/X profile widths, and a refined specimen displacement. A constant-zero model was inferior; refining zero and displacement together was rejected at 99.99% correlation. Texture was negligible. Refinable crystallite size was exactly degenerate with X, and microstrain refined negative after U was released, so neither microstructural quantity was retained. Coordinate refinement gave plausible Mn–O and W–O contacts. ADPs were held at positive source values because free ADP branches became negative.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 100 wt% value is conditional on the modeled crystalline material. With no internal standard, amorphous content and absolute crystalline yield cannot be determined. Trace phases at the few-tenths-wt% level cannot be excluded reliably, but no additional phase passed the full-pattern and unique-peak criteria. Oxygen coordinates are less precise than the heavy-atom framework, and the fixed ADPs should not be interpreted as independently measured thermal parameters.
