# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

The pattern is an almost unreacted/very weakly reacted mixture of monoclinic WO3 and orthorhombic BaCO3. A scheelite BaWO4 contribution refines at the detection level and is reported as tentative.

| Phase | Space group | Crystalline weight fraction | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| WO3 | P 21/c (No. 14) | 0.47991 (47.99 wt%) | Secure major phase |
| BaCO3, witherite | Pmcn, equivalent setting of Pnma (No. 62) | 0.51806 (51.81 wt%) | Secure major phase |
| BaWO4, scheelite | I 41/a (No. 88) | 0.00203 (0.20 wt%) | Tentative trace; fraction and structure not independently determinable |

Fractions sum to one over the modeled crystalline phases. They do not measure amorphous material. Least-squares standard uncertainties are 0.15 wt% for each major phase and 0.029 wt% for BaWO4, but these omit model systematics. Sensitivity to displacement parameters and texture supports a more realistic uncertainty of about 2 wt% absolute on the two major fractions. For BaWO4, only “at most a few tenths of a weight percent” is defensible.

## Evidence and model

The WO3 assignment accounts for its characteristic split low-angle group at calculated 23.438, 23.921 and 24.675 degrees 2theta and the strong 34.473-degree line. BaCO3 independently accounts for the 24.229-degree maximum and lines at 34.401, 42.311, 45.169 and 47.078 degrees. These fingerprints and their full-pattern intensity distributions exclude a single-phase tungstate model.

The major structures started from COD 2311041 (WO3, Loopstra and Boldrini, 1966) and COD 9010928 (room-temperature synthetic witherite). Cells and heavy-atom coordinates were refined; weakly constrained O and C coordinates were retained from the references. The refined cells are WO3: a = 7.70046, b = 7.54428, c = 10.55043 A, beta = 136.11445 degrees; and BaCO3: a = 5.31303, b = 8.92311, c = 6.43885 A. Bond-distance and symmetry checks passed. Thus the phase symmetries, cells and heavy-atom frameworks are supported, while the light-atom coordinates are not independent structure determinations from this powder pattern.

For BaWO4, weak residual reductions occur at several expected scheelite lines, especially 26.801 (112), 43.294 (204), 49.001 (116) and 53.895 degrees. Removing it and re-refining changes chi-square from 24779.46 to 24925.94 and Rwp from 6.0138% to 6.0316%. BaO2 and Ba3W2O9 alternatives gave smaller and/or chemically less credible improvements. Nevertheless, the effect is small enough to be sensitive to profile imperfections. The trace cell and Ba/W sites came from COD 1527204; its unphysical split-oxygen average was replaced by the full-occupancy scheelite oxygen framework of BaMoO4 COD 2300459 with Mo replaced by W. No BaWO4 coordinates or cell parameters were refined.

The converged dual-Cu-Kalpha GSAS-II refinement covers all 8301 observations from 9.90343 to 100.09489 degrees: Rwp = 6.0138%, Rp = 4.5341%, Rexp = 3.4711%, goodness of fit = 1.7326. The model includes Bragg-Brentano displacement, surface roughness, phase size/strain and March-Dollase texture. The goodness of fit above one and correlated residuals indicate remaining systematic mismatch; additional unidentified crystalline material, if present, is below reliable identification in this dataset.
