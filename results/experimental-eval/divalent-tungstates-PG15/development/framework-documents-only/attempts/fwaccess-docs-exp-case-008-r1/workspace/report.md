# Powder XRD phase analysis

The crystalline sample is predominantly wolframite-type MnWO4, with a small amount of residual monoclinic WO3. The refined crystalline-only fractions are 97.42 wt% MnWO4 and 2.58 wt% WO3. These values do not quantify any amorphous material.

MnWO4 is identified by the complete strong-line sequence at approximately 15.72, 18.68, 23.89, 24.36, 30.12, 30.58, 31.36, and 36.27 degrees 2theta. A P 2/c wolframite model accounts for all major reflections. The available COD 1520200 parent was measured at pressure, so its cell was refined to the ambient specimen; the resulting cell is approximately a = 4.8311, b = 5.7572, c = 4.9966 A, beta = 91.2195 degrees. Symmetry-allowed coordinates were refined, while a shared positive Uiso was imposed to avoid nonphysical site-specific ADPs.

The weak residual triplet near 23.45, 23.91, and 24.68 degrees, together with weaker lines in the 33–35 degree region, supports gamma-WO3 in P 21/n. Monoclinic WO3 models (COD 1528915 and its equivalent alternative setting) fit better than triclinic WO3; reduced tungsten oxides were less consistent with the full residual pattern. A tetragonal Mn3O4 control refined to effectively zero abundance and was rejected. Because WO3 is only about 2.6 wt% and overlaps MnWO4 strongly, its oxygen coordinates and detailed microstructure cannot be determined reliably from this scan; the reported CIF therefore retains the reference coordinates with only the cell refined.

The final full-grid GSAS-II refinement gives Rwp = 4.019%, Rp = 3.044%, Rexp = 2.759%, and goodness of fit = 1.460. The model includes Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 radiation, calibrated profile terms, specimen displacement, surface roughness, [001] uniaxial MnWO4 microstrain, and mild [110] preferred orientation. Formal fraction uncertainties are about 0.06 wt%, but model choice and severe overlap dominate; a practical uncertainty of roughly 0.5 wt% for the WO3 fraction is more realistic. Cell scale, zero offset, and specimen displacement remain strongly correlated, so their individual formal uncertainties should also be treated cautiously.
