# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

The modeled crystalline material is single-phase wolframite **MnWO4**, space group **P 2/c** (No. 13). Its weight fraction is **1.0000 (100 wt%) on the normalized modeled-crystalline basis**. This is not an absolute bulk fraction: the pattern has no internal standard, so an amorphous fraction cannot be measured.

The refined cell is (a=4.832175\) Å, (b=5.758691\) Å, (c=4.997873\) Å, β = 91.21858°, and (V=139.0444\) Å³. The final GSAS-II fit has (R_{wp}=3.7526\%\), (R_p=2.8996\%\), (R_{exp}=3.4635\%\), goodness of fit 1.0878, and χ² = 9724.61 for 8,284 observations and 66 refined parameters.

## Evidence

The MnWO4 wolframite model indexes the full peak set, including the characteristic observed groups near 18.63°, 23.85/24.31°, 30.07/30.53°, 36.22°, 41.13°, and 51.45° 2θ. This assignment is also chemically consistent with firing Mn3O4 and WO3 at 900 °C. A COD MnWO4 model (entry 1520200) supplied the structural topology; the cell, atomic coordinates, scale, profile, [001] uniaxial microstrain, weak [100] March-Dollase texture, surface roughness, specimen displacement, and Cu Kα doublet profile were refined. The final crystallographic residuals are (R_F=1.036\%\) and (R_{F^2}=2.133\%\). Refined Mn–O distances are approximately 2.10–2.29 Å and W–O distances 1.79–2.14 Å, consistent with the wolframite coordination environments.

## Uncertainty

Trial additions of unreacted Mn3O4 and WO3 and plausible MnO, Mn2O3, and Mn3WO6 byproducts gave only about 0–0.24 wt% fitted scales. Their largest calculated trace peaks were below the pointwise counting noise, diagnostic reflections were not independently resolved, and results were sensitive to systematic profile/background error. These are not reliable phase detections, so their structures and fractions cannot be determined reliably and no extra CIFs are submitted. A conservative, phase-dependent minor-crystalline-phase sensitivity is roughly 0.5 wt%.

The common isotropic displacement parameter was fixed at (U_{iso}=0.003\) Å² because independent powder-only ADPs were unstable. Zero offset and specimen displacement are strongly correlated, so their individual values and formal cell/coordinate uncertainties should not be overinterpreted; forcing either geometric term to zero worsened the fit, while the phase identity and one-phase fraction conclusion were unchanged.
