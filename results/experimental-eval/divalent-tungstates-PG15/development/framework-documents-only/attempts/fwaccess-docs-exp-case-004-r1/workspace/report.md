# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by two crystalline phases: 29.60 wt% CoWO4 (wolframite type, P 2/c) and 70.40 wt% monoclinic WO3 (P 21/c). Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; no amorphous fraction can be obtained without an internal standard.

## Evidence and structural models

The 23.18/23.67/24.42 degree triplet, the 33.35-34.22 degree group, and the strong features near 41.5-42.0, 50.0, and 56.0 degrees identify monoclinic WO3. The remaining coherent reflections, including those near 19.1, 30.71, 31.5, 36.4, 38.6, 44.4, 45.9, 52.2, and 61.8 degrees, are accounted for by wolframite CoWO4. This phase inventory is also chemically consistent with CoO plus WO3 fired at 900 degrees C.

The WO3 model is based on COD 2311041 and has refined cell parameters a = 7.68798, b = 7.53131, c = 10.53006 A, beta = 136.1020 degrees (an equivalent nonstandard monoclinic setting), V = 422.749 A3. The CoWO4 topology was established from the repaired CoWO4 record COD 5910316. Because that 1951 CIF contains duplicated symmetry-equivalent sites and coarse coordinates, its oxygen framework was cross-checked and adapted from isostructural ZnWO4 (COD 1520641), whose divalent cation has a closely matched octahedral radius. Co and W were substituted/refined in P 2/c. The final CoWO4 cell is a = 4.66658, b = 5.68004, c = 4.94712 A, beta = 90.0133 degrees, V = 131.130 A3. The resulting Co-O distances are 2.059-2.140 A and W-O distances are 1.797-2.125 A.

The full-grid GSAS-II fit gives Rwp = 4.985%, Rp = 3.802%, Rexp = 3.280%, and sqrt(reduced chi-square) = 1.521. Separate trial additions gave only 0.10(9) wt% CoO with a 0.0003 percentage-point Rwp change, and Co3O4 refined to zero. These are not significant; a trace CoO level below roughly 0.3 wt% cannot be excluded.

## Remaining uncertainty

The formal scale-derived fraction uncertainty is about 0.10 wt%, but it understates correlations with Uiso, surface roughness, preferred orientation, and peak broadening. Sensitivity refinements support a more realistic absolute uncertainty of about 1.5 wt% for each reported fraction. The phase identities and wolframite/monoclinic frameworks are reliable. Exact oxygen coordinates are not independently determined by this laboratory powder pattern and remain analogue/reference anchored; unrestricted oxygen refinement was chemically unstable. Instrument width, polarization, and roughness terms approached their allowed bounds, so the submitted profile is a physically bounded effective model rather than a unique decomposition of all instrumental and specimen effects.
