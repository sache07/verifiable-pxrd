# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

The pattern is explained by two crystalline phases: **monoclinic WO3, 72.624 wt%**, and **rutile-type beta-MnO2, 27.376 wt%**. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases.

| Phase | Structural model | Refined cell (angstrom, degree) | Crystalline wt% |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Monoclinic WO3 | P 21/c; COD 2311041 parent | a=7.69520, b=7.53875, c=10.54201, beta=136.11207 | 72.624 |
| beta-MnO2 | P 42/m n m; COD 1514110 parent | a=b=4.40018, c=2.87462 | 27.376 |

## Evidence and refinement

The diagnostic WO3 group at approximately 23.44, 23.92, and 24.69 degrees 2-theta and its higher-angle reflections require the monoclinic WO3 model. The independent peaks at approximately 29.01, 37.68, 56.96, and 59.66 degrees require rutile-type beta-MnO2. Expected strong MnWO4 reflections are absent, and adding a tungstate phase was not supported by the residual pattern.

The final full-pattern GSAS-II refinement used the supplied Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and intensity ratio. It converged with Rwp=5.479%, Rp=4.041%, and goodness of fit=2.039 over 8301 points. Cell parameters, phase scales, modest anisotropic microstrain, and a weak beta-MnO2 [110] preferred-orientation correction were included. Atomic-coordinate refinement was regularized near the database parents to avoid fitting profile imperfections with chemically implausible displacements. Both exported structures pass broad contact and occupancy checks.

## Remaining uncertainty

Phase identities and the approximate two-phase ratio are well supported. Profile-model sensitivity tests changed the ratio by much less than one percentage point, but a practical uncertainty of about +/-2 absolute wt percentage points is more realistic because no external standard, direct counting-error array, or independent instrument-broadening standard was supplied. The fractions do not measure amorphous content; an absolute crystalline/amorphous split cannot be determined reliably from this scan alone. Minor crystalline impurities below the detection limit or hidden by complete peak overlap also cannot be excluded.

The heavy-atom frameworks and unit cells are reliable, while individual oxygen coordinates--especially in WO3--are less precisely determined by Cu X-ray powder data. Some profile-width, displacement, and asymmetry parameters remain correlated, consistent with the residual serial correlation (Durbin-Watson=0.527). These limitations do not change the two-phase assignment.
