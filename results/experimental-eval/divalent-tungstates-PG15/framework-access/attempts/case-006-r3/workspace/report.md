# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is best described by two crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline material; no amorphous fraction can be obtained from this scan without an internal standard.

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, °) | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| CuWO₄ | P -1 | a = 4.70797, b = 5.84220, c = 4.88134; α = 88.3443, β = 92.5327, γ = 97.2501 | 0.9702 (97.02 wt%) |
| WO₃ | P 2₁/c | a = 7.68987, b = 7.53644, c = 10.52532; β = 136.0748 (nonstandard setting) | 0.0298 (2.98 wt%) |

## Evidence

The CuO + WO₃ precursor context and 800 °C firing make CuWO₄ the chemically expected reaction product. A triclinic wolframite-related framework adapted from COD 2101690 (its mixed Cu/Zn site was replaced by Cu, since Zn is absent from the sample chemistry) accounts for the dominant peak set. Refinement of its cell and all independent atomic coordinates gives the submitted CuWO₄ model.

After fitting CuWO₄ alone, reproducible positive residual peaks near 23.43, 24.68, 34.47, 53.34, and 53.80° 2θ matched monoclinic WO₃. The P 2₁/c model based on COD 2311041 gave the best residual match and lowered Rwp from 4.965% to about 3.88%. A competing monoclinic WO₃ model/cell setting fitted less well, and adding CuO was not supported by a coherent set of its diagnostic reflections. No third crystalline phase was retained.

The final project was replayed directly from its saved atomic and profile parameters over all 8,301 points. The replay gives Rwp = 3.8743%, Rp = 2.9979%, Rexp = 2.4219%, and goodness of fit = 1.600 with Poisson count weights. The replay found no negative-width, negative-background, polarization, or displacement-parameter validity flags.

## Remaining uncertainty

The CuWO₄ identity and dominant fraction are robust. The WO₃ phase is weak but is supported by multiple non-overlapping reflections; its fraction is approximately 3 wt%, with a conservative model-dependent uncertainty of roughly ±0.5 percentage points. Its lattice and W sublattice are constrained, but the 3 wt% contribution does not determine the independent oxygen coordinates reliably: refining them produced large uncertainties and a worse fit, so the parent WO₃ oxygen geometry was retained. Minor correlated residual structure remains (goodness of fit above one), so very low-level additional crystalline material cannot be excluded, but none can be identified or quantified reliably from this pattern.
