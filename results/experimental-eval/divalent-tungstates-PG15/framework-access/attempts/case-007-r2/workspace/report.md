# Powder XRD phase analysis

Three crystalline phases account for the pattern. Weight fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; amorphous material, if present, is not quantified.

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction | Main evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Wolframite MgWO4 | P 2/c | 0.7654 (76.54 wt%) | Dominant reflections at about 15.93, 19.23, 24.21–24.92, and 30.81/31.12 degrees 2theta, with the complete higher-angle wolframite sequence. |
| Monoclinic WO3 | P 21/n | 0.1605 (16.05 wt%) | Characteristic 23.44/23.93/24.68 degree triplet, 28.94/29.23 degree pair, and the 33.6–34.5 degree group. |
| Periclase MgO | F m -3 m | 0.0741 (7.41 wt%) | Peaks at about 37.31, 43.21, 62.54, and 75.05 degrees 2theta. |

The refined cells are MgWO4: a = 4.68640, b = 5.66987, c = 4.92500 A, beta = 90.7734 degrees; WO3: a = 7.29649, b = 7.52928, c = 7.68393 A, beta = 90.8091 degrees; and MgO: a = 4.20928 A. The phase assignments use COD 9013902, 1528915, and 9000499 as the respective structural parents.

The final three-phase Rietveld model gives Rwp = 8.279%, Rp = 5.792%, Rexp = 2.860%, and goodness of fit = 2.895 over all 8301 points. A separate Le Bail check reached Rwp about 7.38%; all coherent peak families were assigned, so the residual is better explained by correlated specimen/instrument line-shape effects than by a fourth detectable crystalline phase.

Phase identity is robust. Realistic absolute systematic uncertainties are approximately 2 wt% for MgWO4, 1.5 wt% for WO3, and 1 wt% for MgO; the smaller formal scale errors in result.json do not include profile, displacement-parameter, or preferred-orientation correlations. MgWO4 and MgO frameworks are well constrained. The individual oxygen coordinates of the 16 wt% WO3 phase are not reliably determined by this overlapping, W-dominated laboratory pattern: its submitted coordinates were regularized to the P 21/n literature parent and should not be used for precise bond analysis. That parent also contains a close O...O geometry that triggers review but no hard-contact failure. No unmodeled crystalline fraction can be established reliably from the remaining residual.
