# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled by five crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized to the modeled crystalline material; no external standard was supplied, so amorphous content is not determined.

| Phase | Structural evidence | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| Scheelite PbWO4, I41/a | Dominant peak family and full-pattern reference match (COD 9009811) | 0.6072(22) |
| Pb2WO5, C2/m | Pb-rich intermediate accounting for the coherent residual family near 25.92, 29.18, 30.63/31.04, 34.86, 43.41, 47.29, 51.95, and 53.89 degrees 2theta | 0.1489(24) |
| Massicot PbO, Pbcm | Orthorhombic precursor family (COD 2310433) | 0.0779(13) |
| Litharge PbO, P4/nmm | Independent tetragonal PbO family (COD 9012700, converted from origin choice 1 to the equivalent GSAS-II origin-2 setting) | 0.0730(12) |
| Monoclinic WO3, P21/c | Residual precursor family best matched by the room-temperature COD 2311041 model | 0.0930(15) |

The Pb2WO5 assignment is chemically supported by the PbO–WO3 context and has a stable positive scale across the pattern. No exact Pb2WO5 entry was present in the frozen reference index, so its submitted structure uses the C2/m Pb2MoO5 framework (COD 7220560) with Mo replaced by W. Adding this topology reduced Rwp by about two percentage points in inventory screens and removed the coherent residual family. Its topology and fraction are supported, but its exact oxygen coordinates are less certain than those of the database-matched phases.

Refinement used the reported Cu K-alpha doublet and Poisson count weights because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied. Cells, calibration, smooth background, scales, physical size/strain profiles, and selected heavy-atom coordinates were refined in stages. March-Dollase [100] terms were supported for Pb2WO5 and massicot, and bounded [100] strain anisotropy was supported for Pb2WO5. Surface roughness and other anisotropy branches were rejected when they reached bounds or were correlated. Unconstrained light-atom refinements generated implausible W–O/Pb–O geometry and were rolled back to the physical reference coordinates; therefore light-atom displacements cannot be determined reliably from this multiphase powder pattern.

The final full-grid fit has Rwp = 10.236%, Rp = 6.845%, Rwp(min) = 3.502%, and goodness of fit = 2.923. All exported structures have physical nearest-neighbor geometry, all accepted phases have positive scales, and no coherent unassigned reflection family remains. The formal fraction ESDs in the table understate systematic model uncertainty; approximately 1–3 wt% absolute uncertainty is more realistic for the minor phases, especially analogue-derived Pb2WO5 and strongly overlapped litharge. The final project contains no frozen or dropped variables.
