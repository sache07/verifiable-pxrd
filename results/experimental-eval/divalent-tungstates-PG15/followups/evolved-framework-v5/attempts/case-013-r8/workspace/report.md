# Powder XRD phase analysis

The Cu Kα pattern is best described by five crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; an amorphous fraction was not measured.

| Phase | Space group | Weight % |
|---|---:|---:|
| PbWO4, scheelite | I 41/a | 58.44 |
| PbO, massicot | P b c m | 11.77 |
| PbO, litharge | P 4/n m m | 7.06 |
| WO3, monoclinic | P 21/c | 9.62 |
| Pb2WO5, Pb2MoO5-type | C 2/m | 13.11 |

The final full-grid Rietveld fit has Rwp = 10.322%, Rp = 7.183%, Rexp = 3.502%, and GOF = 2.952 for 8,282 points and 24 refined parameters. With no supplied pointwise uncertainties, counting-statistical weights based on sqrt(counts) were used.

## Evidence

Scheelite PbWO4 accounts for the dominant family, including the intense reflections near 27.7, 32.9, 45.0, 51.5, and 55.5 degrees 2θ. Monoclinic WO3 is required by its characteristic 23.4–24.6 degree multiplet and supporting higher-angle lines. Orthorhombic massicot and tetragonal litharge each have distinct, repeatedly observed PbO reflection families; neither polymorph alone accounts for both sets.

After those four phases were fitted, one broad coherent family remained at approximately 13–15, 25.9, 29–31, 35–43, 47–60, 64, and 72 degrees. It is indexed by a C 2/m Pb2WO5 model transferred from the isostructural Pb2MoO5 reference (COD 7220560) by Mo→W substitution. Its refined cell, a = 14.2321, b = 5.8094, c = 7.3553 Å and β = 113.8448 degrees, remains close to the parent topology and its scale is positive and stable. Raspite PbWO4, Pb3O4, W3O8, WO2, and alternative WO3 models did not provide a stable, broadly supported improvement. No coherent unassigned reflection family remains, so phase search was stopped.

The final model includes modest (100) March–Dollase corrections for massicot and Pb2WO5 and a refined effective polarization of 0.8366. Scheelite oxygen was restored to the reference coordinate because its unconstrained intensity-driven shift produced overlong W–O bonds; the submitted tetrahedron has W–O = 1.775 Å. The litharge model uses the physical P4/nmm origin setting with two Pb and two O atoms per cell (PbO, density 9.29 g cm-3). For WO3, a same-composition P21/c neutron model (COD 2311041) was selected over the slightly poorer 1960 coordinate model because the latter contains a nonphysical 1.58 Å O–O contact; the submitted model has W–O distances of 1.72–2.16 Å. WO3 oxygen coordinates and Pb2WO5 oxygen coordinates could not be refined reliably and were retained from their physical reference/analogue frameworks. The Pb2WO5 assignment is therefore chemically and diffraction-supported, but its detailed oxygen arrangement is provisional rather than independently solved from this powder pattern.

Formal covariance-only fraction uncertainties are about 0.15–0.30 weight percentage points, but these understate practical uncertainty from peak overlap, texture, profile correlations, and reference choice. In particular, the Pb2WO5 fraction should be regarded as approximate (systematic uncertainty of several weight percentage points is plausible). Fractions apply only to the modeled crystalline material.
