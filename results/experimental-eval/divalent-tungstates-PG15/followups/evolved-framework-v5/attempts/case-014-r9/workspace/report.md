# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by three crystalline phases: scheelite SrWO4, monoclinic WO3, and monoclinic Sr3W2O9. The GSAS-II full-grid atomic-model replay gives Rwp = 12.912%, Rp = 9.121%, and no invalid profile or negative-background flags.

## Evidence

The dominant scheelite model accounts for the strong families near 18.32, 28.00, 30.22, 33.39, 45.44, 47.75, 51.98, and 56.08 degrees 2theta. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the characteristic 23.45/23.97/24.73-degree cluster and its higher-angle families. After those two phases were fitted, the coherent residual family at approximately 29.75, 32.63, 44.67, 50.61, 57.00, 61.28, 69.57, 77.49, 81.90, 86.87, and 91.75 degrees indexed consistently as C 2/c Sr3W2O9. SrO, SrCO3, alternate WO3 polymorphs, and other Sr-W-O candidates did not retain a stable positive scale with comparable whole-pattern support. No coherent reflection family remains unassigned in the final three-phase fit.

The starting atomic models were COD 1546036 (SrWO4), COD 2311041 (neutron-derived WO3), and COD 2311977 (Sr3W2O9). Cells, phase scales, background, calibration/specimen terms, and supported profile/texture terms were refined. The accepted structures pass a short-contact screen: minimum W-O distances are 1.883 A in SrWO4, 1.764 A in Sr3W2O9, and 1.715 A in WO3. A legacy WO3 reference that lowered Rwp by only 0.049 percentage points was rejected because it contained an O-O contact of about 1.58 A. A bounded heavy-atom trial on the accepted WO3 model was also rolled back because its small numerical gain was unstable and shortened W-O to about 1.64 A.

## Uncertainty

Fractions are normalized only over the modeled crystalline phases; this measurement does not determine an amorphous fraction. Counting-statistical covariance estimates would understate uncertainty from preferred orientation, peak broadening, absorption, and structural-model choice; several absolute weight percent is a more realistic interpretation.

SrWO4 and WO3 are well supported by distinctive, broad peak families. The Sr3W2O9 topology and phase presence are supported by many residual reflections, but its refined cell volume is about 4.9% below the published high-pressure-synthesis reference and its scale is correlated with strong microstrain and texture terms. Its detailed coordinates and 19.65 wt% fraction therefore cannot be regarded as uniquely or highly precisely determined from this powder pattern alone, even though the C 2/c model is the best physical whole-pattern assignment found.
