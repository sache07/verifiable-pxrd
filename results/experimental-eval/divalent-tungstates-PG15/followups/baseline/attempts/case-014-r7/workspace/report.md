# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

The defensible crystalline model contains **two phases**: scheelite SrWO₄ at **87.70 wt%** and monoclinic WO₃ at **12.30 wt%**, normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. The formal GSAS-II scale-factor uncertainty is ±0.28 wt% for each fraction, but a more realistic model-based uncertainty is about ±1.5 wt% because the instrument profile was not calibrated and systematic residuals remain.

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| SrWO₄ (scheelite) | I 4₁/a | a = 5.41850 Å, c = 11.96062 Å | 87.70 wt% |
| WO₃ (monoclinic) | P 2₁/n | a = 7.30025 Å, b = 7.53969 Å, c = 7.69278 Å, β = 90.765° | 12.30 wt% |

## Evidence

The dominant reflections at 18.33, 27.99, 33.39, 45.47, 47.75, 51.98, 56.08 and 57.34° 2θ form the characteristic scheelite SrWO₄ pattern. The resolved 23.46/23.97/24.73° triplet and additional groups near 42.18, 48.62, 50.22, 53–54, 59.57 and 61.28° support room-temperature monoclinic WO₃. This assignment is also consistent with the SrCO₃ + WO₃, 900 °C synthesis context. SrCO₃, SrO/SrO₂, alternate WO₃ polymorphs and Sr₃W₂O₉ were screened as extra phases; they refined to negligible amounts, worsened the fit, or required implausible lattice distortion.

The final Rietveld statistics are Rwp = 18.67%, Rp = 13.29%, Rexp = 3.54% and GOF = 5.29. Literature atom coordinates were retained because unconstrained coordinate or texture refinements moved to chemically unreasonable/correlated solutions. A reflection-geometry specimen displacement of -0.692 mm was fitted before being fixed, so the reported cell dimensions should not be treated as metrology-grade values.

## Remaining uncertainty

Residual peaks, especially near 29.76 and 32.61° 2θ, are not explained reliably by a chemically plausible third structure. Several weaker residuals occur where Cu Kβ leakage would be expected, although the metadata specify only Kα₁/Kα₂. Thus no third phase or fraction is reported. The fractions exclude any amorphous material and unidentified crystalline content. Since pointwise uncertainties were absent, Poisson weights (σ = √counts) were used; the high GOF and visible systematic residuals make the practical ±1.5 wt% uncertainty more appropriate than the formal least-squares ESD.
