# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled with three crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases only.

| Phase | Structure | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| PbWO₄ | scheelite, *I*4₁/*a* | 77.46 wt% |
| PbO | massicot, *Pbcm* | 9.18 wt% |
| WO₃ | monoclinic, *P*2₁/*c* | 13.36 wt% |

Evidence for scheelite PbWO₄ is the dominant 27.70° peak together with the 29.91°, 33.06°, 45.03°, 47.28°, 51.49°, 55.54°, and 56.82° series. Massicot PbO accounts for the independent 15.33°, 29.35°, 30.62°, 38.09°, 53.38°, 60.53°, and 63.28° features. Monoclinic WO₃ is identified most clearly by its 23.39°/23.89°/24.64° triplet and supporting peaks near 26.77°, 34.43°, 35.95°, and 41.91°. These assignments also agree with the PbO + WO₃ reaction context.

GSAS-II Rietveld refinement used the reported Cu Kα1/Kα2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio over 10.02–99.98° 2θ. Phase scales and cells, zero shift, a smooth background, and profile terms were refined; atomic coordinates and occupancies were held at the database structural-model values. The final fit has Rwp = 14.45%, Rp = 9.80%, Rexp = 3.50%, and goodness of fit = 4.14.

The formal least-squares uncertainties are 0.29, 0.17, and 0.28 wt% for PbWO₄, PbO, and WO₃, respectively, but they understate model uncertainty because pointwise uncertainties and a calibrated instrument profile were unavailable. Background-order and alternative monoclinic-WO₃ tests suggest more realistic uncertainties of approximately ±1.5 wt% PbWO₄, ±1.0 wt% PbO, and ±1.5 wt% WO₃. No amorphous fraction can be determined without an internal standard.

A residual feature remains near 25.91° and there is structured peak-shape misfit. Trials with raspite PbWO₄, litharge PbO, PbO₂, Pb₃O₄, WO₂, and W₃O₈ did not yield a robust fourth phase: most refined to zero, while W₃O₈ gave only a marginal fit improvement with poor phase agreement. Therefore, the identity and fraction of any additional trace crystalline component cannot be determined reliably from this pattern.
