# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

The modeled crystalline fraction is:

| Phase | Space group | Weight % | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| SrWO4, scheelite | I 41/a | 70.24(34) | High confidence |
| WO3, ambient monoclinic | P 21/n | 11.01(23) | High confidence |
| Sr3W2O9 | C 2/c | 10.72(28) | Moderate confidence |
| SrO | F m -3 m | 0.49(10) | Low-confidence trace phase |
| Sr3WO6-like | P 21 prototype | 7.54(33) | Tentative; structure and fraction are not uniquely determined |

Fractions are normalized to the five modeled crystalline phases and sum to 100%. They do not measure an amorphous fraction. Parentheses are covariance-derived statistical standard uncertainties; model-selection uncertainty is larger for the last three phases.

## Evidence

Scheelite SrWO4 accounts for the dominant peak set over the full 10–100° range and refines to a = 5.41861 Å and c = 11.96088 Å with physically sensible W–O (1.882 Å) and Sr–O (2.503–2.562 Å) distances.

Monoclinic WO3 is supported by its characteristic 23.44/23.90/24.67° triplet and additional families through high angle. Ambient monoclinic, alternate monoclinic, triclinic, and orthorhombic records were compared under the same profile; the submitted P 21/n model was best. Its refined coherent-domain size is about 0.19 µm.

The C 2/c Sr3W2O9 model explains coherent paired families near 29.71/29.76, 32.54/32.65, 44.35/44.59, 50.49/50.63, 61.26/61.36, and 67.69/67.95°. Its geometry remains reasonable after cell refinement. The exact database structure was synthesized at high pressure, although measured at 298 K; that source history is unlike the reported 900 °C preparation here, and its refined cell volume is about 3.1% below the reference. The assignment is therefore not definitive despite the multi-family diffraction support.

The SrO scale remains positive at about five standard deviations after the final common profile is rebuilt. Its partly overlapped families occur near 30.34, 35.11, 50.33, 59.74, and 62.67°. At only ~0.5 wt%, its identity and fraction should be treated as trace-level estimates.

No exact Sr3WO6 entry was available. Cross-chemistry prototype screening compared Sr3UO6-derived P 21/c and P 21 models, a Ba3WO6-derived P1 model, and a Ca3WO6-derived P 21/c model. The submitted P 21 model, made by the isovalent U6+ -> W6+ substitution, gave the best fixed-cell and replayed fit and accounts for families near 28.59–30.53, 42.15/42.64, 51.0–53.5, 61.25/61.39, and 69.76/69.86°. However, its inherited coordinates give a broad W–O range of 1.86–2.43 Å. Alternate plausible prototypes changed this phase from roughly 5 to 8 wt% and shifted the other fractions by several percentage points. The CIF is therefore a fitting prototype, not a uniquely solved Sr3WO6 structure.

SrCO3 (ambient and high-temperature polymorphs) and the Sr2WO5 prototype refined to zero fixed-cell scale. A SrW2O7 analogue gave only a tiny improvement and had implausibly short inherited W–O distances; high-pressure fergusonite SrWO4 likewise lacked fixed-cell support. Cell-only improvements for these records required severe distortions and were rejected.

## Refinement quality and limits

The final full-range replay gives Rwp = 12.897%, Rp = 9.111%, Rexp = 3.535%, and goodness of fit = 3.656. The model uses the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 ratio, an eight-term smooth positive background, nonnegative common W/X width terms, one specimen-displacement correction, one positive surface-roughness term, WO3 size broadening, and isotropic microstrain for the two Sr-rich tungstates. Texture, free SH/L, unsupported size/strain terms, and unphysical atom-coordinate/ADP trials were pruned.

Residual correlation remains substantial (Durbin–Watson = 0.176), particularly around the strongest scheelite profile and the crowded ~29.7° region. Consequently, the formal fraction uncertainties understate systematic uncertainty. SrWO4 and WO3 are reliable identifications; Sr3W2O9 is plausible but source-condition-sensitive; SrO is a weak trace assignment; and neither the detailed Sr3WO6 structure nor its fraction can be claimed reliably from this pattern alone.
