## Evidence and refinement

The dominant scheelite SrWO₄ model accounts for coherent low-, middle-, and high-angle families including approximately 18.3° (101), 27.95° (112), 33.35° (200), 45.43° (204), 47.72° (220), 56.05° (312), and 72.98° (316). Monoclinic WO₃ is independently required by its 23.45/23.90/24.67° triplet and families near 33.58°, 41.76/42.16°, 50–51°, and 56.18°. The two Sr-rich models account for several additional families: the C2/c component has strong lines near 29.71°, 32.54°, 44.37–44.59°, 50.63°, and 67.70°, while the P2₁/c prototype contributes near 28.61°, 30.54°, 42.18/42.59°, and 53.36/53.58°. Trace SrO is supported by partly independent rock-salt families near 30.36°, 35.15°, 59.80°, 73.80°, and 94.52°; removing it reproducibly worsened the full-range fit.

Starting structures were COD 1546036 (SrWO₄ topology), 1528915 (ambient monoclinic WO₃), 2311977 (Sr₃W₂O₉), and 7200689 (SrO). No exact Sr₃WO₆ entry was available, so cross-chemistry analogues were screened as requested. The submitted P2₁/c model is the charge-preserving Ca²⁺→Sr²⁺ substitution of Ca₃WO₆ COD 1563081; its refined W–O distances remain physical at 2.051–2.082 Å. The refined SrWO₄ oxygen site gives W–O = 1.744 Å and agrees closely with independent I4₁/a molybdate/tungstate analogue coordinates. SrCO₃ refined to zero. A fergusonite SrWO₄ record improved residuals only by contracting an already 11 GPa reference cell to an even smaller, source-incompatible volume, so it was rejected as a residual surrogate.

The full 10.009–99.994° Cu Kα profile was refined with the reported Kα wavelengths and 0.5 Kα₂/Kα₁ ratio. Since pointwise uncertainties were absent, counting-statistics weights were used. The accepted baseline has a six-coefficient smooth Chebyshev background, one specimen-displacement correction, positive common W/X broadening, fixed low SH/L asymmetry, and a one-parameter low-angle roughness correction. Crystallite-size trials ran to bounds and were pruned. Only the C2/c phase retained replay-stable isotropic microstrain (~6420 µstrain). Axial and diagonal March–Dollase trials stayed close to random orientation and were rejected. The saved/reopened final replay converged with Rwp = 12.987%, Rp = 9.298%, Rexp = 3.535%, and GOF = 3.681.

## Remaining uncertainty

SrWO₄ and WO₃ are reliable phase identifications. The Sr₃W₂O₉-like model comes from a high-pressure-synthesis record and refines to a contracted cell; the Sr₃WO₆ model is a substituted prototype. Their peaks overlap substantially, so their atomic structures and the 11.62/6.80 wt% partition are not uniquely or fully reliably determined from this pattern. Treat both CIFs as provisional and those fractions as semi-quantitative despite the smaller least-squares esds. The 0.56 wt% SrO fraction is also not reliable beyond a trace-level statement. Residual broad/intensity structure and GOF > 1 show remaining systematic model/instrument error, and the quoted statistical esds do not include that uncertainty.
