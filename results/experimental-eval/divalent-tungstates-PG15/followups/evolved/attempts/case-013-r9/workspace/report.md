## Evidence

Scheelite PbWO4 supplies the dominant peak backbone over the complete range and uses the 290 K structure in COD 9009811. Massicot PbO is supported by multiple families and is strongly preferred over litharge. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the low-angle multiplets and many weaker high-angle lines. In staged common-profile omission controls, fits without PbO and WO3 gave Rwp values of 15.24% and 15.06%, respectively.

No exact Pb2WO5 or Pb5WO8 entry was available, so cross-chemistry stoichiometric analogues were screened. Isovalent Mo6+ to W6+ substitution in Pb2MoO5 (COD 7220560) gives a C2/m Pb2WO5 model whose families near 25.97, 30.72, 31.16, 39.52, and 43.49° match coherent residuals. The scale is positive before cell refinement, and the refined model retains tetrahedral W–O distances of 1.77–1.80 Å. Its staged omission control gave Rwp = 16.15%.

The analogous Pb5MoO8 structure (COD 2007541) gives a P21/c Pb5WO8 model supported by partly isolated residual families near 27.53, 28.83, 30.94, and 46.41°. Its refined W–O distances remain physical at 1.70–1.77 Å, and its staged omission control gave Rwp = 13.82%. Pb3WO6 and raspite alternatives lowered residuals only by forcing implausible cell changes; Pb5CrO8/Pb2CrO5 substitutions gave too-short W–O geometry. These were rejected.

The accepted baseline uses a smooth six-coefficient Chebyshev background, one zero correction, nonnegative U/W/X profile terms, SH/L asymmetry, and one polarization term. Isolated low-, middle-, and high-angle families show no systematic position drift, and their widths/asymmetry are broadly reproduced. Pb2WO5 and Pb5WO8 require replay-stable isotropic microstrain; size-plus-strain combinations were redundant. Roughness, transparency, crystallite size, extinction, and texture remain neutral. Symmetry-distinct March–Dollase axes gave non-unique improvements and were rejected. The accepted project was saved, reopened, and replayed over the full measured range before export.

## Remaining uncertainty

The WO3 monoclinic and orthorhombic records fit nearly equally well, so the submitted P21/c cell is a representative room-temperature model rather than a uniquely established polymorph. The Pb2WO5 and Pb5WO8 assignments are chemically and diffraction-supported prototypes, but their atomic coordinates were not refined; their exact structures cannot be determined reliably from this overlapped laboratory pattern.

The two prototype fractions are also not reliable to their small covariance e.s.d. values. Without phase-specific strain they refine to about 14.9 and 9.3 wt%, versus 16.1 and 12.8 wt% in the accepted physical profile, demonstrating several-weight-percent systematic sensitivity. Broad residual intensity remains around roughly 46–49° and 54–56°; it may reflect imperfect prototype intensities, additional disorder, or an unmodeled poorly crystalline component. With no internal standard, an amorphous fraction cannot be quantified.

Pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, so Poisson weights (`sigma = sqrt(counts)`) were used. All reported e.s.d. values are covariance-only and exclude inventory, polymorph, prototype, background, and profile-model uncertainty.
