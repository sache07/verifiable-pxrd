# Powder XRD phase analysis

The 900 °C SrCO3/WO3 product is modeled with three crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases.

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| SrWO4 | Scheelite, I41/a | 0.8153 (81.53 wt%) |
| WO3 | Monoclinic, P21/c | 0.1141 (11.41 wt%) |
| Sr3WO6 | Ordered double-perovskite-type, P21/n | 0.0706 (7.06 wt%) |

The fresh full-grid GSAS-II atomic replay gives Rwp = 15.57%, Rp = 10.71%, and GOF = 4.41 for 8,282 points. The Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 doublet, specimen displacement, background, phase scales, cells, and isotropic crystallite-size broadening are included in `final.gpx`.

SrWO4 is established by the dominant scheelite family across the full range, including the strong lines near 27.99, 33.39, 45.47, 51.98, and 56.08 degrees 2theta. Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the resolved low-angle group near 23.5–24.7 degrees and its higher-angle family. A modern neutron-derived WO3 model was retained; an older numerically close reference was rejected because it contained a nonphysical 1.58 Å O–O contact.

The minority family near 29.76, 44.66, 48.61, 53.44, 61.22, and 67.03 degrees indexes to an Sr3WO6 double-perovskite-type lattice. This assignment is chemically compatible with the Sr/W/O system, and the submitted ordered model has ordinary W–O, Sr–O, and O–O geometry. SrCO3, SrO, fergusonite SrWO4, and Sr3W2O9 trials did not retain stable positive scales.

The Sr3WO6 assignment is less secure than SrWO4 and WO3 because no exact-composition ambient reference was available in the frozen index and some of its relative intensities remain imperfect. Its 7.06 wt% value is therefore semi-quantitative and model-dependent to a few weight percent; the exact minority structure and fraction cannot be determined reliably from this pattern alone. No additional coherent unindexed reflection family was found. Amorphous material, if present, is not quantified.
