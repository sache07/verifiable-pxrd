# Phase analysis and refinement report

## Result

The full 9.99–100.09° pattern is described by three crystalline phases. The result is chemically consistent with partial reaction of the stated CaCO3 and WO3 precursors at 500 °C to form scheelite CaWO4.

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction | Formal GSAS-II e.s.d. |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| CaWO4, scheelite | `I 41/a`, COD 7219229 | 12.244 wt% | 0.080 wt% |
| WO3, monoclinic | `P 1 21/c 1`, COD 2311041 | 55.625 wt% | 0.131 wt% |
| CaCO3, calcite | `R -3 c`, COD 1547347 | 32.131 wt% | 0.154 wt% |

The fractions sum to 100% over the modeled crystalline phases. The final full-grid fit has Rp = 5.462%, Rwp = 7.437%, Rexp = 2.394%, and goodness of fit = 3.111 for 8293 observations.

## Evidence and model selection

Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the characteristic 23.42/23.90/24.67° triplet and its higher-angle reflection family. Calcite accounts for the distinct reflections near 29.71, 39.70, 47.52, and 48.53°, while scheelite CaWO4 accounts for the product reflections near 18.90, 29.04, 47.52, and 56.19°. Overlaps were resolved by simultaneous whole-pattern refinement, not by isolated peak areas. No coherent residual reflection family remains that warrants another crystalline phase.

Same-composition WO3 alternatives were tested. A triclinic model was close but gave a slightly poorer whole-pattern fit and represents a less appropriate low-temperature polymorph; the ordinary monoclinic model was therefore retained. Its exported nonstandard monoclinic setting is crystallographically valid. Final coordination geometry is physical: W–O is 1.73–2.18 Å in WO3, W–O is 1.762 Å in scheelite, and C–O is 1.256 Å in calcite. Unbounded light-atom trials that produced implausible bonds were rolled back.

The refinement used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio in Bragg–Brentano geometry. It included background, phase scales and cells, specimen displacement/calibration, common instrumental profile terms, phase size/microstrain broadening, and a mild calcite [001] March–Dollase correction (ratio 0.938). WO3 required supported [110] anisotropic microstrain. The calculated profile in `fit.csv` is the profile stored in `final.gpx` on the original measured grid.

## Remaining uncertainty

Pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, so Poisson counting estimates, sigma = sqrt(max(counts, 1)), were used. Consequently the formal e.s.d.s above do not include model, preferred-orientation, absorption, or instrumental-profile systematics and are likely optimistic. The normalized crystalline fractions are therefore best regarded as semi-quantitative; an absolute amorphous fraction cannot be determined without an internal standard.

WO3 isotropic displacement parameters could not be refined reliably: a bounded trial drove them to the lower bound or negative values. The reference values were retained and should not be interpreted as measured thermal parameters. Composition and phase identity are more secure than this detail of the WO3 structural model or the distinction among very closely related WO3 distortions.
