# Powder XRD phase analysis

The accepted crystalline inventory is one phase: monoclinic wolframite MnWO4 (space group P 2/c), at 100.0 wt% after normalization over the modeled crystalline phases.

The assignment is supported by the complete reflection family, including the characteristic peaks at approximately 15.68, 18.64, 23.85, 24.31, 30.08/30.56, 36.22, 37.46 and 41.13 degrees 2theta. A P 2/c MnWO4 reference (COD 1520200) was adapted by full-grid Rietveld refinement. The final cell is a = 4.83342, b = 5.76019, c = 4.99907 angstrom and beta = 91.2202 degrees. Refined octahedral distances remain physical: Mn-O = 2.085-2.287 angstrom and W-O = 1.806-2.161 angstrom. Multiple small coordinate starts converged to the same structure.

The independently regenerated, zero-parameter GSAS-II replay over all 8301 raw points gives Rwp = 3.9400%, Rp = 3.0586% and GOF = 1.141 (the counting-statistical Rwp minimum is 3.4605%). No coherent residual reflection family remains; the largest residuals coincide with MnWO4 peaks and are profile/intensity details rather than new peak positions.

Plausible residual phases were checked. Mn3WO6 predicts strong families near 20.0-20.5, 28.8, 35.3 and 46.6 degrees that are absent. A monoclinic WO3 scale was insignificant. Tetragonal Mn3O4 gave a diagnostic scale corresponding to about 0.49(13) wt%, but lowered Rwp by only 0.003 percentage point and did not receive broad support from its non-overlapping 29.2, 32.6, 38.3, 44.7, 60.1 and 81.5 degree families. It is therefore not asserted as a phase; a trace below roughly 1 wt% cannot be excluded or quantified reliably.

The reported 100% fraction is conditional on the accepted crystalline inventory and does not measure amorphous content. Instrument U versus isotropic microstrain, instrument X versus crystallite size, zero versus specimen displacement, and the weak preferred-orientation axis are correlated. These profile/calibration ambiguities do not affect the MnWO4 identification, but formal cell uncertainties and profile-derived size/strain values should be treated as conditional.
