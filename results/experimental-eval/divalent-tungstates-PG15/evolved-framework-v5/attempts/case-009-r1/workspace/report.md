# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is adequately described by one crystalline phase: wolframite-type MnWO4, space group P 2/c (No. 13). Its weight fraction is 1.000 after normalization over the modeled crystalline phases.

## Evidence

The 900 °C Mn3O4 + WO3, 1:1 Mn:W context makes MnWO4 the ordinary reaction product. The complete observed reflection sequence—including the diagnostic lines near 15.66, 18.64, 23.83/24.30, 30.06/30.54, 36.22, and 41.13 degrees 2theta—indexes with the P 2/c wolframite cell. A COD MnWO4 model (COD 1520200) supplied the topology; full-pattern refinement moved its compressed starting cell to the ambient-sized cell a = 4.83110(10), b = 5.75746(13), c = 4.99666(11) A, beta = 91.2168(10) degrees.

The plausible alternatives do not have broad residual-family support. Mn3WO6 would require strong lines near 19.97, 20.50, and 28.77 degrees; tetragonal Mn3O4 would require the 28.90 and 32.35 degree family; and monoclinic WO3 would give a strong low-angle triplet beginning near 23.2 degrees. Those diagnostic families are absent. Positive residuals are concentrated at already indexed MnWO4 peaks rather than at a second coherent set of positions, so phase searching was stopped.

The final Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 Rietveld fit uses all 8,301 points from 9.9034 to 100.0949 degrees and Poisson counting weights. It gives Rwp = 5.0003%, Rp = 3.7432%, Rexp = 3.4505%, and goodness-of-fit = 1.4492. The refined structure is geometrically ordinary: Mn-O distances are 2.113-2.305 A and W-O distances are 1.779-2.128 A. Multiple small coordinate starts converged to the same model. Unsupported angular-zero, uniaxial-strain, and preferred-orientation terms refined to zero or unity or worsened the stable fit and were omitted.

## Remaining uncertainty

The reported 1.000 fraction is a crystalline-phase-normalized result, not an absolute bulk or amorphous-content measurement. No internal standard was supplied, so amorphous material cannot be quantified. Trace crystalline impurities below the pattern's detection level, especially phases whose strongest lines overlap MnWO4, cannot be excluded reliably; no separate fraction is defensible for them.

The scale/surface-roughness and specimen-shift/axial-divergence pairs remain strongly correlated. Uiso was therefore fixed at 0.003 A2, and the instrumental/specimen broadening split should not be interpreted uniquely. These correlations limit the precision of oxygen coordinates and microstructural parameters more than they affect the phase identification or the one-phase normalized fraction.
