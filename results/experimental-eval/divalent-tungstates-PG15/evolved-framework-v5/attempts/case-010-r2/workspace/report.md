# Powder XRD phase analysis

The crystalline specimen is an unreacted/partly processed precursor mixture: monoclinic WO3 (73.2 wt%) and cubic bixbyite Mn2O3 (26.8 wt%), normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. No crystalline manganese tungstate product is required by the pattern.

|---|---|---|---:|
| WO3 | P 21/c | a = 7.69316, b = 7.53684, c = 10.54599, beta = 136.16265 | 73.22 |
| Mn2O3 | I a -3 | a = 9.41286 | 26.78 |

## Evidence

The dominant split peaks at 23.43, 23.91 and 24.68 degrees 2-theta, together with the repeated 33-35, 47-56 and 60-63 degree families, select low-symmetry WO3. Among the exact-composition frozen references, the physical P 21/c model derived from COD 2311041 fit better than the P 21/n and triclinic alternatives; the rejected old idealized model also contained sub-angstrom contacts.

After fitting WO3, a coherent residual family at approximately 33.27, 38.53, 45.49, 49.62, 55.46, 66.03 and 81.83 degrees is indexed by bixbyite Mn2O3. Its refined scale is stable and positive. Cubic I a -3 is both the chemically ordinary ambient structure and marginally better than the orthorhombic and noncentrosymmetric exact-composition alternatives. MnWO4 and Mn3WO6 were tested but their strong independent 18-20 and 30-31 degree families are absent. No coherent reflection family remains unassigned in the accepted two-phase model. An unreacted 1:1 Mn:W precursor charge would contain 74.6 wt% WO3 and 25.4 wt% Mn2O3, close to the refined 73.2:26.8 ratio.

The final full-grid Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 refinement uses the reported wavelengths and 0.5 doublet ratio, Poisson count weights, a smooth 16-term background, cell/scale refinement, specimen displacement, bounded instrument broadening, isotropic size broadening, and supported [010] uniaxial microstrain for WO3. Heavy-atom coordinates were refined before light atoms. A WO3 oxygen branch that produced a 1.53 A W-O contact was rolled back. The submitted structures remain physical: shortest W-O, Mn-O and O-O distances are 1.741, 1.856 and 2.539 A, respectively.

The replayed fit gives Rwp = 6.236%, Rp = 4.544%, Rexp = 2.662% and goodness of fit about 2.34 over all 8301 points. The formal scale-covariance uncertainty is about 0.18 wt% per phase, but unknown instrument/profile correlations dominate; the Gaussian U term and effective polarization reached their physical search bounds. A more realistic fraction uncertainty is roughly +/-2 wt%. Phase identities and structure types are reliable, while oxygen coordinates and fine specimen-profile parameters are less well determined by this laboratory X-ray pattern. Fractions exclude any amorphous material, which cannot be quantified without an internal standard.
