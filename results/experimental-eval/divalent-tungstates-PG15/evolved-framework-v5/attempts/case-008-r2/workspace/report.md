# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

| Phase | Space group | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| MnWO4, wolframite | P 2/c | 97.97(7)% |
| WO3, monoclinic | P 21/c | 2.03(7)% |

Fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases; amorphous material, if present, is not quantified. The quoted parentheses are GSAS-II statistical standard uncertainties and do not include model or instrument systematic error.

## Evidence and structural models

The dominant reflections at 15.71, 18.68, 23.89, 24.36, 30.12, 30.58, 31.36, 36.27, 36.62, 37.52, and 41.19 degrees 2theta, together with the dense high-angle family, index as wolframite MnWO4. The model began from COD 1520200 and refined to a = 4.83105, b = 5.75699, c = 4.99635 A and beta = 91.2183 degrees. Refined Mn-O and W-O ranges are 2.016-2.240 A and 1.870-2.203 A, respectively, with sixfold coordination and no short nonbonded contacts.

After fitting MnWO4, coherent positive residuals at 23.45, 24.68, 33.58, 34.46, 42.18, 50.22, 51.05, 56.22, and additional weaker positions were jointly explained by monoclinic WO3. Its scale remained positive at more than 25 statistical standard uncertainties. The submitted WO3 model uses the neutron-diffraction structure COD 2311041; its cell refined to a = 7.68141, b = 7.54089, c = 10.52121 A and beta = 136.0662 degrees (a nonstandard P 21/c setting). It retains six-coordinate W with W-O = 1.715-2.159 A and a minimum O-O separation of 2.539 A.

An initially better-fitting but low-precision WO3 reference (COD 1528915) was rejected because it produced a nonphysical 1.58 A O-O contact. The selected physical WO3 model gives Rwp = 6.352%, only 0.007 percentage point above that defective-coordinate checkpoint. A physical triclinic WO3 alternative gave Rwp = 6.383%, and an orthorhombic alternative gave 6.421%; the room-temperature monoclinic model was therefore retained. Trial MnO and Mn3O4 scales were only about 0.14(9)% and 0.18(17)% before WO3 was included and lacked broad residual-family support. Characteristic Mn3WO6 reflections were also absent; its numerical trial was unstable, so numerical failure itself was not treated as evidence of absence.

## Fit and uncertainty

The final full-grid refinement converged with Rwp = 6.3515%, Rp = 4.4345%, Rexp = 2.7588%, goodness of fit = 2.3087, and Durbin-Watson = 0.4531 over 8,301 points. The remaining residual is dominated by correlated peak-shape/intensity mismatch around strong MnWO4 reflections rather than an unindexed reflection family. Axial-divergence and specimen-displacement terms remain strongly correlated (96.7%), and the uncalibrated instrument profile cannot be uniquely separated from crystallite-size/microstrain broadening.

The MnWO4 topology, cell, and dominance are reliable. The approximately 2.0 wt% WO3 fraction is well supported, but its practical systematic uncertainty is larger than the 0.07 wt% counting-statistical value; a few tenths of a weight percent is more realistic across valid WO3 profile/ADP choices. WO3 atomic coordinates could not be refined reliably at this abundance and were retained from the physical published model; only its cell, scale, and a common positive isotropic displacement parameter were adjusted. Trace phases below roughly a few tenths of a weight percent cannot be excluded.
