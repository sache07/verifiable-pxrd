# Phase analysis

The pattern is best described as an unreacted two-phase precursor mixture, consistent with the low reported firing temperature (200 °C):

- **BaO₂**, tetragonal *I4/mmm* (COD 1010113): **0.3466(12)** modeled crystalline weight fraction.
- **WO₃**, triclinic *P-1* (COD 2106293 topology): **0.6534(12)** modeled crystalline weight fraction.

The fractions are normalized over these two modeled crystalline phases. The parenthesized values are least-squares standard uncertainties only; profile/model systematics are larger, so a practical interpretation is approximately **35 wt% BaO₂ / 65 wt% WO₃** rather than precision at the 0.1 wt% level.

## Evidence

The WO₃ assignment accounts for the characteristic low-angle triplet near 23.44, 23.94, and 24.68° 2θ and the associated families around 33–35, 42, 47–51, and 54–56°. The BaO₂ model accounts for the strong family near 26–27, 33.6, 43, 46.7, and 55.8°. Physical ambient WO₃ references were compared under the same multiphase profile model; the triclinic *P-1* topology gave the best overall peak splitting and intensity agreement. A short W-coordinate adjustment was retained, while unrestricted oxygen-coordinate refinement was rolled back because it overfit and did not improve the replay-consistent model.

The refined cells are BaO₂: *a* = 3.81290 Å, *c* = 6.84855 Å; and WO₃: *a* = 7.31594 Å, *b* = 7.53312 Å, *c* = 7.69153 Å, α = 89.4962°, β = 90.7187°, γ = 90.2788°. Geometry remains physical: O–O in peroxide is 1.315 Å and the six-coordinate W–O range is 1.714–2.234 Å.

The full-grid GSAS-II fit has Rwp = **7.183%**, Rp = **5.371%**, Rexp = **3.641%**, and goodness of fit = **3.892**. Reloading and recomputing the saved project reproduced Rwp to better than 0.000001 percentage point.

## Excluded phases and uncertainty

BaWO₄ refined to only 0.017 ± 0.066 wt% and did not improve Rwp, so no crystalline tungstate product is supported. Cubic BaO gave an apparent ~0.30 wt% scale, but its predicted reflections did not form a coherent residual family and the Rwp change was only 0.014 percentage point; it is therefore treated as overlap/systematic residual rather than a determined phase. Fractions for either excluded phase cannot be determined reliably.

Peak breadth requires strong size/strain broadening (about 0.069 µm and 7700 ppm for BaO₂; 0.090 µm and 8000 ppm for WO₃). The WO₃ strain term reaches the physically allowed screening range, and the uncalibrated instrumental/specimen contributions are correlated. Consequently, crystallite size, microstrain, light-atom coordinates, and the formal fraction uncertainties should not be overinterpreted. No additional coherent residual reflection family remains unindexed by the accepted phases.
