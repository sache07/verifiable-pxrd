# Powder XRD phase analysis

The pattern is explained by two crystalline precursor phases; no crystalline reaction product is required. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases.

The formal covariance uncertainty is ±0.15 wt% for each complementary fraction. A more realistic uncertainty is about ±1–2 wt% because preferred orientation, surface roughness, profile partitioning, and the close WO3 polymorph checkpoint are systematic rather than counting-statistical uncertainties. Amorphous material, if present, was not quantified.

## Evidence and model selection

The WO3 assignment is anchored by the characteristic 23.443/23.932/24.693° triplet and the related 33.59–34.48°, 41–56°, and high-angle families. The physical room-temperature neutron model (COD 2311041) indexes all of them. A triclinic WO3 checkpoint was numerically very close at a matched two-phase/profile stage (Rwp 6.95% versus 6.97% for monoclinic), but adds lower-symmetry freedom without a uniquely required reflection family; the chemically ordinary room-temperature monoclinic model was therefore retained. Orthorhombic WO3 fit poorly, and an old WO3 entry with sub-ångström contacts was rejected as nonphysical.

Pyrolusite accounts for the coherent 29.007, 37.678, 56.955, 59.661, and 72.527° family. Its strong 110 line relative to the other rutile-type lines supports a modest [110] March–Dollase texture term. No coherent residual family remains after fitting WO3 and MnO2.

MnWO4 was explicitly added at the final two-phase checkpoint. It refined to only 0.15(6) wt% and changed Rwp by 0.003 percentage point, without broad support from its unique low-angle/30° lines. This trace suggestion is below reliable phase-detection/model-selection significance (formal three-sigma upper scale is about 0.31 wt%), so MnWO4 is not included in the reported crystalline inventory.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The full 9.903–100.095° Cu Kα grid was refined with Poisson count weights because point uncertainties were not supplied. The final GSAS-II model includes a smooth background, phase scales and cells, specimen displacement, bounded instrument/profile terms, WO3 size/microstrain, and the supported MnO2 texture. Heavy-atom positions were refined first. The unconstrained WO3 oxygen branch produced excessive shifts and a 1.59 Å W–O contact and was rolled back; the submitted oxygen adjustment was bounded to approximately 0.05 Å RMS and retains a minimum W–O distance of 1.73 Å. Oxygen coordinates are consequently less certain than the phase identity, cells, and heavy-atom framework.

Final statistics are Rwp=5.523%, Rp=4.054%, Rexp=2.693%, and goodness of fit=2.054 (8301 points, 28 varied parameters). The project independently replays on the complete observed grid with the same Rwp, the same 0.71510/0.28490 fractions, and no physical profile flags. The remaining excess over Rexp is mainly structured intensity/profile mismatch, so the formal uncertainties should not be read as total accuracy.
