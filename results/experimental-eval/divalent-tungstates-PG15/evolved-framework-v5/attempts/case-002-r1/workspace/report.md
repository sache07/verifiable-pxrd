# Powder XRD phase analysis

## Result

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| Triclinic-like WO3 | P -1, derived from COD 2106293 | 0.6597 (65.97 wt%) |
| BaO2 | I 4/mmm, derived from COD 1010113 | 0.3403 (34.03 wt%) |

Fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. The formal GSAS-II standard uncertainty is about 0.11 wt% for each fraction, but a more realistic uncertainty is several wt% because profile broadening, calibration, WO3 polymorph choice, and any amorphous material are not represented by that statistical value.

## Evidence and refinement

The BaO2 model accounts for the coherent family at approximately 26.33, 27.07, 33.54, 42.98, 46.64, 55.83, and 68.90 degrees 2theta. WO3 accounts for the intense 23.42/23.92/24.66-degree triplet, the 33.5--34.4-degree group, and the many broader high-angle families. A physical triclinic WO3 reference gave a substantially better same-protocol fit than the physical monoclinic alternative (about 7.18% versus 7.89% Rwp before final nuisance refinement), principally through the observed reflection splitting.

BaWO4 was tested but rejected: its diagnostic approximately 28.04- and 31.85-degree reflections are absent (the other strong lines overlap BaO2). Ba3W2O9, Ba3WO6, and Ba-poor tungsten-oxide references likewise require strong unobserved families. After fitting WO3 and BaO2, the remaining positive residuals coincide with their modeled reflections rather than forming another indexable family, so no third crystalline phase was retained.

The final full-grid Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 refinement used a smooth 12-term background, separate phase scales and cells, specimen displacement, phase-size and uniaxial microstrain terms, and refined polarization/asymmetry. The refined cells are WO3: a = 7.31616, b = 7.53450, c = 7.69240 A, alpha = 89.5646, beta = 90.7221, gamma = 90.2936 degrees; BaO2: a = 3.81362 and c = 6.84902 A. Final statistics are Rwp = 7.098%, Rp = 5.333%, Rexp = 3.641%, and goodness of fit = 1.952.

## Remaining uncertainty

The WO3 assignment is best described as triclinic-like: broad, strained peaks make an exact low-symmetry versus monoclinic polymorph determination less secure than the phase-composition assignment. Bounded coordinate refinement did not produce a physical improvement, so reference atomic coordinates were retained; oxygen displacements therefore cannot be determined reliably from this pattern. The submitted structures pass geometry checks (minimum WO3 W--O = 1.716 A and O--O = 2.562 A; BaO2 peroxide O--O = 1.315 A and Ba--O = 2.767 A). Trace crystalline phases below the residual/noise level and amorphous content cannot be quantified, and the reported fractions apply only to the modeled crystalline material.
