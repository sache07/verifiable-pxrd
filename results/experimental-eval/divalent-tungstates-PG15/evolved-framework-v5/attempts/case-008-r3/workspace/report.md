# Powder XRD phase analysis

The specimen is modeled as 97.51 wt% wolframite MnWO4 (P 2/c) and 2.49 wt% monoclinic WO3 (P 21/c), normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. The covariance-only uncertainty is 0.063 wt% for either fraction. A more realistic uncertainty for the minor WO3 amount is about 0.7 percentage point because its scale changes with the supported minor-phase broadening model; the phase is reliable, but its abundance is less precise than the formal covariance estimate.

MnWO4 accounts for the dominant families near 15.72, 18.68, 23.89/24.36, 30.12/30.58/31.37, 36.27/37.52, and 41.18 degrees 2theta. A MnWO4-only fit left a coherent family near 23.45, 24.68, 33.58, 34.47, 42.18, 50.21, and 56.23 degrees; these reflections are jointly indexed by monoclinic WO3 and disappear from the coherent residual after it is added. This inventory is also chemically ordinary for MnO + WO3 heated at 900 degrees C: MnWO4 is the reaction product and a small amount of residual WO3 is plausible. No coherent family remains that supports MnO, Mn3WO6, or a third crystalline phase.

The starting structures were COD 1520200 for MnWO4 and COD 2311041 for WO3 (the neutron structure of Loopstra and Boldrini, 1966). Cells, phase scales, Mn/W y coordinates in MnWO4, calibration, background, specimen terms, and supported profile terms were refined against all 8301 points with counting-statistics weights. Oxygen coordinates in MnWO4 and all coordinates in the 2.5 wt% WO3 phase were retained from the references because coordinate refinements were unstable or worsened physical geometry. The WO3 reference's zero placeholder displacement factors were replaced by fixed physical values (Uiso = 0.005 A^2 for W and 0.008 A^2 for O); the minor phase cannot determine ADPs reliably.

Independent CIF checks recover the stated compositions and space groups. Mn-O distances are 2.102-2.276 A, MnWO4 W-O distances are 1.797-2.132 A, and WO3 W-O distances are 1.714-2.158 A, with sixfold metal-oxygen coordination and no short-contact warning. The final full-grid refinement converged with Rwp = 4.242%, Rp = 3.274%, GOF = 1.540, chi-square = 19626.8, and maximum shift/esd = 0.016. Residual serial correlation and scale/surface-roughness correlation remain, so the quoted minor fraction should not be interpreted at the formal third-decimal precision.
