# Phase analysis and Rietveld refinement

## Result

The crystalline pattern is adequately described by the two precursor oxides; no crystalline manganese tungstate product is required after heating at 300 °C. Fractions are normalized only over the modeled crystalline material, so they do not measure amorphous content.

| Phase | Structural model | Crystalline wt.% |
|---|---|---:|
| WO3 | triclinic, P -1, COD 2106293 | 73.59 ± 0.26 (formal) |
| MnO2 | pyrolusite, P 42/m n m, COD 1514110 | 26.41 ± 0.26 (formal) |

The final fit has Rwp = 5.8746%, Rp = 4.3520%, Rexp = 2.6932%, and goodness of fit = 2.1847. The formal fraction errors come from the GSAS-II covariance matrix. A more realistic model-dependent uncertainty is about ±1–2 wt.% per phase because WO3 displacement parameters and polarization are strongly correlated and plausible nuisance-model variants move the fractions by about 1.5 percentage points.

## Identification evidence

The WO3 assignment accounts for the diagnostic 23.44/23.93/24.69° triplet and the families near 33.55–34.48°, 47.52–50.19°, and 55–56°. Pyrolusite is independently required by its 110, 101, 211, 220, and 301 families near 29.01, 37.67, 56.96, 59.67, and 72.56°. These include isolated or partly overlapped peaks over the low-, middle-, and high-angle ranges.

Several exact WO3 records were screened with the same restrained background, scale, displacement, cell, and common-profile model. Baseline Rwp values were 7.07% for the selected P -1 record, 7.48% for a P21/c record, 8.70% for P21/n, and 14.52% for Pnma. A malformed record and a record with a 0.464 Å contact were rejected on import/geometry rather than used as evidence against the phase. The selected structures are fully occupied and ordered. Exported minimum distances are 1.715 Å for WO3; pyrolusite Mn–O distances are 1.884–1.888 Å.

The exact low symmetry of WO3 remains less certain than its phase identity. Its refined cell is nearly orthogonal, and one small triclinic reciprocal-metric cross term alternated between basis-equivalent solutions. That term was held after cell refinement; the other cell terms replay stably. Thus the submitted P -1 model is the best tested powder model, but a closely related monoclinic description cannot be excluded absolutely.

## Inventory challenges

The final common profile was re-challenged with plausible weak phases. Fixed-cell scale tests gave about 0.23 wt.% MnWO4, 0.12 wt.% Mn3WO6, and 0.76 wt.% orthorhombic Mn2O3; ambient hausmannite Mn3O4 and an alternative MnO2 polymorph refined to zero. These small positive scales were not accepted automatically:

- MnWO4 lacks coherent support at its strongest unique lines near 18.8 and 30.3/30.8°. The exact database record is a 2.4 GPa structure; an analogue search also returned ambient P2/c wolframite parents, with the same absent diagnostic family.
- Mn3WO6 is below useful significance and its strong low-angle families are absent.
- The apparent Mn2O3 scale is driven mainly by one broad residual near its strongest 222 line. Its next diagnostic families do not improve coherently, and freeing its orthorhombic cell produces an implausibly large distortion. A trace oxide below roughly 1 wt.% cannot be ruled out, but its identity and fraction are not reliable enough to model.

## Refinement and remaining limitations

The 8301 raw-count points from 9.9034–100.0949° were fitted as Cu Kα1/Kα2 data using the supplied wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio. Since no uncertainties were supplied, Poisson count weights were used. The final Bragg–Brentano model uses one specimen shift (-549 µm), a positive smooth eight-term Chebyshev background (643–2475 counts), nonnegative U/X common width terms with W fixed at zero after its free trial became slightly negative, and fixed minimal SH/L asymmetry. Roughness, transparency, unused size/strain, and WO3 texture terms are neutral.

WO3 requires replay-stable [001] uniaxial microstrain (about 0.414%/0.487%); an isotropic model worsened Rwp by about 0.03 percentage point, while size was insignificant. MnO2 requires modest [110] March–Dollase texture (ratio 0.807): removing texture raised Rwp to about 6.11%, and [001], [101], and [100] axes were poorer. Element-grouped WO3 Uiso values refined to 0.0058 Å² for W and 0.0112 Å² for O. A MnO2 Uiso trial ran to bounds and was rejected.

Observed/calculated peak positions and widths are broadly consistent across the range (for example, low-angle isolated FWHM values agree within about 0.01°), but intensity and width residuals remain around some strong WO3 families and at high-angle overlaps. Atomic coordinates were therefore kept at the physically screened source values rather than over-refined. The final project has no frozen/bound parameters, reopens and replays with maximum shift/esd = 0.0019, and is the exact source of the submitted CIFs, fractions, and profile.
