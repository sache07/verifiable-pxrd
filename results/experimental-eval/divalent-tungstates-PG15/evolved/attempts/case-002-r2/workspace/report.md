# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled as four crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases; no amorphous fraction can be obtained without an internal standard.

The final full-range refinement has Rwp = 7.700%, Rp = 6.051%, and GOF = 2.118 for 8301 points and 25 refined parameters. The quoted fraction uncertainties are formal one-sigma least-squares values. Correlated residuals (Durbin-Watson 0.490), overlap, and the uncalibrated instrumental profile make the true uncertainties—especially for the trace phases—larger.

## Evidence and model choice

The precursor context (BaO2 and WO3, heated to 200 °C) is consistent with the two major phases remaining largely unreacted. Triclinic WO3 reproduces the resolved 23.43/23.92/24.65° cluster and its middle- and high-angle families. Under the same provisional nuisance model, the selected COD 2106293 P-1 record gave Rwp about 8.17%, compared with 9.02% for the best tested monoclinic P21/c record; orthorhombic and older half-cell records were worse. The powder data therefore favor P-1, but do not independently establish every oxygen displacement against the close monoclinic alternative.

Tetragonal BaO2 is supported by coherent families near 26.3, 27.1, 33.6, 43.0, 46.6, 48.0, and 55.9°. The old COD 1010113 oxygen coordinate implied an implausibly short 1.32 Å peroxide bond. Because the X-ray data did not robustly determine this light-atom coordinate, O was fixed to give O-O = 1.49 Å; the refined cell is a = 3.81619 Å and c = 6.85369 Å. Only equatorial size broadening was supported, 0.172(13) µm; axial size and strain were pruned after becoming indeterminate or collapsing to zero.

Trace rocksalt BaO persists through profile rebuilds and is supported by partly overlapped families near 28.58, 33.07, 47.32, 56.08, and 68.99°. Its refined a = 5.461(4) Å. Its approximately 1 wt% amount should not be interpreted to the formal precision shown.

Ba3W2O9 has a positive scale on replay and a distinct residual-supported family at 30.86°, with weaker support near 27.8, 33.1, 43.1, and 53.8°. Some expected weak families conflict with residuals, and releasing its cell was unstable and did not improve the fit. Thus its identification and approximately 0.5 wt% fraction are tentative; the submitted R-3c structure uses the physically sound, fully occupied COD 1533878 model (W-O 1.90–2.23 Å) with its source cell fixed.

BaWO4 was below two formal standard deviations and did not improve the final inventory. Ba3WO6 fitted essentially one residual near 29.5° while worsening expected families near 42.1 and 52.1°, so it was rejected as incoherent. Other Ba-rich tungstates and bronze candidates lacked supported unique families.

## Refinement scope and remaining uncertainty

The Cu Kα1/Kα2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio were fixed from metadata. A six-term smooth background, one zero correction, polarization, and nonnegative U/X profile terms were refined; W was fixed at zero, and asymmetry, roughness, strain, extinction, and unsupported size/texture terms were neutralized. A small WO3 [101] March-Dollase term was replay-stable. Atomic coordinates were otherwise held to screened literature models, while WO3, BaO2, and BaO cells were refined. Consequently, the submitted CIFs are phase models consistent with the powder data, not independent full crystal-structure determinations.
