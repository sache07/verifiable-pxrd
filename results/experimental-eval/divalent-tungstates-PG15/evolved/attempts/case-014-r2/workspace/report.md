# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled by three crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases; amorphous material, if present, is not quantified. The SrCO3 + WO3 precursor context and 900 °C treatment are consistent with dominant SrWO4, residual WO3, and an Sr-rich oxide; the modeled bulk Sr:W ratio is about 1.06. Both tested SrCO3 polymorphs refined to zero scale under the final profile.

## Evidence and model selection

Scheelite SrWO4 matches isolated families throughout the scan, including the peaks near 18.38°, 28.04°, 30.30°, 33.42°, 45.51°, 47.75°, 51.98°, and 56–57°. The starting structure was COD 1546036. Refining its oxygen position gave O = (0.2298(14), 0.1071(11), 0.0443(5)); the resulting W–O distance is 1.756 Å and Sr–O distances are 2.612–2.616 Å.

Monoclinic WO3 (COD 1528915) accounts for the resolved 23.45°/23.98°/24.77° group and consistent families at middle and high angle. Pnma and P21/m records were tested under the same nuisance model and fit materially worse. The exported setting is P21/n, which is setting-equivalent to P21/c for space-group No. 14. Its W–O distances are 1.856–2.144 Å.

No exact Sr2WO5 record was available. A stoichiometric-analogue search was therefore used. The accepted model derives from Ba2ReO5 (COD 1529756) by the isovalent substitutions Ba2+→Sr2+ and Re6+→W6+. Its scale was positive before cell refinement and it explains coherent families near 29.7°, 30.9°, 32.6°, 44.7°, 48.6°, 57.0°, 61.2°, and 67°. The refined geometry is plausible: W–O = 1.753–2.010 Å and principal Sr–O = 2.599–2.908 Å. Alternative Nd2TiO5- and Gd2ReO5-derived prototypes went to zero scale. A single isotropic microstrain term, 1135 microstrain, was retained; adding size was highly correlated and was pruned.

The common baseline uses a smooth 10-coefficient Chebyshev background, one specimen shift (-582 μm for the 200 mm geometry), one low-angle roughness term, and positive W/X profile terms with U fixed at zero. Low-, middle-, and high-angle peak positions, widths, and Kα1/Kα2 splitting replay consistently. Scheelite size/strain, WO3 broadening, polarization refinement, extra roughness, and four tested texture axes were unsupported, unphysical, or non-unique and were removed.

## Remaining uncertainty

The formal covariance errors on the three weight percentages are only about 0.15, 0.09, and 0.15 percentage points. They do not include structural-model, fixed-displacement-parameter, overlap, or background uncertainty. A more realistic interpretation is approximately 66 ± 2 wt% SrWO4, 9 ± 1 wt% WO3, and 25 ± 2 wt% Sr2WO5.

The exact atomic structure of the Sr2WO5 phase cannot be determined reliably from this pattern alone: Pnma and the cell are strongly supported, but its coordinates are inherited from the chemically justified prototype. Its reported fraction is therefore provisional at the few-weight-percent level. WO3 oxygen coordinates were likewise not independently refined. Trace Sr3WO6-, Sr3W2O9-, fergusonite-, or orthorhombic SrO2-like trials borrowed less than 0.7 wt% each mainly from overlapped families; none had sufficient distinctive support to include, so sub-1 wt% trace material cannot be excluded.
