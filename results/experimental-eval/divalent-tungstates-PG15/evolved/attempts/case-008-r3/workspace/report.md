# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

| Modeled crystalline phase | Structural model | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| MnWO4 | wolframite, P 2/c | 0.98032(66), or 98.032(66) wt% |
| WO3 | monoclinic, P 21/c | 0.01968(66), or 1.968(66) wt% |

The fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases; no amorphous fraction can be obtained without an internal standard. The final full-range fit has Rwp = 6.731%, Rp = 4.718%, and GOF = 2.445 using Poisson weights because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied.

## Evidence

The dominant pattern is indexed by wolframite MnWO4. Its refined cell, a = 4.83054, b = 5.75629, c = 4.99572 Å and beta = 91.21765 degrees, accounts for the intense low-, middle-, and high-angle families. COD 1520200 supplied the exact P 2/c topology; although that record was measured at 2.4 GPa, the cell and atomic coordinates were refined here. The resulting octahedra are physically plausible (Mn-O 2.002-2.222 Å; W-O 1.892-2.210 Å).

Monoclinic WO3 is supported by a coherent set of weak, mostly unique or partly overlapped families near 23.44, 24.66, 33.57-34.46, 42.16, 50.21, and 56.20 degrees 2theta. Monoclinic records fit substantially better than triclinic, orthorhombic, or P21/m alternatives. COD 2311041 was selected because its neutron-derived, fully occupied WO6 geometry (W-O 1.715-2.159 Å) is physical; an approximately setting-equivalent older record gave a marginally lower residual but implausible short/long W-O contacts. Because WO3 is only about 2 wt%, its cell and scale were refined but its atomic coordinates were retained from the reference model.

## Refinement checks

The final Bragg-Brentano model uses both reported Cu K-alpha wavelengths and the fixed reported K-alpha2/K-alpha1 ratio of 0.5, a smooth 10-term Chebyshev background, nonnegative common U/W/X profile terms, one specimen-displacement correction, and refined polarization. A small MnWO4 [110] March-Dollase term is replay-stable (1.06555(35)) and is supported relative to symmetry-distinct axes; removing texture raises Rwp to 6.880%. Size, isotropic strain, symmetry-distinct uniaxial strain, SH/L, and minor-phase texture trials were unsupported, hit neutral bounds, or drifted on reopen, so they were pruned. Three final save/reopen refinements reproduced the fractions and Rwp; the last maximum shift/sigma was 0.003 and no parameters were frozen at bounds.

## Remaining uncertainty

MnO and Mn2O3 each produced a formal sub-0.5 wt% scale in isolated trials, but neither was reliable: the result changed with the reference cell, the strongest expected MnO high-angle line had the wrong residual sign, and Mn2O3 was supported mainly by one clean family while comparably strong families were absent or overlapped. Mn3O4 and Mn3WO6 refined to zero. These species were therefore left on the watch list rather than reported as phases. The quoted fraction uncertainties are covariance estimates and are likely optimistic because residuals are correlated (Durbin-Watson 0.405), preferred orientation is present, and there is no calibrated instrumental profile or internal standard. Full occupancy and nominal stoichiometry are assumed; subtle disorder or trace phases below roughly the sub-percent level cannot be determined reliably from this pattern.
