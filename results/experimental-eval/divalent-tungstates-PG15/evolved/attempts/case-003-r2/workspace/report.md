# PXRD phase analysis

The fitted crystalline inventory is WO3, calcite CaCO3, scheelite CaWO4, and trace CaO. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; no amorphous fraction was measured.

The evidence is the coherent recurrence of phase-specific families, not isolated matches. WO3 accounts for the intense 23.42/23.90/24.67 degree triplet and its numerous middle/high-angle families. Scheelite CaWO4 is supported near 18.89, 29.02, 34.46, 47.52, and 54.43 degrees. Calcite is independently marked near 29.71, 35.83, 39.71, 43.46, 48.53, and 64.92 degrees. Trace CaO is supported by a residual family centered near 37.66 degrees, with weaker consistent contributions near 32.5, 54.1, 67.6, and 79.9 degrees. Ca3WO6 refined to the scale lower bound; aragonite and two vaterite models were weak, record-dependent, or zero-scale and were not retained.

The GSAS-II model used the reported Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson counting weights because uncertainties were absent, a smooth six-term Chebyshev background, one zero correction (0.35798 degrees), and a nonnegative common U/W/X profile (U fixed 1.0, W 12.219, X 7.337) with SH/L 0.0392. Unused roughness, transparency, extinction, and phase profile terms were neutralized. Supported phase terms were 33 nm isotropic CaWO4 size, unique-b uniaxial WO3 microstrain, and replay-stable March-Dollase terms for CaWO4 [110] and calcite [104]. The full-range cold replay converged at Rwp = 7.910%, Rp = 6.079%, and GOF = 3.310.

The principal uncertainty is the exact WO3 polymorph. The selected P -1 record (COD 2106293) gives the better full-profile fit and refined alpha/gamma departures from 90 degrees of about six formal standard uncertainties. An ambient monoclinic P21/c neutron model (COD 2311041) remains chemically plausible because measurement temperature was not reported; under the same restrained profile it gave Rwp about 8.21% before the trace-CaO challenge. Thus the WO3 metric assignment and model-dependent fractions are less certain than the formal covariance errors imply. Atomic coordinates were held at the screened database values; oxygen coordinates are not independently determined by this multiphase X-ray pattern. The trace CaO presence is supported by several families, but its 0.32 wt% fraction should be regarded as approximate. Correlated residuals (Durbin-Watson 0.22) and GOF above one likewise show that counting statistics alone understate systematic uncertainty.
