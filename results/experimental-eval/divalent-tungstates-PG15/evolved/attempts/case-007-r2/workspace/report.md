# Quantitative crystalline-phase analysis

## Result

Three crystalline phases are required. Fractions are normalized to the modeled crystalline material only.

The final full-range fit has Rwp=6.739%, Rp=5.262%, Rexp=2.863%, and GOF=2.358 for 8,301 observations and 29 refined variables. Parenthesized fraction uncertainties are formal GSAS-II standard uncertainties, not total accuracy estimates.

## Evidence and structural inventory

The phase assemblage is chemically consistent with the stated MgO + WO3 precursors heated at 900 °C: MgWO4 is the reaction product, with residual WO3 and MgO. Diagnostic families include MgWO4 010/100 at 15.943/19.246° and its 30.820/31.132° pair; the WO3 002/020/200 cluster at 23.452/23.923/24.693°; and the MgO 200, 220, 222, and 400 reflections at 43.219, 62.577, 78.866, and 94.237°. The remaining large residual extrema occur at modeled strong reflections rather than forming a coherent extra peak family, so a fourth crystalline phase is not supported.

The starting structures were physically screened exact-composition records: synthetic ambient-pressure MgWO4 (COD 9013902), neutron WO3 (COD 2311041), and MgO measured at 23 °C and 1 atm (COD 9000499). All modeled sites are fully occupied; no substitution or occupational disorder was introduced. COD 2311041 was transformed from its highly skewed equivalent P21/c basis to the conventional low-skew P21/n setting before refinement. This is a unimodular basis change, not a different structure. Refined geometry remains plausible: MgWO4 has six Mg–O distances of 2.049–2.170 Å and six W–O distances of 1.779–2.130 Å; WO3 has six W–O distances of 1.714–2.159 Å; MgO has six Mg–O distances of 2.1065 Å.

Several physical WO3 alternatives were challenged under a common restrained profile. Orthorhombic, tetragonal, hexagonal, and high-pressure records fit substantially worse. In a preliminary matched comparison, a low-temperature triclinic record lowered Rwp only marginally (7.332% versus 7.355% for the retained monoclinic neutron record), while requiring more reflections/parameters and an unusually large common oxygen Uiso near 0.039 Å². The robust conclusion is WO3; the exact monoclinic-versus-subtly-triclinic distortion is less certain. The ambient monoclinic model is retained on physical parsimony, especially because measurement temperature is not reported in the supplied metadata.

## Refinement and adequacy checks

The final GSAS-II model covers the complete measured 9.9034–100.0949° range. Cu Kα1/Kα2 wavelengths and the reported 0.5 intensity ratio were fixed. Since point uncertainties were absent, counting-statistical weights were estimated from the raw counts. A fixed-grid test selected effective polarization 0.75. The background is an eight-coefficient Chebyshev-1 function that is smooth and positive throughout (about 2,306 counts at the low-angle edge and 253 counts at its minimum).

One specimen-displacement correction refined to -0.5999 mm; zero, transparency, and roughness were fixed neutral. The common nonnegative profile uses U=83.94, W=0 fixed, X=4.134, and SH/L=0.07073. W was fixed at zero after unconstrained trials drove it negative. Low-, middle-, and high-angle isolated families were checked for position, width, and asymmetry; releasing SH/L removed the repeated low-angle asymmetry. A small MgWO4 [110] March–Dollase term, 1.0671(23), is supported by multiple families. MgWO4 isotropic microstrain is 2100(121) µstrain; WO3 requires replay-stable uniaxial strain of 1860(132) µstrain transverse and 10747(567) µstrain along [110]. The latter may represent unresolved microstructural or symmetry-related broadening, so it should not be read literally as a unique defect model. Crystallite-size terms, MgO strain, additional texture, and MgWO4 anisotropic strain were pruned because they were unsupported, nonphysical, or correlated with the common profile.

Cells, phase scales, background, the common profile, specimen displacement, accepted texture/strain terms, and one common WO3 W Uiso were refined. Atomic coordinates were retained from the screened records; WO3 oxygen Uiso was fixed at 0.008 Å² after its common refinement was weak. The project was saved, reopened, and replayed over the full range to a stationary converged solution before CIF and profile export.

## Remaining uncertainty

The formal fraction uncertainties are about 0.09–0.13 weight percentage points, but they omit model uncertainty from the unknown effective polarization/profile, WO3 polymorph choice, fixed coordinates/oxygen displacement parameters, and peak overlap. A more realistic reading is approximately ±1–2 absolute weight percentage points for these crystalline fractions. Site occupancies, subtle oxygen displacements, and a unique physical interpretation of the WO3 anisotropic width cannot be determined reliably from this multiphase powder pattern. No amorphous fraction or material below the crystalline detection limit is quantified, so the reported fractions must not be interpreted as absolute bulk fractions if such material is present.
