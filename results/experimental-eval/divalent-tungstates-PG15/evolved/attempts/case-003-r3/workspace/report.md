## Evidence

Monoclinic WO3 accounts for the characteristic 23.40/23.88/24.64° triplet and the dense 33.5–34.5° and higher-angle families. Calcite is established by 29.69, 36.26, 39.70, and 43.45° families. Scheelite CaWO4 has the distinctive broad 18.91° and 31.70° peaks and coherent higher-angle reflections.

The weak components survive leave-one-phase-out refinements in which every remaining scale and nuisance term was reconverged. Omitting orthorhombic WO3 raises Rwp from 7.591% to 7.944%, with coherent losses near 23.19, 23.73, 24.35, 33.29–34.13, 41.54, and 60.85°. Omitting triclinic WO3 raises Rwp to 7.748%, notably worsening 23.43, 24.66, 34.21, 42.29, 50.24, and 62–64°. Omitting CaO raises Rwp to 7.600%; its 32.50, 37.65, 64.43, 79.92, and 91.72° families improve together.

Ca3WO6 and aragonite trials refined to zero scale. A vaterite trial was insignificant and its cell moved implausibly when released. An alternate ambient monoclinic WO3 record (COD 1528915) gave poorer relative intensities under the same baseline; COD 1563541 was excluded as a high-pressure, half-volume polymorph. Exact-composition records were available, so no substituted analogue was required. All submitted structures have complete occupancy and physically plausible geometry.

## Refinement and fit

GSAS-II used the supplied Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 ratio over nominally 10–100° 2theta, with Poisson weights derived from the raw counts. The accepted baseline has a six-coefficient smooth Chebyshev background, one specimen-displacement correction, refined polarization, and an interior nonnegative U/W/X profile. SH/L asymmetry was rejected because it was 98% correlated with displacement and did not improve the fit. Supported phase terms were monoclinic-WO3 uniaxial strain, orthorhombic-WO3 isotropic strain, scheelite isotropic size, calcite [104] texture, and scheelite [110] texture; unsupported or bound terms were pruned.

Final statistics are Rwp = 7.591%, Rp = 5.912%, Rexp = 2.394%, and GOF = 3.177 for 8,284 observations and 32 refined parameters. The submitted project was saved, reopened, and replayed twice; the second replay had maximum shift/e.s.d. below 0.002. `fit.csv`, the CIFs, and `result.json` were exported from that exact project state.

## Remaining uncertainty

The orthorhombic and triclinic WO3 patterns overlap strongly with monoclinic WO3. Their presence is residual-supported, but the individual polymorph fractions and fitted weak-phase cells are more model-dependent than their formal e.s.d.s; the combined WO3 fraction (59.03 wt%) is more robust than its polymorph split. Measurement temperature was not supplied, so orthorhombic WO3 could represent a quenched/metastable remnant of the reported 500 °C treatment.

CaO is near the practical trace limit. Its coherent families support detection, but 0.278 wt% should be treated as approximate and its real uncertainty may exceed the formal 0.064 wt%. Atomic coordinates were kept at the screened reference values; cells were refined, with the three weak-phase cells fixed at their fitted values for stable replay. No internal standard was supplied, so amorphous content and absolute crystalline yield cannot be determined; the reported fractions apply only to the modeled crystalline phases.
