# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

The pattern is adequately described by two crystalline phases. Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases only.

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction |
|---|---:|---:|
| Monoclinic WO3 | P 21/c | 0.7678 (formal esd 0.0025) |
| Co3O4 spinel | F d -3 m | 0.2322 (formal esd 0.0025) |

The final full-range refinement (9.9034–100.0949 degrees 2theta, 8,301 points) gives Rwp = 3.379%, Rp = 2.476%, Rexp = 1.722%, and goodness of fit = 1.965. A save, reopen, and full-range replay converged with maximum shift/esd = 0.010 and no singular values.

## Evidence

The WO3 assignment is anchored by the resolved 23.46/23.95/24.71 degree triplet and by coherent families throughout the full angular range. Several physically screened WO3 records and polymorphs were compared with the same nuisance model; COD 2311041, a nonstandard-cell setting of room-temperature monoclinic P 21/c WO3, gave the best coherent intensity match. The refined cell is a = 7.69183 A, b = 7.53431 A, c = 10.54297 A, beta = 136.1585 degrees.

Co3O4 is independently supported by its 19.37, 31.64, 37.20, 38.91, 45.14, 59.67, 65.55, and 94.35 degree families. Its refined cubic cell is a = 8.08120 A. The resulting tetrahedral and octahedral Co–O distances are 1.934 and 1.920 A. The refined WO3 model retains six-coordinate W with W–O distances of about 1.74–2.17 A, so neither exported structure contains implausible short contacts.

CoWO4 was specifically tested because it is chemically plausible after heating Co3O4 and WO3 at 500 degrees C. CoO and two W18O49 records were also kept on the watch list. Under the final common background/profile model, every one of these candidates returned reproducibly to the nonnegative scale floor; no coherent unique family justified retention. Their structures or fractions therefore cannot be determined reliably from this pattern, and a zero refined trial scale should be read as “not supported,” not proof of absolute absence.

## Refinement model and uncertainty

The GSAS-II model uses the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and fixed 0.5 intensity ratio, Poisson counting weights because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, a six-term smooth background, one specimen-displacement correction, and a common nonnegative W/X peak-width model. Unused size, roughness, transparency, texture, and extra profile terms are fixed at neutral values. WO3 alone required replay-stable [011] uniaxial microstrain. Cells, phase scales, two W coordinates, and one constrained common W Uiso were refined; the source oxygen positions and the Co3O4 internal coordinates were retained because the powder data did not determine them robustly.

The formal fraction esds are statistical values conditional on this model and are too optimistic as total uncertainties. Plausible structure/profile variants moved the WO3 fraction by roughly two percentage points, and the strongest WO3 peaks retain modest systematic shape/intensity residuals. The defensible composition is therefore about **77 wt% WO3 and 23 wt% Co3O4**, with model-choice uncertainty of order a few wt%. The correlated W-coordinate shifts (the two W y shifts correlate at about 96%) should not be interpreted as a high-precision independent structure determination. No external standard or calibrated instrumental profile was available, and amorphous content cannot be quantified; the reported fractions do not represent absolute whole-sample fractions if amorphous material is present.
