# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

The pattern is best described by four crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized to the modeled crystalline material only.

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, °) | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| PbWO₄, scheelite | *I*4₁/*a* | a = 5.46702, c = 12.06536 | 67.88(26)% |
| PbO, massicot | *Pbcm* | a = 5.89992, b = 5.49206, c = 4.76023 | 7.24(13)% |
| WO₃, monoclinic | *P*2₁/*c* | a = 7.70625, b = 7.54810, c = 10.58261, β = 136.3297 | 10.36(21)% |
| Pb₂WO₅ | *C*2/*m* | a = 14.23486, b = 5.81284, c = 7.36451, β = 113.8863 | 14.52(23)% |

The parenthetical uncertainties are formal GSAS-II covariance standard uncertainties in percentage points. They describe counting-statistical precision under the adopted model and are smaller than the likely systematic uncertainty.

The final full-range refinement converged with Rwp = 12.571%, Rp = 8.573%, Rexp = 3.492%, goodness of fit = 3.600, and 44 refined parameters for 8,282 observations. The large goodness of fit reflects both visible profile/background mismatch and the use of Poisson weights estimated from counts because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied.

## Evidence and refinement

The dominant peak sequence is that of tetragonal scheelite PbWO₄. Distinct subsidiary features support each minor phase: the monoclinic WO₃ cluster near 23.4–24.7° 2θ, massicot PbO lines near 15.3°, 29.3°, 30.6°, and 38.1°, and a separate coherent series near 25.9°, 30.2°, 31.1°, and at higher angles matching monoclinic Pb₂WO₅. This assemblage is chemically consistent with incomplete reaction of PbO and WO₃ at the reported 500 °C, with PbWO₄ and Pb₂WO₅ products plus residual oxides.

Diagnostic phase-removal refinements worsened Rwp from about 12.7% to 14.2% without WO₃, 14.6% without PbO, and 15.1% without Pb₂WO₅. A trial raspite-PbWO₄ phase refined to effectively zero scale, and the remaining residual does not provide a robust coherent fifth-phase pattern.

Starting structures were COD 9009811 (PbWO₄), COD 2310433 (PbO), and COD 2311041 (WO₃). The WO₃ CIF uses a valid nonconventional *P*2₁/*c* cell setting. No Pb₂WO₅ entry was available in the local screened structure set, so its model was constructed from the isostructural *C*2/*m* Pb₂MoO₅ prototype (COD 7220560) by replacing Mo with W. Its lattice parameters, scale, and diffraction profile then refined against the data.

The GSAS-II model used the reported Cu Kα₁/Kα₂ wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio. Refined terms included phase scales and cells, a 16-term Chebyshev background, zero offset, global pseudo-Voigt width terms, axial divergence, and effective polarization. The dominant PbWO₄ oxygen position was also refined. Occupancies and displacement parameters were fixed, as were minor-phase atomic coordinates, because the overlapping laboratory data did not determine them stably.

## Remaining uncertainty

The four-phase identification is well supported, but detailed atomic coordinates for Pb₂WO₅ are not independently established by this pattern; its submitted CIF should be treated as an isostructural working model rather than a high-precision structure determination. Minor-phase fractions may also shift through correlated background/profile errors, preferred orientation, or microabsorption. A practical interpretation is that systematic fraction uncertainty is on the order of one to a few absolute percentage points, larger than the formal values in the table.

No internal standard was present, so amorphous material, if any, cannot be measured. The reported fractions therefore sum to 100% over the four modeled crystalline phases and are not absolute whole-sample fractions.
