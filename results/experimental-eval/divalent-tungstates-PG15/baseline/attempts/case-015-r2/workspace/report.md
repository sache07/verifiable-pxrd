# PXRD phase analysis

## Result

The modeled crystalline material contains three phases:

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, °) | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| ZnWO4, wolframite | P 2/c | a = 4.69070, b = 5.71856, c = 4.92676, beta = 90.62798 | 0.7544 (75.44 wt%) |
| WO3, gamma-monoclinic | P 21/c | a = 7.30368, b = 7.53787, c = 7.69058, beta = 90.77910 | 0.1729 (17.29 wt%) |
| ZnO, wurtzite | P 63mc | a = 3.24937, c = 5.20876 | 0.0727 (7.27 wt%) |

The fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases; they do not measure any amorphous content. The final GSAS-II fit gives Rwp = 11.245%, Rp = 8.633%, Rexp = 3.796%, and GOF = 2.970 over 10–100° 2theta.

## Evidence

The dominant P 2/c ZnWO4 model accounts for the characteristic sequence near 18.98, 23.87, 24.62, 30.69, 36.50, 41.39, 51.86, 61.90, and 64.9–65.6° 2theta. A one-phase fit left a coherent WO3 series, including the split 23–24° and 33–34° groups. Full-pattern candidate tests favored gamma-monoclinic P 21/c WO3 (fixed-cell Rwp about 13.6%) over P 21/m (15.3%), Pnma (16.3%), and tetragonal models (about 17.5%). After adding WO3, residual peaks at approximately 31.9, 34.4, 47.6, 56.7, and 63.0° formed the wurtzite ZnO series; adding ZnO lowered Rwp from 11.83% to 11.28% before final joint refinement.

The refinement used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, a 16-term smooth Chebyshev background, specimen displacement, and a constrained positive-width profile. All three cells and phase scales were refined. ZnWO4 coordinates were refined and retain plausible octahedral distances; minor-phase coordinates and all displacement parameters were held to their starting structural values because the overlapping powder data do not determine them independently.

## Uncertainty

Formal covariance standard uncertainties on the fractions are 0.24, 0.16, and 0.21 wt% for ZnWO4, WO3, and ZnO, respectively, but these are optimistic because pointwise intensity uncertainties and a calibrated instrument profile were unavailable. Background/profile alternatives move the fractions by roughly 1–2 wt%, which is the more realistic systematic precision. The exact oxygen arrangement within the minor monoclinic WO3 phase cannot be refined reliably from this pattern, though the P 21/c phase assignment is favored clearly. Trace phases below roughly 1–2 wt% and amorphous material cannot be excluded.

The profile, CIFs, fractions, and statistics were exported from `final.gpx`.
