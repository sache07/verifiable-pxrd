# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline model contains 34.8 wt% BaO₂, 61.5 wt% WO₃, and 3.7 wt% BaCO₃, normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. The final GSAS-II fit gives Rwp = 7.10%, Rp = 5.38%, Rexp = 3.64%, and goodness of fit = 1.95 over 9.903–100.095° 2θ.

## Evidence

Tetragonal BaO₂ (I4/mmm) accounts for the characteristic reflections at approximately 26.32, 27.05, 42.97, 46.64, and 55.84° 2θ. Low-symmetry WO₃ accounts for the intense 23.43/23.92/24.65° triplet, the split 33–35° group, and the remaining broad higher-angle features. Under an otherwise identical two-phase protocol, the triclinic P-1 WO₃ model fit better than the closely related monoclinic model (Rwp 7.82% versus 8.60% before the final profile treatment), so the triclinic model was retained.

Weak residual intensity at the expected orthorhombic witherite positions near 24.2–24.6, 34–35, and 42–45° supports minor BaCO₃. Adding BaCO₃ lowered Rwp from 7.53% to 7.10%. Its refined cell remained close to the reference structure. By contrast, trial BaWO₄ and Ba(OH)₂ scales converged to the numerical lower bound. In particular, no convincing BaWO₄ diagnostic reflection near 31.9° is present, so no crystalline tungstate product is reported.

The Cu Kα₁/Kα₂ wavelengths and reported 0.5 intensity ratio were fixed from the metadata. Original counts were fitted with Poisson weights (1/I) because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied. A smooth 18-term Chebyshev background, phase scales and cells, isotropic crystallite-size/microstrain terms for the major phases, and a 2θ zero correction were refined. Atomic coordinates were retained from COD 1010113 (BaO₂), 2106293 (WO₃), and 9010928 (BaCO₃); the powder data do not support reliable independent coordinate refinement. The minor carbonate cell/profile was estimated in sensitivity fits and then fixed for the final scale refinement to avoid an unstable width/scale correlation.

## Remaining uncertainty

The formal fraction ESDs are about 0.1–0.2 wt%, but they understate model uncertainty because no calibrated instrumental profile or pointwise uncertainties were supplied, the peaks are strongly broadened and overlapped, and Ba/W phases may be affected by microabsorption. Alternative reasonable carbonate broadening models gave 3.2–3.8 wt% BaCO₃. The selected triclinic WO₃ distortion is favored but is not uniquely established over a closely related monoclinic description. Fractions are crystalline-normalized; amorphous material, if present, is not quantified.
