# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline product is predominantly triclinic copper(II) tungstate, CuWO4 (space group P -1), with a small amount of room-temperature monoclinic WO3 (space group P 21/n, equivalent standard setting P2_1/c).

| Phase | Refined cell (A, degrees) | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| CuWO4 | a = 4.71105, b = 5.84552, c = 4.88386; alpha = 91.6555, beta = 92.5351, gamma = 82.7487 | 97.852(63)% |
| WO3 | a = 7.30361, b = 7.54638, c = 7.68932; beta = 90.8634 | 2.148(63)% |

Fractions are normalized over these two modeled crystalline phases; they do not measure any amorphous content. Parentheses give the formal one-sigma scale/covariance uncertainty from GSAS-II.

## Evidence

The CuWO4 model accounts for the complete dominant peak sequence, including the reflections near 15.61, 19.32, 23.24-24.43, 26.27, 28.98, 30.44, 31.94, 32.44, and 37.16 degrees 2theta. Its refined cell agrees with the ambient triclinic wolframite-type cell. A single-phase screening refinement left coherent positive residuals at the monoclinic WO3 positions, especially near 23.44, 24.68, 33.56, 33.89, 34.45, 41.73, and 42.18 degrees. Adding WO3 refined to the physically expected ambient cell and reduced Rwp from 8.09% to 7.55% in the fixed-coordinate screening comparison. A CuO trial instead drove its scale to the nonnegative lower bound, so CuO was not retained.

The final Rietveld refinement used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and fixed 0.5 intensity ratio, a refined zero offset and empirical profile coefficients, a 16-term Chebyshev background, phase cells and scales, and the Cu position in CuWO4. The final statistics are Rwp = 7.377%, Rp = 5.357%, Rexp = 2.433%, and goodness of fit = 3.038 over 8,301 points. Because point uncertainties were not supplied, sigma = sqrt(counts) was used.

## Remaining uncertainty

CuWO4 oxygen coordinates and all atomic coordinates of the 2.1 wt% WO3 phase cannot be determined reliably from this laboratory X-ray pattern; they were held to reference structures (COD 4000806 and 1528915). Only the Cu site in CuWO4 was released. The formal phase-fraction uncertainty (0.063 wt%) does not include background, reference-structure, preferred-orientation, microabsorption, or uncalibrated-profile systematics. Model variants changed the WO3 result by roughly 0.2 wt%, which is a more realistic indication of its absolute uncertainty. Crystalline phases below the practical detection limit, and amorphous material if present, cannot be excluded or quantified from this refinement.
