# Powder diffraction phase analysis

## Result

Two crystalline phases were required. The crystalline-only quantitative result is **triclinic WO3 (P -1): 67.20 wt%** and **BaO2 (I 4/mmm): 32.80 wt%**. The formal covariance-matrix standard uncertainty is 0.12 percentage point for each normalized fraction; model-dependent uncertainty is larger (about 2 percentage points).

The refined WO3 cell is a = 7.32107 A, b = 7.53846 A, c = 7.69686 A, alpha = 89.4961 deg, beta = 90.7051 deg, gamma = 90.2436 deg (P -1). The refined BaO2 cell is a = 3.81502 A and c = 6.85314 A (I 4/mmm).

## Evidence

The resolved WO3 triplet at approximately 23.43, 23.92 and 24.65 deg 2theta and its split 33--35 deg group match low-symmetry WO3. A triclinic model (COD 2106293) fit the resolved splitting materially better than the monoclinic alternatives tested. BaO2 is identified by the paired 26.3/27.1 deg reflections and the consistent 43.0, 46.6, 48.0 and 55--56 deg series. No separate unmodeled peak family remains, and the diagnostic BaWO4 reflections (especially near 31.8 deg) are absent.

The final GSAS-II Rietveld fit has Rwp = 7.570%, Rp = 5.711%, Rexp = 3.641% and goodness of fit = 2.079. Cu K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and their reported 0.5 ratio were fixed from metadata. Refined terms were the phase scales and cells, a common zero offset, two common Lorentzian profile terms, a 15-term Chebyshev background, and the symmetry-allowed BaO2 oxygen z coordinate; other atomic coordinates were held to reference values. With no supplied point uncertainties, GSAS-II used counting-statistical weights (1/I).

## Uncertainty and limits

Fractions are normalized only over the two modeled crystalline phases; amorphous content and absolute sample composition cannot be obtained from this scan. The triclinic WO3 model is preferred by the resolved splitting and fit quality, but its metric is close to monoclinic and the broad peaks do not make that polymorph distinction absolute. The peroxide lattice and intensities are compatible with BaO2/BaO2-x, but Cu X-ray contrast beside Ba is insufficient to determine oxygen-vacancy concentration reliably, so the submitted model uses nominal full-occupancy BaO2. Plausible peroxide structural templates shift the phase result by roughly 1--2 wt%, which dominates the formal scale-factor uncertainty. Remaining residuals are concentrated around the strongest overlapping WO3 peaks and are attributable mainly to imperfect line-shape/structure modeling rather than a resolved third phase.
