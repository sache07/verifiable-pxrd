# Phase analysis and quantitative refinement

The pattern is described by two residual precursor phases: triclinic WO3 and rutile-type beta-MnO2 (pyrolusite). No crystalline manganese tungstate reaction product is required at the reported 300 °C heating temperature.

The fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. Their least-squares standard uncertainties are 0.0018 in weight fraction (0.18 percentage point), but a more realistic uncertainty is about ±3 percentage points because no internal standard, calibrated instrument profile, or microabsorption correction was available.

## Evidence

WO3 accounts for the resolved cluster at 23.44, 23.93, and 24.69° 2theta and the strong lines near 34.48, 50.23, and 56.21°. Beta-MnO2 accounts for the independent lines near 29.01, 37.68, 56.96, 59.66, and 72.53°. A test MnWO4 phase refined to 0.047(54) wt%, statistically zero, and its diagnostic low-angle lines were absent; it was therefore excluded from the submitted model.

GSAS-II refinement used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio over the full 9.903–100.095° range. With Poisson uncertainties assumed from the counts, the converged fit has Rwp=6.197%, Rp=4.533%, Rexp=2.693%, and goodness of fit=2.305. A mild [001] March-Dollase correction for MnO2 and phase-specific WO3 broadening were included.

## Remaining uncertainty

The WO3 formula and phase family are secure, but its exact low-symmetry assignment is less certain. The triclinic model gave a reproducibly better full-profile fit than tested monoclinic WO3 models, while the refined cell remains close to pseudo-monoclinic. The measurement temperature and an external angular standard were not supplied, so a monoclinic/triclinic distinction near the WO3 transition should be treated cautiously. Atomic coordinates and displacement parameters were retained from the cited database structures; only lattice metrics and profile/quantitative terms were refined.

The reported fractions do not include any amorphous material. The strong absorption contrast between W- and Mn-bearing phases, possible particle-size effects, and the absence of supplied pointwise uncertainties make the formal fraction errors optimistic.
