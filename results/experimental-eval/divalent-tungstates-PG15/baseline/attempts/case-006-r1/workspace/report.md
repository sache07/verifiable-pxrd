# Phase analysis

The modeled crystalline product is **97.67 wt% CuWO4** (triclinic, P -1) and **2.33 wt% WO3** (room-temperature monoclinic, P 21/n), normalized to the two crystalline phases. Formal covariance uncertainties are 0.09 wt% for each complementary fraction.

## Evidence

The CuWO4 model accounts for the dominant peak sequence at approximately 15.61, 19.32, 23.24, 23.88, 24.43, 26.27, 28.98, 30.44, 31.94, and 32.43 degrees 2-theta and the higher-angle pattern. A CuWO4-only refinement gave Rwp = 7.05%. Monoclinic WO3 explains an independent reflection at 33.56 degrees and related intensity near 33.9, 34.5, 50.2, and 56.2 degrees; adding it lowered Rwp to 6.48% and produced a nonzero scale well above its formal uncertainty. Alternative WO3 polymorphs fitted worse. CuO refined to zero, and Cu2O/Cu3WO6 challenge fits were below reliable detection and did not justify additional phases.

The final Cu K-alpha Rietveld refinement used the reported K-alpha1/K-alpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio over 10-100 degrees 2-theta. Final statistics are Rp = 4.67%, Rwp = 6.48%, Rexp = 2.43%, and goodness of fit = 2.67.

## Structural refinement and uncertainty

For CuWO4, the cell, atomic coordinates, Cu/W isotropic displacement parameters, and a common oxygen displacement parameter were refined. The refined cell is a = 4.70787, b = 5.84209, c = 4.88098 A; alpha = 91.6553, beta = 92.5338, gamma = 82.7492 degrees. For the 2.3 wt% WO3 phase, the cell and a common W displacement parameter were refined, but the literature atomic coordinates and fixed O displacement parameter were retained because this minor phase does not support reliable coordinate refinement. Its refined cell is a = 7.29998, b = 7.54032, c = 7.68571 A, beta = 90.8754 degrees.

The stated fraction uncertainties are statistical only. Peak overlap, the absence of a calibrated instrumental profile, and the constrained minor-phase model make a realistic WO3 uncertainty a few tenths of a weight percent. Absolute lattice uncertainties also have unmodeled systematic contributions from the large refined specimen displacement. Amorphous content cannot be assessed without an internal standard; no additional crystalline phase is reliable at roughly the 0.3 wt% conditional detection level for CuO.
