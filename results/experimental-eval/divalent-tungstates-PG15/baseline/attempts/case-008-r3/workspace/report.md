# PXRD phase analysis

The modeled crystalline product contains two phases:

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, °) | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| MnWO4, wolframite | P 2/c (No. 13) | a = 4.83632, b = 5.76338, c = 5.00198, β = 88.7818 | 98.03(7)% |
| WO3, ambient monoclinic | P 21/n (No. 14) | a = 7.30903, b = 7.55020, c = 7.68421, β = 90.7918 | 1.97(7)% |

Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases only. Parentheses reflect the formal GSAS-II scale-factor covariance (one standard uncertainty); they do not include model-choice or systematic uncertainty.

## Evidence

The dominant reflection sequence at 15.706, 18.684, 23.889, 24.356, 30.115/30.582, and 36.265/36.624° 2θ, including the characteristic monoclinic splitting, indexes as wolframite MnWO4 and is chemically consistent with reaction of MnO and WO3 at 900 °C. A refined +0.3498° zero offset was required. The submitted MnWO4 setting has β < 90°; reversing the c axis gives the equivalent conventional β = 91.2182° setting.

A MnWO4-only refinement gave Rwp = 7.065%. Coherent residual peaks at approximately 23.48, 24.69, 33.60, and 34.47° match ambient monoclinic WO3; adding and refining this phase reduced Rwp to 6.735%. Hexagonal and tetragonal WO3 models refined to effectively zero, while orthorhombic WO3 gave a substantially poorer fit. This supports residual unreacted monoclinic WO3 rather than a high-temperature polymorph.

The final fit has Rp = 4.738%, Rwp = 6.735%, Rexp = 2.759%, and goodness of fit = 2.447 over 9.903–100.095° 2θ. The refinement converged with no Hessian singularities.

## Remaining uncertainty

Pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, so Poisson counting weights were assumed. The goodness of fit above unity and structured residuals around the strongest asymmetric peaks show that the formal fraction uncertainty is optimistic. With no calibrated instrumental profile, instrumental broadening cannot be separated reliably from crystallite size or microstrain; no physical size/strain result is claimed.

MnO, Mn2O3, Mn3O4, and Mn3WO6 were tested after including WO3. Mn3O4 and Mn3WO6 refined to zero. MnO and Mn2O3 each absorbed only about 0.2 wt% of the same small systematic residual and changed Rwp by less than 0.003 percentage point, so neither identity nor fraction is reliable and neither is included. A practical bound for an additional well-crystallized oxide is therefore roughly a few tenths of a weight percent, but it is phase- and overlap-dependent.

The MnWO4 cell and symmetry are well determined; its symmetry-allowed coordinates were refined while occupancies and displacement parameters were fixed. At only ~2 wt%, the WO3 scale and monoclinic metric are supported, but its atomic coordinates/displacement parameters and any very subtle monoclinic-versus-near-triclinic distortion cannot be determined independently from this pattern. The submitted WO3 CIF therefore retains literature ambient-monoclinic coordinates.
