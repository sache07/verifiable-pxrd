# Phase analysis and refinement report

The pattern is best described by three crystalline phases. Weight fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases.

| Phase | Refined cell | Weight fraction |
|---|---|---:|
| Scheelite SrWO4, *I* 41/*a* | *a* = 5.41853 Å, *c* = 11.95995 Å | 78.55 wt% |
| Monoclinic WO3, *P* 21/*n* | *a* = 7.30419 Å, *b* = 7.54099 Å, *c* = 7.69033 Å, beta = 90.7958° | 10.89 wt% |
| Monoclinic Sr3W2O9 model, *C* 2/*c* | *a* = 9.63254 Å, *b* = 5.53386 Å, *c* = 13.45793 Å, beta = 103.4191° | 10.56 wt% |

The phase assignments used COD starting models 1546036 (SrWO4), 1528915 (WO3), and 2311977 (Sr3W2O9). SrWO4 is established by its complete scheelite sequence, including the strong peaks near 18.32, 28.00, 30.22, 33.39, 45.44, 47.75, 51.98, and 56.08° 2theta. Monoclinic WO3 is identified independently by the diagnostic 23.45/23.97/24.73° triplet and its higher-angle partners. After fitting those phases, the remaining peak sequence at about 29.75, 32.63, 44.67, 53.44, 61.28, 75.68, and 93.32° indexes coherently with the refined Sr3W2O9 cell. Adding that phase reduced Rwp from 17.77% for SrWO4 + WO3 to 15.29% before texture and instrumental-intensity corrections.

The final GSAS-II fit used the reported Cu K-alpha doublet and intensity ratio, a 15-term Chebyshev background, refined cells and phase scales, and an empirical sample displacement. Because no calibrated profile or effective polarization was supplied, polarization was fixed at its physical upper bound after an unconstrained trial exceeded it. A Gaussian Caglioti profile was used with zero Lorentzian contribution; its variance remains positive throughout the fitted range. Strong preferred orientation in the Sr3W2O9-like phase required a March-Dollase correction along [2 0 -4] (ratio 0.559). Final statistics are Rwp = 12.894%, Rp = 9.136%, Rexp = 3.538%, and GOF = 3.652 over 10.1–99.9° 2theta.

The first two phase identities are reliable. The Sr3W2O9 lattice match is strong, but its exact atomic structure is less secure: the reference model was reported for a high-pressure product, its phase R factors remain high, and its strongest reflection is texture sensitive. A related Sr–W–O structure with the same or a closely related lattice cannot be excluded. Consequently, the 10.56 wt% value for this phase is model dependent. The covariance-only fraction uncertainties (about 0.18–0.25 wt% absolute) do not include profile, polarization, texture, or structural-model uncertainty; a practical systematic uncertainty of several weight-percentage points is more realistic. Residual intensity near 22.09 and 30.89° and the GOF above unity likewise show that the detailed intensity model is imperfect. No amorphous fraction can be obtained from these data without an internal standard.
