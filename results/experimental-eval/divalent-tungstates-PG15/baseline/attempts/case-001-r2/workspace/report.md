# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by two reliably quantifiable crystalline phases:

| Phase | Structural model | Weight fraction* |
|---|---|---:|
| Witherite, BaCO3 | orthorhombic Pmcn (No. 62; equivalent Pnma setting) | 54.34 wt% |
| Tungsten(VI) oxide, WO3 | triclinic P-1 (No. 2) | 45.66 wt% |

\*Normalized over the two modeled crystalline phases. The least-squares formal uncertainty is 0.13 wt% for each fraction, but a more realistic uncertainty is about 1–2 wt% because no calibrated instrumental profile or measured pointwise uncertainties were supplied. Amorphous material, if present, is not quantified.

## Evidence and refinement

The witherite model accounts for its characteristic reflections near 19.77, 24.21, 34.44, 42.29, 45.18, and 47.08 degrees 2theta. WO3 accounts for the 23.43/23.90/24.66 degree triplet, the split 33.57–34.89 degree group, and the repeated higher-angle peaks including those near 50.21 and 56.21 degrees. The detailed WO3 splitting favors triclinic P-1 over the closely related monoclinic P21/c model: otherwise comparable refinements gave Rwp = 7.397% and 7.461%, respectively. That model choice changes the phase fractions by about 1 wt%, which is included in the practical uncertainty above.

GSAS-II refinement used the supplied Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and intensity ratio, Poisson counting estimates because uncertainties were absent, a 15-term Chebyshev background, refined phase scales and major-phase unit cells, and refined zero, polarization, and W/X profile terms. A modest March-Dollase correction was refined for BaCO3 along [110] (ratio 0.904); WO3 did not support a preferred-orientation correction. Atomic coordinates were retained from the reference structures because unconstrained coordinate refinement was not justified by this multiphase laboratory pattern. The final fit has Rp = 5.464%, Rwp = 7.197%, Rexp = 3.481%, and goodness of fit = 2.071 over 8,301 points. The refinement converged with maximum shift/esd = 0.126.

The refined cells are a = 5.31159, b = 8.91981, c = 6.43685 A for BaCO3 and a = 7.31162, b = 7.54207, c = 7.69799 A, alpha = 89.8595, beta = 90.7703, gamma = 90.0333 degrees for WO3.

## Remaining uncertainty

Scheelite BaWO4 is chemically plausible after heating BaCO3 with WO3 at 400 degrees C, but it cannot be established reliably here. A conventional I41/a structure refined to a fraction statistically indistinguishable from zero, while a different historical split-oxygen model absorbed residual intensity at roughly 0.6 wt%. Because this sub-1 wt% estimate is structure-model dependent and lacks a distinct unoverlapped peak family, BaWO4 is not included in the submitted phase list or normalization. No other tested Ba-W-O candidate produced a convincing independent peak family. Residual structure around the strongest overlapped peaks is most consistent with profile, texture, and reference-intensity limitations rather than a reliably identifiable additional phase.
