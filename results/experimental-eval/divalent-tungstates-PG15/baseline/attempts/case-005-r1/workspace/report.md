# Powder diffraction phase analysis

## Result

The crystalline pattern is modeled by two residual precursor phases:

- monoclinic gamma-WO3, space group P 21/n: **72.52 wt%** (formal 1-sigma 0.30 wt%);
- cubic Co3O4 spinel, space group F d -3 m: **27.48 wt%** (formal 1-sigma 0.30 wt%).

Fractions are normalized only over these modeled crystalline phases. No amorphous fraction was measured.

## Evidence and refinement

The WO3 assignment is fixed by the resolved low-angle triplet at 23.465, 23.954 and 24.714 degrees 2theta and its matching higher-angle multiplets. The Co3O4 assignment is independently supported by its 220/311/400/511/440 series near 31.636, 37.200, 45.143, 59.672 and 65.551 degrees. A specimen-displacement correction accounts coherently for the positive, angle-dependent offset of both phases. The refined cells are a=7.30303, b=7.53409, c=7.69145 A, beta=90.6903 degrees for gamma-WO3, and a=8.08114 A for Co3O4.

Whole-pattern Rietveld refinement used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio. Refined quantities were phase scales, lattice metrics, a reflection-geometry specimen displacement, global profile terms, a smooth 20-term background, and one symmetry-constrained isotropic displacement parameter for Co3O4. Atomic coordinates were retained from COD 2311041 (WO3, transformed to the near-orthogonal P 21/n setting) and COD 9005896 (Co3O4), because positional refinement was correlated and not reliable from this mixture pattern. The final fit has Rp=2.403%, Rwp=3.361%, Rexp=1.722% and goodness-of-fit=1.955.

CoWO4 was specifically tested because it is the expected reaction product. Its strongest diagnostic reflection would occur near 30.9 degrees after the fitted displacement, where no peak is present; adding a CoWO4 phase drove its scale to the lower bound. A triclinic WO3 alternative also fit worse and refined toward the monoclinic metric. Thus neither is included in the submitted phase list.

## Remaining uncertainty

The formal scale-factor uncertainties are about 0.30 wt%, but they understate model uncertainty. The instrument profile was not calibrated, thermal parameters are weakly constrained, and structured residuals remain around some overlapping WO3 peaks. A realistic fraction uncertainty is about +/-2 wt% absolute. Trace additional phases below a few weight percent cannot be excluded. The oxygen coordinates and a fully independent WO3 displacement model cannot be determined reliably from these data and were therefore not refined.
