# PXRD phase analysis

The crystalline product is dominated by wolframite-type MnWO4 (space group P 1 2/c 1), with tentative sub-1 wt% traces of cubic Mn2O3, monoclinic WO3, and tetragonal Mn3O4. The crystalline-normalized result is:

| Phase | Weight fraction | Formal s.u. | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| MnWO4 | 98.308 wt% | 0.247 wt% | secure |
| Mn2O3 | 0.671 wt% | 0.119 wt% | tentative trace |
| WO3 | 0.339 wt% | 0.078 wt% | tentative trace; least secure |
| Mn3O4 | 0.682 wt% | 0.191 wt% | tentative trace |

## Evidence and refinement

All prominent reflections, including the diagnostic doublets near 23.8/24.3 and 30.1/30.5 degrees 2theta, are indexed by MnWO4. Alternative Mn3WO6 predicts strong peaks near 20 and 29 degrees that are absent. The refined MnWO4 cell is a = 4.830709(85) A, b = 5.757026(95) A, c = 4.996246(86) A, beta = 91.21777(112) degrees, and V = 138.91681 A^3. Its cell and symmetry-constrained atomic coordinates were refined.

The trace phases give positive scales and reduce chi-square from 18988.3 (one phase, 35 variables) to 18856.1 (four phases, 38 variables) with the same profile/background model. Their strongest diagnostic regions are consistent with weak residual intensity (notably Mn2O3 near 33.2 degrees and Mn3O4 near 36.3/60.0 degrees). WO3 relies mostly on overlapped low-angle peaks, so that assignment is the least secure.

The final GSAS-II fit over 10.001-100.008 degrees has Rwp = 5.225%, Rp = 3.863%, and goodness of fit = 1.512. Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and the reported 0.5 intensity ratio were fixed; background, sample displacement/surface roughness, MnWO4 cell/coordinates, phase scales, and Gaussian/Lorentzian profile terms were refined.

## Remaining uncertainty

No calibrated instrument profile or pointwise uncertainties were supplied, so Poisson weights were assumed. The trace fractions are model-sensitive: plausible profile/background variants placed Mn2O3 at about 0.3-0.7 wt%, Mn3O4 at about 0.4-0.8 wt%, and WO3 at about 0.3-0.5 wt%. These systematic ranges exceed the formal least-squares s.u. values in the table. There is not enough independent intensity to refine trace-phase cells or atomic positions reliably; their reference structures were held fixed. The reported fractions are normalized only over modeled crystalline phases and do not quantify amorphous content.
