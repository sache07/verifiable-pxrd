# Phase analysis report

## Result

The best three-phase model, normalized over the modeled crystalline material, is:

| Phase | Space group | Weight fraction | Assessment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Scheelite SrWO4 | I 41/a | 0.7695 (76.95 wt%) | Secure |
| Monoclinic WO3 | P 21/n | 0.1100 (11.00 wt%) | Secure |
| Fergusonite-type SrWO4 | I 2/a | 0.1206 (12.06 wt%) | Provisional |

The GSAS-II formal one-sigma errors are 0.28, 0.22, and 0.22 weight-percentage points, respectively. These are counting-statistical errors only and are smaller than the realistic model uncertainty. A more defensible practical reading is approximately 77 +/- 3, 11 +/- 2, and 12 +/- 3 wt%. No amorphous fraction can be obtained without an internal standard.

## Evidence

Scheelite SrWO4 accounts for the dominant pattern, including the peak series near 18.33, 27.99, 30.21, 33.39, 38.20, 45.47, 47.75, 51.98, 54.13, 56.08, and 57.34 degrees 2theta. The refined tetragonal cell is a = 5.41835 A and c = 11.95964 A.

Monoclinic WO3 is independently identified by its characteristic low-angle triplet near 23.46, 23.97, and 24.73 degrees and by matching higher-angle multiplets. Its refined cell is a = 7.30137 A, b = 7.54014 A, c = 7.69062 A, beta = 90.7943 degrees. This is consistent with unreacted WO3 in the SrCO3 + WO3 synthesis context.

The remaining coherent peak family, notably near 29.77, 32.61, 44.67, 48.61, 50.55, 59.56, and 61.28 degrees, is best indexed by the monoclinic fergusonite-type SrWO4 model. Adding it lowers Rwp from about 18.85% for the two-phase scheelite-SrWO4 + WO3 model to 16.47%. A search across the available chemically plausible Sr-W-O, Sr-O, W-O, and Sr carbonate structures did not find a better match to this residual peak sequence.

The fergusonite-type assignment remains provisional. Its reference structure (COD 1546037) is a pressure-stabilized polymorph, while the supplied metadata reports heating at 900 C but no pressure treatment. The refined cell (a = 5.14213 A, b = 11.10179 A, c = 5.10523 A, beta = 90.2414 degrees, V = 291.44 A3) is also strongly contracted relative to the reference. Thus the diffraction evidence supports a fergusonite-like structural motif, but the exact composition/polymorph and its fraction cannot be claimed with the same reliability as the two major assignments. Reasonable profile/orientation variants moved this component between roughly 12 and 14 wt%.

## Refinement and limitations

The final fit used the supplied Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio over 10.05-99.95 degrees 2theta. Phase scales, unit cells, an 18-term smooth background, a reflection-geometry specimen displacement, and an empirical U/V/W peak profile were refined. Atomic coordinates were retained from the reference structures because this overlapped laboratory pattern does not support stable independent coordinate refinement. The profile terms were fixed for the last scale/cell cycle because no calibrated instrumental profile was supplied.

Final statistics are Rp = 12.25%, Rwp = 16.47%, Rexp = 3.54%, and goodness of fit = 4.66. Point uncertainties were not supplied, so sigma = sqrt(counts) was used. The elevated goodness of fit and residual intensity mismatch show that the formal errors underestimate systematic uncertainty from peak overlap, the uncalibrated profile, specimen displacement, and the provisional third-phase model. No additional crystalline phase could be established reliably from the remaining residual.
