# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is best described by two crystalline phases:

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| monoclinic γ-WO₃ | P 2₁/n | a = 7.30487 Å, b = 7.53627 Å, c = 7.69025 Å, β = 90.7904° | 69.59 wt% |
| wolframite-type CoWO₄ | P 2/c | a = 4.66750 Å, b = 5.68334 Å, c = 4.95011 Å, β = 89.9666° | 30.41 wt% |

The γ-WO₃ assignment is supported by its diagnostic 23.18/23.67/24.42° triplet and the associated lines at 26.66°, 28.68/28.91°, 33.35/33.58°, 34.22°, 47.26°, 50.01°, and 55.98°. CoWO₄ accounts for the independent reflections at 19.09°, 30.71°, 36.44°, 38.62°, and its higher-angle line set. This is chemically consistent with partial reaction of the reported CoO + WO₃ precursors at 900 °C, leaving excess/unreacted WO₃.

The submitted GSAS-II Rietveld refinement used the full 9.903–100.095° range and the reported Cu Kα₁/Kα₂ wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio. With counting-statistics weights estimated as σ(I)=√I, the final statistics are Rp = 5.385%, Rwp = 7.336%, Rexp = 3.280%, and GOF = 2.239. Trial additions of CoO and Co₃O₄ drove their nonnegative scales to effectively zero, and their strongest unique lines are not evident, so they were not retained.

The cells and phase scales were refined, while atomic coordinates were retained from reference γ-WO₃ (COD 1528915) and CoWO₄ (COD 5910316, reconstructed as a valid asymmetric unit). The broad, overlapping multiphase laboratory data do not determine independent atomic coordinates reliably. Likewise, because no calibrated instrumental profile was supplied, the fitted breadth terms should not be interpreted as quantitative crystallite sizes or strain.

The least-squares fraction uncertainty is ±0.21 wt% for each phase, but it reflects only the assumed counting weights. A more realistic systematic uncertainty is about 1–2 weight-percentage points because of peak overlap, background/profile choices, and fixed atomic parameters. Fractions are normalized only over the modeled crystalline phases; amorphous material and trace phases below roughly the 1–2 wt% level cannot be excluded or quantified reliably.
