# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is described by two crystalline phases: **wolframite-type NiWO4** and **gamma-WO3**, at **75.98 wt%** and **24.02 wt%**, respectively, normalized over the modeled crystalline material. The final GSAS-II fit gives Rwp = 4.40%, Rp = 3.42%, and GOF = 1.27.

## Evidence

NiWO4 accounts for the dominant lines at approximately 19.21, 23.90, 24.85, 30.87, 36.52, 41.62, 54.57, and 65.78 degrees 2theta. The distinct 23.06/23.54/24.29 degree triplet, together with lines near 26.53, 28.82, 33.20, 33.52, and 34.09 degrees, requires residual monoclinic WO3. This assignment is chemically consistent with reaction of the reported NiO and WO3 precursors at 900 degrees C.

The refined cells are:

- NiWO4, P 2/c: a = 4.59948 A, b = 5.66171 A, c = 4.90903 A, beta = 89.9902 degrees.
- WO3, P 21/c: a = 7.68759 A, b = 7.53236 A, c = 10.5263 A, beta = 136.092 degrees. This is an alternative setting of the usual P 21/n gamma-WO3 cell (approximately a = 7.30071 A, b = 7.53236 A, c = 7.68759 A, beta = 90.8152 degrees).

A trial NiO phase refined to 0.05(10) wt% and did not improve Rwp; its strongest expected line near 43.4 degrees is absent. NiO was therefore excluded rather than reported as a detected phase.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio were fixed to the metadata. Refined terms were a 12-term Chebyshev background, an effective zero offset, global pseudo-Voigt profile terms, phase scales and cells, Ni/W positions in NiWO4, and heavy-atom isotropic displacement parameters; the two WO3 W displacement parameters were constrained equal. Oxygen coordinates and displacement parameters were held to reference values because this Cu X-ray powder pattern does not determine them reliably. Phase-specific size and microstrain were omitted because no calibrated instrumental profile was supplied and those terms were strongly correlated with profile broadening.

The covariance-only fraction uncertainty is 0.11 wt% per phase, but sensitivity to reasonable profile, displacement-parameter, and monoclinic/triclinic WO3 choices indicates a more realistic uncertainty of roughly 1-2 absolute wt%. A weak triclinic distortion of WO3 cannot be excluded, but the lower-symmetry model is not required by the data. The reported fractions exclude any amorphous or unidentified material, which cannot be quantified without an internal standard.
