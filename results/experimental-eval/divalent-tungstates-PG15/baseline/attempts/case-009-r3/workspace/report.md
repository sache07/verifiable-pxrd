# Phase analysis and refinement

The modeled crystalline product is single-phase manganese tungstate, MnWO4, in the wolframite structure (monoclinic P 2/c, No. 13). Its weight fraction is 1.000 after normalization over the modeled crystalline phases. This is not an absolute crystallinity measurement and does not constrain amorphous material.

## Evidence

All prominent reflections from 9.90 to 100.09 degrees 2-theta are indexed by wolframite MnWO4, including the diagnostic low-angle peaks near 15.66, 18.64, 23.83, and 24.30 degrees and the strong pair near 30.06/30.54 degrees. The identification is chemically consistent with reaction of Mn3O4 and WO3 at 900 degrees C. The refined cell is a = 4.83401 A, b = 5.76087 A, c = 4.99959 A, beta = 91.2179 degrees, and V = 139.198 A^3.

Explicit scale-only challenge fits were made for residual Mn3O4, WO3, Mn3WO6, MnO, and Mn2O3. Mn3O4 and Mn3WO6 refined to zero, and MnO was below 0.1 wt% with an insignificant scale. WO3 and Mn2O3 produced formal sub-percent scales (about 0.65 and 0.52 wt%, respectively), but their strongest non-overlapping peaks were absent or had mixed-sign residuals; the small improvements came mainly from overlap with MnWO4 peaks. These trace assignments are therefore not reliable and were not included in the submitted phase model. Secondary crystalline phases below roughly the sub-percent-to-one-percent level cannot be ruled out from this pattern.

## Refinement and uncertainty

The final GSAS-II Rietveld refinement used the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, a smooth background, an effective peak-profile model, and a Bragg-Brentano surface-roughness correction. Cell parameters and symmetry-allowed atomic coordinates were refined; displacement parameters were retained from the starting structural model. The final statistics are Rp = 3.885%, Rwp = 5.251%, Rexp = 3.454%, and goodness of fit = 1.520 (8301 points, 32 refined parameters).

Because pointwise uncertainties and a calibrated instrument profile were not supplied, counting uncertainties were approximated as sqrt(counts), and profile/sample terms are effective nuisance parameters. Formal cell and coordinate standard uncertainties consequently understate systematic uncertainty, especially correlation with the refined 2-theta zero offset. Oxygen coordinates are also less secure than the heavy-atom framework in Cu-radiation powder data.

The submitted structural model is in `phase_1_MnWO4.cif`; the complete observed/calculated/background/difference profile is in `fit.csv`; and `final.gpx` is the final project that generated those outputs.
