# PXRD phase analysis and quantitative refinement

The crystalline pattern is modeled by two phases:

| Phase | Space group | Refined cell (Å, °) | Crystalline weight fraction |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Monoclinic WO3 | P 21/n | a = 7.30297, b = 7.53393, c = 7.69137, β = 90.6818 | 71.26 wt% |
| Spinel Co3O4 | F d -3 m | a = 8.08102 | 28.74 wt% |

The fractions are normalized over the modeled crystalline phases. Their covariance-only standard uncertainty is 0.18 wt% for each phase, but a more realistic uncertainty is about ±1 wt% absolute because no calibrated instrument profile, internal standard, or pointwise experimental uncertainties were supplied. Amorphous material, if present, cannot be quantified from this refinement.

## Evidence

The WO3 assignment is supported by the diagnostic low-angle triplet at 23.46, 23.95, and 24.71° 2θ and its characteristic multiplets from 33.6–34.5° and at higher angles. After the refined specimen-height correction, these match the gamma-monoclinic WO3 model. A modern monoclinic model (COD 2311041, transformed to the conventional P 21/n cell) fit better than the tested older monoclinic, orthorhombic, and triclinic alternatives.

The narrower peaks at 19.37, 31.64, 37.20, 38.91, 45.14, 59.68, and 65.56° match spinel Co3O4 (starting model COD 9005896). A trial CoWO4 phase refined to the nonnegative scale boundary: 0.00 ± 0.09 wt% (formal 1σ), and its strongest expected line near the corrected 30.9° position is absent. CoWO4 was therefore not retained; a practical detection limit is approximately 1 wt% given the overlap and model systematics.

## Refinement and uncertainty

GSAS-II used the reported Cu Kα1/Kα2 wavelengths and fixed 0.5 intensity ratio. With no supplied uncertainties, counting-statistical weights σ(I) = √I were used. Refined terms were the two phase scales and cells, a 15-term Chebyshev background, a Bragg–Brentano specimen displacement, and a parsimonious positive U/W/X peak profile. The final fit is Rwp = 3.561%, Rp = 2.554%, Rexp = 1.722%, and goodness of fit = 2.071.

Atomic coordinates were retained from the reference structures. Trial refinement of the two independent W sites produced greater than 99% parameter correlations, so the powder data do not determine those coordinates reliably. Small systematic intensity residuals at the strongest WO3 peaks and the goodness of fit above one likely reflect residual profile, preferred-orientation, microabsorption, or structure-factor effects; these dominate the practical fraction uncertainty.
