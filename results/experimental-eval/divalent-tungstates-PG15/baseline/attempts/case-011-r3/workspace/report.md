# PXRD phase analysis

The modeled crystalline fraction consists of **70.87 wt% WO3** and **29.13 wt% beta-MnO2 (pyrolusite)**. These values are normalized to the two modeled crystalline phases; they do not quantify amorphous material. The covariance-only uncertainty is ±0.17 percentage point for each fraction, but uncertainty from the uncalibrated instrumental profile, texture, microabsorption, and structural-model choice is larger.

## Evidence

WO3 accounts for the intense 23.44/23.93/24.69 deg triplet and the characteristic groups near 33.6–34.5, 50.2, and 54–57 deg 2theta. Rutile-type beta-MnO2 accounts for the independent lines near 29.01, 37.67, 42.2, 56.2, 59.66, and 72.5 deg. A common refined specimen displacement explains the approximately +0.3 deg offset of both sets from uncorrected reference positions. No unassigned family of sharp peaks remains in the difference profile.

Trial additions of MnWO4 and Mn3WO6 each refined below 0.1 wt% and below meaningful detection. A cubic Mn2O3 trial gave about 0.9 wt%, but its apparent intensity came almost entirely from overlap beneath WO3 features at about 33.2 and 55.4 deg, without a convincing set of independent lines. It was therefore not retained; trace Mn2O3 at roughly the 1 wt% level cannot be ruled out reliably.

The selected WO3 model is triclinic P-1 and strongly pseudo-monoclinic. Its refined cell is a = 7.30474, b = 7.53426, c = 7.69108 A, alpha = 89.9152, beta = 90.7704, gamma = 90.0336 deg. A monoclinic P21/n-equivalent model gave a fit only about 0.1 Rwp percentage point worse, and the line widths do not cleanly resolve all symmetry-lowering splittings. Thus WO3 itself is secure, but the exact P-1 versus monoclinic gamma-WO3 assignment is not fully reliable. The beta-MnO2 cell is a = 4.39747 and c = 2.87310 A in P42/mnm.

## Refinement and fit

The final GSAS-II Rietveld refinement used the complete 9.903–100.095 deg range, the reported Cu Kalpha1/Kalpha2 wavelengths and 0.5 intensity ratio, counting-statistics weights, a smooth Chebyshev background, specimen displacement, unit cells, bounded profile/domain-size terms, and one stable second-order texture coefficient for beta-MnO2. Literature atomic coordinates were held fixed because the overlapping two-phase powder data do not support reliable coordinate refinement.

The converged fit has Rp = 4.301%, Rwp = 5.861%, Rexp = 2.693%, and goodness of fit = 2.180 (sqrt reduced chi-squared). The remaining residual is dominated by small peak-shape and relative-intensity mismatches, consistent with the absence of a calibrated instrumental profile rather than a clearly missing crystalline phase.
