# PXRD phase analysis

The pattern is modeled by two crystalline phases:

- **NiWO4, wolframite type, P 2/c:** **75.32 wt%**
- **WO3, triclinic, P -1:** **24.68 wt%**

Fractions are normalized over these modeled crystalline phases. Because pointwise uncertainties were not supplied, Poisson weights were inferred from the measured counts. The formal least-squares standard uncertainty is 0.08 wt% for each complementary fraction, but a more realistic uncertainty is about **1 wt%** because no calibrated instrumental profile was supplied and systematic effects (fixed displacement parameters, texture, and possible microabsorption) dominate over counting statistics. No amorphous fraction can be obtained from this analysis.

## Evidence

The NiO + WO3 precursor chemistry and 900 °C treatment make NiWO4 the expected reaction product. Its model accounts for the dominant reflections, including those near 15.59, 19.19, 23.91, 24.85, 30.87, 36.52, 41.63, 54.58, and 65.79° 2theta. Extra resolved peaks near 23.06, 23.53, and 24.28°, together with the 33–34° group, require residual WO3.

The WO3 polymorph was tested explicitly. At the same global-profile refinement stage, triclinic P -1 gave Rwp = 4.56%, compared with 4.87% for monoclinic P21/n and 7.72% for orthorhombic Pnma. The final triclinic angles, alpha = 89.8620(83)°, beta = 90.8186(41)°, and gamma = 90.1018(72)°, are resolved from monoclinic metric symmetry. The final two-phase fit has **Rwp = 4.350%, Rp = 3.357%, Rexp = 3.472%, and GOF = 1.253**.

The refinement used the reported Cu K-alpha doublet, a 12-term Chebyshev background, refined cells and phase scales, zero offset, pseudo-Voigt profile, and effective polarization. A mild [111] March-Dollase correction was used for NiWO4. The Ni and W symmetry-restricted coordinates in NiWO4 were refined; oxygen coordinates and the WO3 coordinates were held at reference values because this W-dominated laboratory X-ray pattern does not determine them reliably. Starting structures were based on COD 5910277 (NiWO4, with duplicate symmetry-equivalent sites removed) and COD 2106293 (triclinic WO3).

## Remaining uncertainty

A test NiO phase refined to only about 0.2(1) wt%. That signal was driven by overlap around 37° and did not reproduce an independent NiO line near 43.4°; it is therefore below a reliable identification/quantification threshold and was omitted. Trace NiO at roughly the sub-0.5 wt% level cannot be excluded. No other unmodeled crystalline peaks are evident above the profile/model residuals.
