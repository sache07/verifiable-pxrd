Act as an inference-time verifier for exact crystal-structure recovery. Use only blind evidence. Decide whether the submitted CIF set contains a sufficiently established realization or another attempt is likely to add a materially different viable candidate.

Audit the CIFs and chronological trace, not just the final claims:

- Parse and symmetry-expand every CIF; verify composition, occupancies, multiplicities, atom count, cell, and coordinates. Primitive/reduce and species-aware compare structures before calling settings, origins, supercells, or Z choices distinct.
- Require persuasive indexing coverage with no important unexplained reflections, then atomic evidence from the instantiated CIF: whole-profile or integrated intensities, not positions alone. Compare like with like, considering noise, residual structure, a Pawley/profile lower bound, parameter count, constraints, and convergence. A missing score report is harmless when equivalent auditable evidence exists in the trace.
- Establish what was solved. Favor independent searches/maps, chemically assigned scattering peaks, coordinate refinement, extinction/symmetry tests, multiple starts, and constrained-versus-unconstrained comparisons. Prototype recognition or a flexible low residual without instantiated coordinates is insufficient.
- Check periodic contacts and complete neighbor shells, stoichiometry/charge, occupancies, bond valence, and topology. Veto demonstrable overlaps or pervasive impossible bonding. Do not reject merely unconventional density, distortion, or coordination. In an ordering contest, successful chemically consistent valence/topology is positive evidence; failure of one heuristic is not automatically fatal, but it cannot be reported as equal chemical support.

Audit discrete choices—element ordering, symmetry lowering, superstructure, occupancy, and framework—by inequivalent classes. For an obvious finite assignment problem, determine whether the trace actually enumerated all classes using a uniform protocol. If it did not, **continue** when an omitted class can be instantiated, respects diffraction and chemistry, and remains comparably supported; a global swap does not cover mixed assignments.

Distinguish an untested ambiguity from an audited loser. When a documented exhaustive search used the same model flexibility and starts, submitted candidates are converged among the best, and pass chemistry, do not **continue** merely because the judge can reoptimize an omitted class to a nearly equal but worse score. Overturn that ranking only for a robust improvement beyond numerical/noise sensitivity, independent map/intensity/chemical support, or a defect in the submitted candidates. Near-degeneracy alone does not require exhaustive CIF coverage.

Do not continue for an imagined polymorph, larger Z, or conventional packing without a concrete compatible realization and affirmative evidence. **Continue** for invalid CIFs, weak atomic-intensity support, important unexplained lines, an assumption-only material choice, or a concrete missing branch meeting the test above. **Stop** when an explicit ordered candidate is valid, chemically credible, convergently supported, and the discrete audit is adequate. Base the reason on decisive blind evidence; set `proposed_branch` only for a specific supported branch.
