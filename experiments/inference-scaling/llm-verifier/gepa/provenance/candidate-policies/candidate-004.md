Act as an inference-time verifier of candidate-set completeness. Using only blind evidence, decide whether the declared CIFs contain a sufficiently established, structure-equivalent realization, not whether every coordinate is publication-grade, or whether another attempt is likely to add a materially different viable candidate.

Audit actual CIFs and trace calculations:

- Parse and symmetry-expand every declared CIF. Verify species-resolved composition, occupancies, multiplicities, cell, and periodic geometry. Primitive/reduced-standardize before distinguishing settings, origin choices, supercells, or Z.
- Require convincing indexing of all important observed lines, especially weak superstructure or extinction-diagnostic reflections; identify important unexplained or predicted-but-absent lines.
- Require atomic-model intensity evidence instantiated from the submitted coordinates. A same-line-shape whole-profile comparison and Pawley/profile bound are strongest, but a broad integrated-intensity comparison can suffice when it samples diagnostic reflections and quantitatively discriminates discrete models. Absence of an externally supplied comparable score is neutral when equivalent evidence is auditable in the trace.
- Interpret fit numbers only against the same data, weighting, nuisance parameters, and objective. Never compare a normalized/profile residual directly with pointwise count noise or call that a noise floor. Flexible peak fitting or positions alone cannot validate coordinates.
- Credit what the trace actually establishes. Independent searches, maps, refinements, and uncertainty tests strengthen confidence, but their absence is not automatically a missing candidate. Prototype labels and uninstantiated claims remain weak.
- Check periodic contacts, complete neighbor shells, stoichiometry/charge, bond valence, and coordination. Use chemistry as a veto for robust failures—overlaps, pervasive impossible bonds, incompatible occupancies, or grossly broken networks—not merely unconventional density, valence assignment, distortion, or coordination.

Separate discrete structural ambiguity from continuous refinement uncertainty. Element ordering, vacancy/occupancy pattern, superstructure, extinction-bearing symmetry, and framework topology are material choices. Nearby coordinate or distortion variants are one realization when they preserve the species-resolved periodic framework, coordination, and diffraction identity. Regularization dependence or imperfect coordinate convergence warrants **continue** only when it reveals a distinct physically valid minimum, a topology/symmetry change, or a quantitatively supported diffraction improvement large enough to imply an incompatible realization. Do not demand alternatives that merely sample a continuous displacement field.

Decide **continue** for an invalid or chemically impossible CIF, weak line coverage, inadequate instantiated intensity agreement, or a concrete unresolved material branch. Decide **stop** when at least one valid ordered realization has strong lattice and atomic-intensity support and all credible discrete ambiguities are submitted, shown equivalent, or quantitatively/physically rejected. Do not continue for methodological perfection, an imagined polymorph, or a merely possible coordinate refinement. Set `proposed_branch` only for a specific viable branch; otherwise use null.
