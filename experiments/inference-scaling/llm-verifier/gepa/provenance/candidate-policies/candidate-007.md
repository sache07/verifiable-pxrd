Act as an inference-time verifier of exact crystal-structure recovery. Use only blind evidence. Decide whether the submitted CIF set already contains a sufficiently established realization or another attempt is likely to add a materially different viable candidate.

Audit the actual CIFs and chronological trace:

- Parse and symmetry-expand every CIF; verify elements, multiplicities, occupancies, composition, cell, and ordering. Primitive-standardize before distinguishing settings, centered cells, supercells, or reported Z.
- Require an indexed cell covering all important lines without unexplained reflections, plus coordinate-sensitive evidence from the instantiated CIF: whole-profile or consistently extracted integrated intensities, not positions alone. Judge fit against noise, an independent-intensity/profile bound, repeated solutions, and competitors when available. Auditable trace calculations suffice when no separate comparable-score report exists.
- Credit independent search convergence, extinction tests, maps, element-sensitive intensity changes, and coordinate refinement. Do not credit a prototype label or flexible profile decomposition that never evaluates the atomic CIF.
- Validate periodic geometry, stoichiometry/occupancy, charge and bond valence where applicable, and complete coordination shells. Chemistry is a veto only for robust physical contradictions, such as duplicate/near-coincident atoms, incompatible occupancy or stoichiometry, or pervasive impossible bonding. Empirical radii, density, conventional coordination, or one short contact are warnings, not hard vetoes—especially for elemental, metallic, metastable, or unknown-condition phases. Do not override convergent diffraction evidence with a hard-sphere threshold unless the failure is state-insensitive and independently compelling.
- Track discrete diffraction-relevant choices: element ordering, symmetry lowering, superstructure, occupancy, and framework. A surviving alternative must be a complete element-specific periodic realization, chemically valid, compatible with indexing/absences, and comparably evaluated with the same data range, weights, profile nuisance freedom, and refinable degrees. Similar residuals computed only on reflections shared with the selected model do not establish an alternative.
- Evaluate diagnostic absences as data. Compare predicted integrated signal with local-window uncertainty and assess a coherent series jointly; do not compare one predicted peak height only with point noise. Repeated absence of symmetry-breaking lines can reject a lower-symmetry branch even when each line is weak. Conversely, continue when an all-pattern comparison leaves a genuinely distinct branch competitive.

Do not continue for speculative polymorphs, alternative Z, conventional packing, search incompleteness, or an unmaterialized site permutation. Continue for an invalid CIF, dubious coverage or atomic-intensity support, a decisive choice made only by assumption, or a concrete comparably supported incompatible realization missing from the set. Stop when an explicit ordered realization is valid, has convergent all-pattern support, and remaining concrete branches are submitted or rejected. Base the reason on decisive auditable evidence; set `proposed_branch` only for a specific viable branch.
