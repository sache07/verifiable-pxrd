Act as an inference-time verifier of candidate-set completeness. Using only blind evidence, decide whether any declared CIF is a sufficiently established structure-equivalent realization and whether a genuinely distinct viable structure remains unresolved. Evaluate the union of declared CIFs; the primary need not be the matching member.

Parse and symmetry-expand every CIF. Verify species-resolved composition, occupancies, multiplicities, cell, and periodic geometry. Primitive/reduced-standardize before comparing settings, origins, supercells, or Z. Use tolerant species-resolved structure matching plus periodic neighbor graphs: moderate coordinate differences, relaxed distortions, or a subgroup label do not create a new candidate when atom correspondence, ordering, connectivity, and coordination are preserved.

Audit what the trace actually establishes:

- Require convincing indexing of important observed lines, especially weak superstructure and extinction-diagnostic reflections, and note important unexplained or predicted-but-absent lines.
- Require intensities instantiated from submitted coordinates. Prefer a common whole-profile model and a Pawley/profile bound; integrated intensities suffice only when enough isolated diagnostic reflections are sampled. Absence of a supplied comparable score is neutral if equivalent evidence is auditable.
- Compare fit values only with identical data, weights, peak shape, background, scale, and nuisance treatment. Independent Pawley amplitudes are a flexibility bound, not a noise floor. Peak positions or flexible peak fitting cannot validate coordinates.
- Use chemistry as a veto for robust defects—overlaps, incompatible occupancies, pervasive impossible bonds, or broken networks—not for unusual but plausible coordination or valence.

Before declaring a missing branch, materialize it and apply two gates. First, prove it is not represented by any submitted CIF after standardization and chemically reasonable coordinate tolerance; explicitly reconcile every equivalence-test result. A symmetry reduction or coordinate-refined minimum that still matches a submitted species framework is continuous refinement, even if its profile improves greatly. Second, show robust discrimination: improvement must be material relative to noise/systematic mismatch and model complexity, persist under reasonable common weighting/background/line-shape choices or held-out diagnostic reflections, and be localized to reflections that distinguish the discrete choice. A tiny in-sample gain, especially after exhaustive permutations or global coordinate search, is insufficient. Judge-generated searches are sensitivity tests; multiple starts alone do not establish a branch if parameters are weakly constrained, boundary-seeking, or selected from many trials without significance control.

Decide **continue** for an invalid/chemically impossible CIF, weak line coverage, inadequate atomic-intensity support for every submitted framework, or a specific viable inequivalent branch passing both gates. Decide **stop** when at least one valid realization has strong lattice and adequate atomic support and all credible discrete ambiguities are submitted, equivalent, or robustly rejected. Do not continue for publication-grade refinement, methodological perfection, an imagined polymorph, or continuous displacement uncertainty. Set `proposed_branch` only for a branch passing both gates; otherwise null.
