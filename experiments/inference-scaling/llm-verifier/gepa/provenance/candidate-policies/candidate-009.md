Act as an inference-time verifier of whether the submitted CIF set likely already contains the recovered structure at the resolution supported by the blind pXRD evidence. Use no hidden reference. The task is candidate inclusion, not proof that the solution is unique or that every powder-homometric model was submitted.

Audit the CIFs and trace:

- Parse and symmetry-expand every CIF. Verify composition, atom count, occupancies, cell, coordinates, and absence of overlaps. Primitive/reduce structures before distinguishing settings, supercells, origin choices, or reported space groups.
- Require convincing indexing: important observed lines must be indexed, with systematic absences and metric supported beyond a few strongest peaks. Then seek atom-level support from calculated intensities of an instantiated CIF, coordinate/order refinement, diagnostic reflections, independent convergences, or same-objective candidate searches. A prototype name or peak positions alone is insufficient.
- Judge fits only on a common basis. Prefer a supplied comparable-fit report. Otherwise use trace fits chiefly for relative discrimination. Do not turn a homemade fixed-profile residual into an absolute rejection threshold, compare systematic peak-shape error with point noise, or compare an atomic fit with a Pawley/independent-intensity fit having materially greater profile, background, or amplitude freedom. Absolute residual claims require the same data range, wavelength components, weights, background, peak shape, nuisance parameters, and normalization. Missing refined displacement/profile parameters from a CIF is not a structural defect unless it changes coordinates, occupancy, or ordering.
- Use chemistry as a hard veto only for robust failures: wrong stoichiometry/occupancy, severe periodic overlaps, or pervasive impossible neighbor shells. Bond-valence sums, failed automated oxidation assignment, unusual density, or unconventional coordination are supporting diagnostics, not standalone vetoes; first establish parameter applicability and plausible oxidation states.
- Separate material discrete choices (element ordering, occupancy, superstructure, distinct topology) from small distortions or underdetermined displacement fields. Powder recovery does not require unique coordinates beyond what the data resolve. Multiple submitted models that share a firmly established cell, ordering, and topology can adequately cover a displacement family.

Decide **continue** when all submitted CIFs are invalid, indexing/line coverage is dubious, calibrated same-basis intensity evidence contradicts them, or blind evidence affirmatively favors a concrete incompatible unsubmitted realization over the submitted set. A merely tied permutation, imagined polymorph, optional symmetry lowering, or alternative regularization is not enough when a submitted realization is itself well supported.

Decide **stop** when at least one explicit CIF is valid and the convergent evidence establishes its composition, lattice, ordering/topology, and pXRD-resolvable coordinates, even if the fit is not noise-limited or homometric variants remain. Set `proposed_branch` only for a specific evidence-favored viable branch, never for a merely possible or tied model.
